### 1. [Investigating the dynamics of collective behavior among pedestrians crossing roads: A multi-user virtual reality approach.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aap.2024.107477)
`相关度 95` · 虚拟现实研究行人过街行为与社交影响

*Accident Analysis and Prevention* | Jae-Hong Kwon, Jinho Won, Gi-Hyoug Cho | 2024-02-15

**TLDR:** This experimental study develops a multi-pedestrian virtual reality simulator specifically designed to investigate the impact of social interaction on pedestrians' eye-scanning patterns, perceived responses, crossing behaviors, and the associated crash risk.

> The utility maximization theory, based on the rationality of human beings, has proven effective in modeling pedestrians' decision-making processes while crossing roads. However, there are still unexplained variations in crossing behavior, and deviations from the rational utility model frequently occur in real-life scenarios. This experimental study sheds new light on the presence of inter-individual interactions among pedestrians and the nature of collective behaviors during road crossings. The present study develops a multi-pedestrian virtual reality simulator specifically designed to investigate the impact of social interaction on pedestrians' eye-scanning patterns, perceived responses, crossing behaviors, and the associated crash risk. Our findings indicate that the collective behavior significantly influences pedestrians' behaviors by diverting their attention from essential eye-scanning patterns that reflect their cognitive processes. Pedestrians in pairs exhibit a higher tendency to fixate on each other, spend less time in the decision phase, walk at a slower pace during the crossing phase, and consequently face a higher degree of exposure to dangerous situations compared to when crossing alone. Encouraged by these findings on the effects of social interaction, we discuss preventive strategies to mitigate the negative impacts of collective behavior and foster pedestrians' safety awareness.

**[试图解决的问题]**：行人过街决策中，基于理性效用最大化理论的模型无法解释真实场景中频繁出现的偏离理性行为，即行人间的社会交互与集体行为对过街安全的影响机制不明。

**[核心方法]**：开发多用户虚拟现实（multi-pedestrian VR）模拟器，在受控虚拟环境中操控社会交互条件，同步采集行人眼动扫描模式、感知响应、过街行为决策与碰撞风险相关指标。

**[创新与效果]**：发现集体行为通过转移注意力显著改变行人认知过程；与独自过街相比，成对过街的行人相互注视频率更高、决策阶段耗时更短、过街阶段步行速度更慢，导致危险暴露程度增加。基于此提出缓解集体行为负面效应、提升行人安全意识的干预策略。

### 2. [Analysis of Pedestrians’ Road Crossing Behavior, in Social Groups](https://doi.org/10.1177/03611981231180206)
`相关度 95` · 社交群体行人过街行为分析

*Transportation Research Record* | Le Baron, S. Faria, Emanuel Sousa, E. Freitas | 2023-07-10

**TLDR:** 无

> A significant percentage of pedestrians walk in social groups (friends, families, or acquaintances who walk together). Although patterns generated by social interactions among group members have been shown to affect crowd dynamics, studies on the effect of social interactions at different crossing phases under low pedestrian density are limited. This study aims to comprehensively examine the influence of size and sex composition on pedestrians’ behaviors when walking alone and with friends in different phases before, during, and after the road crossing. For this, experiences were carried out with controlled small groups of friends (varying size and sex composition) at three unsignalized crosswalks with low pedestrian density. The average speed and distance between the young pedestrians in six segments of the trajectories (two in each phase), extracted from video recordings, were analyzed with linear mixed models. Results show that pedestrians reduce their speed when approaching the curb, they accelerate while on the crosswalk, and reduce again when they reach the other side. In all phases, the average speed of the groups was lower than the single pedestrians, and the females’ groups walked slower than the males, except during the crossing, where no sex-related differences were found. On the contrary, before the crossing, the distance increased and decreased from the second segment in the crosswalk. The smallest distance was observed between the female groups and dyads. These findings have relevant implications for research on pedestrian behavior, helping to better understand the complexity of pedestrian dynamics and improve pedestrian safety.

**[试图解决的问题]**：行人结伴出行普遍存在，但低行人密度下社交互动对不同过街阶段（过街前、中、后）行为的影响研究有限，缺乏对群体规模与性别组成作用的量化分析。

**[核心方法]**：在三个无信号人行横道开展受控小规模朋友群体实验（变化规模与性别组成），从视频提取轨迹，划分六个路段（每阶段两个），采用线性混合模型分析平均速度与行人间距。

**[创新与效果]**：发现行人在接近路缘时减速、过街时加速、到达对侧后再次减速；所有阶段群体平均速度低于单人，女性群体速度低于男性群体（但过街时无性别差异），女性群体及二人组的行人间距最小。该结果为行人动力学建模与过街安全改善提供了实证依据。

### 3. [Observational-based study to explore pedestrian crossing behaviors at signalized and unsignalized crosswalks.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aap.2021.105990)
`相关度 95` · 信号交叉口行人过街行为观测

*Accident Analysis and Prevention* | Kayvan Aghabayk, Javad Esmailpour, Ahmad Jafari, N. Shiwakoti | 2021-01-20

**TLDR:** The influence of gender, age group, group-crossing, technological devices and carrying items on pedestrians crossing behaviors at signalized and unsignalized crosswalks simultaneously in Tehran is comprehensively examined.

> Despite many studies on exploring the behaviors of pedestrians crossing the road, there is a need for comprehensive studies that identify the factors that may influence pedestrians crossing behavior at signalized and unsignalized intersections. This study aims to comprehensively examine the influence of gender, age group, group-crossing, technological devices and carrying items on pedestrians crossing behaviors at signalized and unsignalized crosswalks simultaneously. Observational data of 552 pedestrians at two signalized and two unsignalized crosswalks in Tehran were collected. Temporal and spatial violations, conflict experience and collision avoidance, situational awareness, and pedestrians crossing speed were used as pedestrians crossing behaviors indicators. To model crossing behaviors, linear mixed models (LMMs) and Generalized linear mixed models (GLMMs) with fixed-effect approach were applied for the continuous outcome (pedestrians' crossing speed) and binary outcomes, respectively. Phi and Cramer's V coefficients were used to avoid multicollinearity. Results showed that traffic checks before and while crossing showed a high positive correlation with crossing at "don't walk" and flashing "don't walk" signals and conflict experience at signalized crosswalks. As compared to females, males started their crossing more on flashing "don't walk" signal and crossed the crosswalk faster. Older pedestrians exhibited more cautious behaviors at signalized intersections but less in unsignalized intersections. Alone pedestrians behaved more cautiously than groups and crossed the crosswalk more quickly. Using technological devices, regardless of their types, caused pedestrians to not exhibit one or more safe crossing indicators considered in this study. Pedestrians talking on their phones had the least cautious behaviors. Pedestrians listening to music mostly looked at the ground or straight direction rather than looking left-right for traffic. The findings from this study are a valuable resource to road authorities and policy makers to develop appropriate targeted strategies to prevent pedestrians' injuries and fatalities and improve crosswalks safety.

**[试图解决的问题]**：同时探究性别、年龄、群体过街、使用电子设备及携带物品等因素对行人在有信号和无信号人行横道过街行为（时间/空间违规、冲突经历、避让、情境意识、过街速度）的影响。

**[核心方法]**：在德黑兰2处信号灯和2处无信号灯人行横道观测552名行人；采用线性混合模型（LMMs）建模连续结果（过街速度），广义线性混合模型（GLMMs）建模二元结果；使用Phi和Cramer's V系数处理多重共线性。

**[创新与效果]**：发现过街前/过街中的交通观察行为与“禁止通行”及“闪烁禁止通行”信号下过街及冲突经历高度正相关；男性更多在闪烁“禁止通行”信号启动过街且速度更快；老年人在信号交叉口更谨慎但在无信号处更不谨慎；独自过街者比群体更谨慎且速度更快；使用电子设备（尤其打电话者）会导致至少一项安全过街指标缺失，听音乐者多注视地面或正前方而非左右观察交通。结果为道路管理部门制定针对性干预策略、减少行人伤亡和提升人行横道安全提供依据。

### 4. [W/CDM-MSFM-driven pedestrian path prediction at signalized crosswalk with mixed trafﬁc flow](https://doi.org/10.1080/21680566.2020.1828197)
`相关度 95` · 信号人行横道行人路径预测

*未知会议/期刊* | Hao Chen, Xi Zhang, ..., Wangwang Zhu, Baixuan Zhao | 2020-10-05

**TLDR:** The results show that the method outperforms the existing methods and accurately predicts the pedestrians’ paths, which makes it possible for autonomous vehicles to present the feasibility to protect the safety of pedestrians and improve the efficiency of dynamic traffic.

> In this paper, pedestrian path prediction at a signalized crosswalk with pedestrian-electric bicycle-vehicle mixed flow is investigated. Firstly, a waiting/crossing decision model (W/CDM) is developed to predict pedestrians’ waiting/crossing intentions with approaching vehicles. Secondly, a Modified Social Force Model (MSFM) is developed by taking the evasion with conflicting road users, the reaction to traffic lights and crosswalk boundary into consideration. The influence of pedestrians’ heterogeneous characteristics and mixed trafﬁc flow are considered for the first time. Then the mixed traffic data at a signalized crosswalk is recorded and analysed. A maximum likelihood estimation is proposed to calibrate the parameters. Finally, the integrated method (W/CDM-MSFM) is compared with the existing methods. The results show that the method outperforms the existing methods and accurately predicts the pedestrians’ paths, which makes it possible for autonomous vehicles to present the feasibility to protect the safety of pedestrians and improve the efficiency of dynamic traffic.

**[试图解决的问题]**：在信号控制人行横道处，面对行人-电动自行车-车辆混合交通流，如何准确预测行人过街路径以支撑自动驾驶安全决策。

**[核心方法]**：提出等待/过街决策模型（W/CDM）预测行人意图；构建改进社会力模型（MSFM），引入与冲突交通参与者的避让行为、对信号灯及人行横道边界的反应；首次考虑行人异质性特征与混合交通流影响，并用极大似然估计对混合交通实测数据进行参数标定。

**[创新与效果]**：相较于现有方法，W/CDM-MSFM综合模型对行人路径预测精度更高，验证了其在混合交通信号交叉口场景下的有效性，为自动驾驶保障行人安全并提升动态交通效率提供了可行性支撑。

### 5. [Insights and Strategies for an Autonomous Vehicle With a Sensor Fusion Innovation: A Fictional Outlook](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2020.3010940)

*IEEE Access* | Farrukh Hafeez, U. U. Sheikh, ..., Z. Arfeen, S. A. Khalid | 2020

**TLDR:** This review paper discusses related research in the field of prediction of the pedestrian’s intentions and includes open questions, challenges, and proposed solutions in this area of research.

> A few decades ago, the idea of a car driving without human assistance was something inconceivable. With the advent of deep learning-based machine learning in artificial intelligence, this imaginary idea has become part of our life. Like in other fields, these technological revolutions have brought drastic changes to the field of automated driving systems. The autonomous vehicle is in the transition state between level 3 and level 4 of automation, but many mysteries are still waiting to be solved. Understanding the environment as precisely as a human driver is still far in the future. To attain human perception requires the capturing of extensive surrounding information that depends on the onboard sensors installed on the vehicle. Because the recent autonomous vehicle is equipped with several sensors, it captures surrounding information in diverse forms. Combining these multi-domain data with sensor fusion is the open area of research that is considered in this paper. Along with sensor fusion, another area of prime importance that is necessary to be explored is the prediction of pedestrian intentions. Though the study of the prediction of a pedestrian’s intentions started approximately fifteen years ago, most of the research is based on detection rather than intention. Furthermore, this paper also discusses related research in the field of prediction of the pedestrian’s intentions. At the end of the article, this review paper includes open questions, challenges, and proposed solutions.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶处于L3到L4过渡阶段，车辆虽搭载多类传感器，但仍无法以接近人类驾驶员的方式精确理解环境；同时，行人意图预测研究约始于十五年前，但已有工作多聚焦于“检测”而非“意图”，是亟待突破的开放问题。

**[核心方法]**：以综述视角梳理多传感器融合与行人意图预测的相关研究，重点讨论将多域传感器数据进行融合以提升环境感知，并结合行人行为/意图预测的已有方法与框架，最后总结开放性问题、挑战及提出的解决策略。

**[创新与效果]**：明确指出现有研究“重检测、轻意图”的局限，汇总传感器融合与行人意图预测的关键挑战，并提出面向未来的研究方向与解决建议；属于前瞻性综述，未提供量化指标。
