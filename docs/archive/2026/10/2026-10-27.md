### 1. [STC-MGNet: Spatial-Temporal-Channel Attention-Enhanced Multimodal Graph Network for Pedestrian Intention Prediction](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/115fed2dd452eb54cbaf030038acf6a747e1a09b)
`相关度 95` · 行人过街意图预测，核心匹配

*未知会议/期刊* | Yuan-Zhe Li, Meng Wang | 2026-10-16

**TLDR:** A spatial-temporal-channel attention-enhanced multimodal graph network (STC-MGNet) for pedestrian crossing intention prediction is proposed and a multi-stage cross-modal channel modulation mechanism is introduced to adaptively refine pose representations through modality-specific channel gating at successive graph encoding stages.

> Accurate prediction of pedestrian crossing intentions plays a critical role in enhancing the safety of pedestrian-vehicle interactions in autonomous driving. However, effectively capturing subtle spatiotemporal patterns in pedestrian pose sequences and integrating complementary visual and ego-vehicle motion information remain challenging. This paper proposes a spatial-temporal-channel attention-enhanced multimodal graph network (STC-MGNet) for pedestrian crossing intention prediction. STC-MGNet introduces a spatial-temporal-channel (STC) attention-enhanced pose graph encoder to learn discriminative representations from pedestrian pose sequences. Three parallel attention branches selectively emphasize informative skeletal joints, observation frames, and feature channels, with their outputs integrated through learnable fusion weights. To leverage complementary visual and ego-vehicle motion information, a multi-stage cross-modal channel modulation mechanism is introduced to adaptively refine pose representations through modality-specific channel gating at successive graph encoding stages. Experimental results demonstrate that STC-MGNet achieves accuracy rates of 70%, 87%, and 88% on the JAADbeh, JAADall, and PIE datasets, respectively, outperforming several representative baseline methods. Ablation experiments further highlight the complementary contributions of visual and ego-vehicle motion information to prediction performance.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶中，如何从行人姿态序列中捕捉细微时空模式，并融合视觉与自车运动信息，以提高行人过街意图预测的准确性。

**[核心方法]**：提出 STC-MGNet 多模态图网络，包含：1）空间-时间-通道（STC）注意力增强的姿态图编码器，三个并行注意力分支分别突出关键骨骼关节点、观测帧和特征通道，并通过可学习融合权重整合输出；2）多阶段跨模态通道调制机制，在连续图编码阶段通过模态特定通道门控自适应细化姿态表征，以融合互补的视觉与自车运动信息。

**[创新与效果]**：在 JAADbeh、JAADall 和 PIE 数据集上分别取得 70%、87% 和 88% 的准确率，优于多种代表性基线方法；消融实验验证了视觉与自车运动信息对预测性能的互补贡献。

### 2. [Learning Intention-Behavior Consistency for Pedestrian Crossing Prediction](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCNEA70678.2026.11722207)
`相关度 95` · 行人过街预测，直接匹配

*2026 9th International Conference on Computer Network, Electronic and Automation (ICCNEA)* | Xiao-Dong Feng, Miao Wang, Chao Shen | 2026-09-01

**TLDR:** 无

> Pedestrians are the vulnerable group in road traffic, so understanding the behavior of pedestrians and accurately predicting their crossing intentions is of great significance for traffic safety. Most existing methods treat the prediction task as a binary classification problem. However, such traditional methods usually can only establish a deterministic mapping between the environment and the pedestrian's state, and cannot fully model the multimodality of the pedestrian's intentions in highly dynamic scenarios. In recent years, researchers have introduced conditional generative models to capture these unobserved uncertainties by introducing latent variables, thereby enabling the modeling of complex intention distributions. Nevertheless, in highly dynamic traffic scenarios, traditional multimodal conditional generation modules still have significant limitations. To address these problems, we propose a dynamic consistency constraint module, namely MAA-IAC, which aligns the action and intention modalities and improves the model performance gradually. To evaluate the proposed framework, we conducted extensive comparative experiments studies on the JAAD benchmark dataset. The results show that introducing dynamic intention-behavior consistency constraints can significantly improve the prediction performance of the model. Specifically, our proposed method boosts the baseline Accuracy from 0.7945 to 0.8754, while also achieving notable improvements in other key metrics such as F1-score and AUC.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有行人过街意图预测方法大多将任务视为二分类，只能建立环境与行人状态的确定性映射，难以建模高动态场景下行人意图的多模态分布。

**[核心方法]**：提出动态一致性约束模块 MAA-IAC，显式对齐动作模态（action）与意图模态（intention），在训练过程中逐步增强模态间一致性，用于行人过街意图预测。

**[创新与效果]**：在 JAAD 基准上，将基线 Accuracy 从 0.7945 提升至 0.8754，同时在 F1-score 和 AUC 等关键指标上也取得显著改善。

### 3. [CD-PIP: Conditional Diffusion for Pedestrian Intention Prediction under Occlusion](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCNEA70678.2026.11722188)
`相关度 95` · 遮挡下行人心意图预测，直接匹配

*2026 9th International Conference on Computer Network, Electronic and Automation (ICCNEA)* | Miao Wang, Xiao-Dong Feng, Chao Shen | 2026-09-01

**TLDR:** 无

> Pedestrian crossing intention prediction is a core perceptual capability for intelligent vehicles operating in urban traffic environments. Most existing intention-prediction methods assume that pedestrian motion observations are complete, whereas in real traffic scenes, vehicles, other pedestrians, and roadside infrastructure often cause short-term occlusions, leading to missing motion cues such as bounding boxes, center coordinates, and ego-vehicle speed. Reducing the impact of such missing cues on intention prediction requires not only effectively recovering the missing motion information, but also ensuring that the recovered results genuinely serve the downstream prediction task. To this end, this paper proposes CD-PIP, an occlusion-robust pedestrian intention prediction framework for occluded scenes. The framework builds explicit conditions from visible observations, occlusion masks, and diffusion time steps, learns the conditional distribution of pedestrian motion features in the missing regions, and jointly optimizes a reconstruction loss together with an intention classification loss, so that the imputed results balance reconstruction accuracy against downstream discriminative requirements. Experiments with three random seeds on the PIE dataset show that conditional diffusion imputation reduces the masked MAE from 0.0419 to 0.0109, a relative decrease of 73.9%, while raising the F1 score from 0.8960 to 0.9053.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有行人过街意图预测方法假设运动观测完整，而真实交通场景中因遮挡导致边界框、中心坐标、自车速度等运动线索缺失，影响意图预测精度。

**[核心方法]**：提出 CD-PIP 框架，利用条件扩散模型（conditional diffusion），从可见观测、遮挡掩码和扩散时间步构建显式条件，学习遮挡区域行人运动特征的条件分布；联合优化重建损失与意图分类损失，使插补结果兼顾重建精度与下游判别任务需求。

**[创新与效果]**：在 PIE 数据集上（3 个随机种子）实现遮挡掩码 MAE 从 0.0419 降至 0.0109（相对降低 73.9%），F1 分数从 0.8960 提升至 0.9053，显著提升遮挡场景下的意图预测鲁棒性。

### 4. [Modeling Spatiotemporal Pedestrian-Environment Interactions for Predicting Pedestrian Crossing Intention from the Ego-View](https://doi.org/10.25394/pgs.14831025.v1)
`相关度 95` · 行人过街意图预测，直接匹配

*Figshare* | Chen Chen | 2021-08-06

**TLDR:** This thesis proposes a novel model that predicts pedestrian crossing intention using not only the pedestrian’s action behaviors (bounding box and pose estimation), but also their relationship to their environment using graph en- codings, pedestrian bounding boxes, and human pose estimation.

> For pedestrians and autonomous vehicles (AVs) to co-exist harmoniously and safely in
the real-world, AVs will need to not only react to pedestrian actions, but also anticipate
their intentions. In this thesis, we propose to use rich visual and pedestrian-environment
interaction features to improve pedestrian crossing intention prediction from the ego-view.
We do so by combining visual feature extraction, graph modeling of scene objects and their
relationships, and feature encoding as comprehensive inputs for an LSTM encoder-decoder
network.

 Pedestrians react and make decisions based on their surrounding environment, and the
behaviors of other road users around them. The human-human social relationship has already been explored for pedestrian trajectory prediction from the bird’s eye view in stationary
cameras. However, context and pedestrian-environment relationships are often missing in
current research into pedestrian trajectory, and intention prediction from the ego-view. To
map the pedestrian’s relationship to its surrounding objects we use a star graph with the
pedestrian in the center connected to all other road objects/agents in the scene. The pedestrian and road objects/agents are represented in the graph through visual features extracted
using state of the art deep learning algorithms. We use graph convolutional networks, and
graph autoencoders to encode the star graphs in a lower dimension. Using the graph en-
codings, pedestrian bounding boxes, and human pose estimation, we propose a novel model
that predicts pedestrian crossing intention using not only the pedestrian’s action behaviors
(bounding box and pose estimation), but also their relationship to their environment.

 Through tuning hyperparameters, and experimenting with different graph convolutions
for our graph autoencoder, we are able to improve on the state of the art results. Our context-
driven method is able to outperform current state of the art results on benchmark dataset
Pedestrian Intention Estimation (PIE). The state of the art is able to predict pedestrian
crossing intention with a balanced accuracy (to account for dataset imbalance) score of 0.61,
while our best performing model has a balanced accuracy score of 0.79. Our model especially
outperforms in no crossing intention scenarios with an F1 score of 0.56 compared to the state
of the art’s score of 0.36. Additionally, we also experiment with training the state of the art model and our model to predict pedestrian crossing action, and intention jointly. While
jointly predicting crossing action does not help improve crossing intention prediction, it is
an important distinction to make between predicting crossing action versus intention.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶车辆（AV）不仅要感知行人动作，还需预测其过街意图；现有基于自我视角（ego-view）的研究常忽略场景上下文与行人-环境交互关系，导致意图预测准确率不足。

**[核心方法]**：以目标行人为中心构建星型图（star graph），连接场景中所有道路对象/智能体，节点特征由SOTA深度学习算法提取的视觉特征表示；采用图卷积网络（GCN）与图自编码器（Graph Autoencoder）进行低维图编码，并将图编码、行人边界框（bounding box）和人体姿态估计（pose estimation）共同输入LSTM编码器-解码器网络以预测穿越意图。

**[创新与效果]**：在PIE（Pedestrian Intention Estimation）基准数据集上，平衡准确率从SOTA的0.61提升至0.79；在不穿越意图场景中F1分数达0.56（SOTA为0.36）。此外，实验表明联合预测穿越动作与意图不会提升意图预测性能，明确了动作预测与意图预测的任务差异。

### 5. [VTP Trajectory Prediction Using Destination Induction and TF-GRU-ATM for Autonomous Vehicles](https://doi.org/10.1109/JIOT.2026.3724910)
`相关度 92` · VTP轨迹预测，含行人，直接相关

*IEEE Internet of Things Journal* | Hao Chen, Jing Zhao, ..., Xu-Lei Liu, Xi Zhang | 2026-11-01

**TLDR:** 无

> This article conducts a comprehensive investigation of vulnerable traffic participants (VTPs, pedestrians, cyclists, and electric cyclists) trajectory prediction with the destination induction and the Transformer-gate recurrent unit-attention mechanism (TF-GRU-ATM). First, a social psychology-based extended social force model (ESFM) is presented to predict the future destination of VTPs, which is subsequently integrated with the historical trajectories of VTPs as destination induction. In addition, a traffic dataset collected by an uncrewed aerial vehicle (UAV) is obtained, and a maximum likelihood estimation method is employed to calibrate the parameters of ESFM. Second, a K-Means++ algorithm (KMA) is used to cluster the traffic dataset; the clustering results are fused as prior knowledge into the TF-GRU-ATM to enhance the model’s ability to learn potential behavioral patterns in the real world. Furthermore, a multifeature-based prediction network (FPN) is developed to predict VTPs’ crossing intentions. Historical trajectories and poses of VTPs, vehicle speed, and distance between VTP and vehicle are considered. Finally, a novel TF-GRU-ATM is presented for VTP trajectory prediction. Future destination, behavioral patterns, crossing intentions, interactions between traffic participants, and VTP heterogeneity are considered. An attention mechanism is used to obtain the attention weights associated with the above factors. The findings indicate that the TF-GRU-ATM outperforms the existing methods, achieving a more than 6% increase in prediction accuracy with the collected traffic dataset, which demonstrates the potential of the proposed model for future autonomous vehicle traffic prediction systems.

**[试图解决的问题]**：面向自动驾驶场景，对行人、自行车、电动自行车等弱势交通参与者（VTPs）的轨迹预测不准确，尤其缺乏对目的地、行为模式与过街意图等深层因素的建模。

**[核心方法]**：提出 TF-GRU-ATM 轨迹预测模型。具体包括：(1) 基于社会心理学扩展社会力模型（ESFM）预测 VTP 未来目的地，并与历史轨迹拼接作为目的地引导；(2) 采用无人机采集交通数据，并用最大似然估计标定 ESFM 参数；(3) 用 K-Means++ 对数据集聚类，将聚类结果作为先验知识融合进网络；(4) 设计多特征预测网络（FPN）预测过街意图，输入包括历史轨迹、姿态、车辆速度、VTP 与车辆距离；(5) 在 TF-GRU-ATM 中融合未来目的地、行为模式、过街意图、参与者交互和 VTP 异质性，通过注意力机制分配各因素权重。

**[创新与效果]**：在所采集的真实交通数据集上，TF-GRU-ATM 相比现有方法预测准确率提升超过 6%，验证了融合多源高层语义信息对 VTP 轨迹预测的有效性。
