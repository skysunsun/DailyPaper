### 1. [Impact of Posture and Social Features on Pedestrian Road-Crossing Trajectory Prediction](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIM.2021.3139691)
`相关度 95` · 直接行人过街轨迹预测

*IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement* | I-Hsi Kao, Ching-yao Chan | 2022

**TLDR:** 无

> A comparative experiment on machine learning (ML)-based pedestrian trajectory prediction was conducted in this study. The study was aimed at improving functional capabilities, where pedestrian safety is the key, such as in relation to intelligent transportation, autonomous vehicles, and automated-guided vehicles. In this article, the effects of integrating social and posture features into pedestrian trajectory prediction are explored. The experimental results show that incorporating social and posture features can improve the accuracy of pedestrian trajectory prediction. Furthermore, various deep learning (DL) models were adopted and compared to deal with these different types of features. Our work shows that a DL approach can handle complex features better than conventional ML. To enable the neural network process different types of data, four types of networks are designed, namely, C-network, S-network, P-network, and SP-network, which are a 2-D convolutional neural network (CNN) with only coordinates, 2-D CNN with the social feature, 2-D CNN with posture features, and 3-D CNN with social and posture features, respectively. The angle mean square errors (MSEs) of SP-network are 4.99, 1.31, and 0.05 less than those of C-network, S-network, and P-network, respectively. The scalar MSEs of SP-network are 0.204, 0.024, and 0.154 less than those of C-network, S-network, and P-network, respectively. Moreover, feature visualization and importance are discussed. It is concluded that the posture features make it possible to separate features with different scalars and angles. It is also shown that the most important posture feature is the edge of the shoulders.

**[试图解决的问题]**：在行人过街轨迹预测中，现有模型主要依赖坐标信息，缺乏对社交交互和人体姿态的利用，导致预测精度不足。

**[核心方法]**：设计了四种神经网络结构进行对比：C-network（仅坐标的2D CNN）、S-network（加入社交特征的2D CNN）、P-network（加入姿态特征的2D CNN）、SP-network（同时加入社交和姿态特征的3D CNN），用于融合不同模态特征进行行人过街轨迹预测。

**[创新与效果]**：SP-network相比C/S/P-network，角度MSE分别降低4.99、1.31、0.05，标量MSE分别降低0.204、0.024、0.154；特征可视化表明姿态特征能分离不同标量和角度特征，且最重要的姿态特征是肩部边缘。

### 2. [Pedestrians’ Crossing Decisions While Interacting with Automated Vehicles – Insights from a Longitudinal Study](https://doi.org/10.54941/ahfe1002445)
`相关度 92` · 行人过街决策与AV交互长期研究

*Human Factors in Transportation* | Philip Joisten, Nina Theobald, Bettina Abendroth | 2022

**TLDR:** 无

> The decisions of pedestrians when crossing in front of automated vehicles (AVs) have been studied under the usage of external human machine interfaces (eHMIs) as explicit means of communication between AVs and other road users. Long-term effects of AV and eHMI exposure on pedestrians’ crossing decisions have not yet been intensively researched. Therefore, a longitudinal study with three sessions and two eHMI designs was conducted in a controlled field environment with 21 participants. A participant’s decision to cross in front of an AV was continuously measured using a hand-held device whose button was pressed when the participant felt safe to cross. Findings show that with increasing experience, pedestrians’ perceived safety to cross at close distances to a yielding AV increases when the AV is equipped with an eHMI displaying the vehicle’s status, perception and yielding intention. We conclude that when interacting with AVs, pedestrians' perceived safety benefits from eHMIs whose impact depends on pedestrians' experience.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有研究多关注自动驾驶汽车（AV）eHMI对行人过街决策的短期影响，缺乏长期暴露（纵向）效应证据。

**[核心方法]**：在受控场地开展纵向研究，共3次实验、2种eHMI设计、21名被试；使用手持设备按钮持续测量行人认为安全并决定过街的时机。

**[创新与效果]**：发现随着经验增加，当eHMI显示车辆状态、感知信息和让行意图时，行人在近距离接近减速让行AV时的过街安全感显著提升；表明eHMI的效益依赖行人的交互经验。

### 3. [External Human-Machine-Interfaces on Automated Vehicles: Which message and perspective do pedestrians in crossing situations understand best?](https://doi.org/10.54941/ahfe100960)
`相关度 90` · 行人过街与AV外交互信息理解

*International Conference on Intelligent Human Systems Integration* | Sebastian Ludwig Weiß, D. Eisele, Tibor Petzoldt | 2022

**TLDR:** 无

> In the future, external-Human-Machine-Interfaces (eHMIs) may facilitate the communication between automated vehicles (AVs) and nearby pedestrians. The aim of this study was to investigate which messages (AVs’ intention to yield or not to yield) and perspective (does the message refer to the behavior of the vehicle or the behavior that is expected of the pedestrian) of eHMIs are understood best in terms of objective comprehension, subjective comprehensibility, and speed. Participants in an online study (N = 85) indicated whether they can safely cross or not in reaction to six different eHMI icons. Messages that tell them to cross the street were understood better and faster compared to those that instructed them not to do so. Those referring to the pedestrian were best understood objectively as well as subjectively. We advise caution regarding eHMIs that communicate that the AV is not yielding / that the pedestrian cannot cross

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶汽车（AV）与行人过街场景中，外部人机界面（eHMI）应显示何种消息（让行/不让行）及采用何种视角（车辆行为/期望行人行为）才能被行人最好地理解。

**[核心方法]**：在线研究（N=85），让被试对6种不同的eHMI图标判断是否可以安全过街，比较客观理解正确率、主观可理解性和反应速度。

**[创新与效果]**：指示“可以过街”的消息比指示“不可过街”的消息理解更快、更准；以“行人”为视角的消息在客观和主观理解上均最佳；建议谨慎使用表达“AV不让行/行人不能过街”的eHMI。

### 4. [Evaluation of an assistance system supporting older pedestrians’ road crossing in virtual reality and in a real-world field test](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.966096)
`相关度 88` · 辅助系统支持老年行人过街

*Frontiers in Psychology* | Rebecca Wiczorek, J. Protzak | 2022-12-20

**TLDR:** 无

> Older pedestrians are at a high risk of becoming victims of car accidents because they tend not to pay sufficient attention to upcoming traffic. Within our research project, an assistance system for older pedestrians has been developed. It detects the street and communicates with the users through a vibrotactile interface. Two evaluation studies have been carried out in order to understand the potential benefits and drawbacks of the developed assistance system. One study was conducted in a virtual environment (VR) with 23 participants, aged 65+. The other experiment was a field test in a real street environment with 26 participants, aged 65+. Objective dependent variables in both experiments were checking for traffic (operationalized via head tracking) and stopping in front of the street (VR study), i.e., approaching time (field test). Workload and acceptance served as subjective dependent variables. Analysis of the VR experiment showed significantly more head rotation with the assistance system than without it, as well as significantly more with cars than without cars. The same was true for the frequency of stopping. No significant difference was found concerning workload. With regard to acceptance, the majority of participants indicated that the system was supportive and able to reduce risks in traffic. In the field test, results for head rotation confirmed the findings of the VR study. Analysis showed a marginally significant higher head rotation frequency with the alarm system than without, and significantly different patterns of checking for traffic at marked and unmarked crossings. However, unlike in the VR study, no differences were found in approaching time with and without the assistance system. Approaching time was slower at marked crossings. No difference was found with regard to workload, meaning the use of the assistance system did not increase the subjectively perceived workload of participants. Analysis of the acceptance questionnaire showed a positive attachment to the assistance system. However, most reported that they did not experience any advantage from the use of the system, and expressed no intention to buy such a system for themselves.

**[试图解决的问题]**：年长行人过街时对来往车辆注意不足，交通事故风险高。

**[核心方法]**：开发基于街道检测的辅助系统，通过振动触觉界面与用户通信；采用VR实验（23人，65+）与真实道路实地测试（26人，65+）评估，以头部追踪量化交通查看行为，并记录接近时间、主观工作负荷与接受度。

**[创新与效果]**：VR中辅助系统显著增加头部旋转次数与停步频率，有车时更明显；实地测试确认头部旋转频率提高（边缘显著），且未增加主观工作负荷，多数参与者认为系统有支持性。但实地接近时间无显著改善，多数参与者报告未感到实际优势，且无购买意愿。

### 5. [Text to Blind Motion](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2412.05277)
`相关度 85` · 盲人过街运动生成，关注行人行为

*Neural Information Processing Systems* | Hee Jae Kim, Kathakoli Sengupta, ..., Hernisa Kacorri, Eshed Ohn-Bar | 2024-12-06

**TLDR:** 无

> People who are blind perceive the world differently than those who are sighted, which can result in distinct motion characteristics. For instance, when crossing at an intersection, blind individuals may have different patterns of movement, such as veering more from a straight path or using touch-based exploration around curbs and obstacles. These behaviors may appear less predictable to motion models embedded in technologies such as autonomous vehicles. Yet, the ability of 3D motion models to capture such behavior has not been previously studied, as existing datasets for 3D human motion currently lack diversity and are biased toward people who are sighted. In this work, we introduce BlindWays, the first multimodal motion benchmark for pedestrians who are blind. We collect 3D motion data using wearable sensors with 11 blind participants navigating eight different routes in a real-world urban setting. Additionally, we provide rich textual descriptions that capture the distinctive movement characteristics of blind pedestrians and their interactions with both the navigation aid (e.g., a white cane or a guide dog) and the environment. We benchmark state-of-the-art 3D human prediction models, finding poor performance with off-the-shelf and pre-training-based methods for our novel task. To contribute toward safer and more reliable systems that can seamlessly reason over diverse human movements in their environments, our text-and-motion benchmark is available at https://blindways.github.io/.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有3D人体运动数据集缺乏多样性、偏向明眼人，导致运动模型（如自动驾驶系统）无法准确捕捉盲人 pedestrians 特有的、看似不可预测的运动行为。

**[核心方法]**：构建首个面向盲人 pedestrians 的多模态运动基准 BlindWays，使用可穿戴传感器采集11名盲人参与者、8条真实城市路线的3D运动数据，并为每段运动提供详细文本描述，涵盖盲人独特运动特征及其与导盲工具（白杖/导盲犬）和环境的交互；基于该基准评测了现有SOTA 3D人体运动预测模型。

**[创新与效果]**：首次引入“文本到盲人运动”任务；评测发现现成及基于预训练的方法在该新任务上表现差，揭示了现有模型无法泛化至盲人运动模式的局限；提供公开的 text-and-motion benchmark 以支持更安全、更可靠的系统研究。
