### 1. [Modeling illegal pedestrian crossing behaviors at unmarked mid-block roadway based on extended decision field theory](https://doi.org/10.1016/J.PHYSA.2020.125327)
`相关度 93` · 乱穿马路动态决策模型构建

*未知会议/期刊* | Yongjie Wang, Bin Shen, ..., Q. Su, Wenqiang Chen | 2021-01-15

**TLDR:** A dynamic decision model of jaywalking for pedestrians based on the extended decision field theory is proposed, which is conducive to the traffic management department to optimize the traffic infrastructure settings of potential road sections, and take effective regulatory measures to reduce the occurrence ofJaywalkers.

> Abstract There is a huge risk of accidents when pedestrians jaywalk across the road. It is a difficult problem to predict and reduce the behavior of jaywalking. This paper proposes a dynamic decision model of jaywalking for pedestrians based on the extended decision field theory. The model connects pedestrians’ perceptions in three dimensions such as efficiency, safety, and fairness with the dynamic traffic environment, and shows the evolution of pedestrians’ decision-making. The tester’s decision data is collected through a questionnaire survey for the purpose of model parameter estimation and validity testing. The established model is used to analyze the pedestrian’s behavior of jaywalking. The results show that the preference of pedestrians for jaywalking is inversely proportional to the traffic density. As the preference threshold increases, the probability of pedestrians choosing to jaywalk gradually decreases. For the distance between the site of jaywalking and the bus stop, the probability of jaywalking basically follows a normal distribution. The average distance gradually increases, with the increase of the car arrival rate. Additionally, location of the crosswalk has a great impact on the jaywalking behaviors. The potential application of this model is that it can help the traffic management department to predict the probability distribution of jaywalking events in the road network, which is conducive to the traffic management department to optimize the traffic infrastructure settings of potential road sections, and take effective regulatory measures to reduce the occurrence of jaywalkers.

**[试图解决的问题]**：行人乱穿马路（jaywalking）行为难以预测和减少，存在巨大事故风险，缺乏动态决策模型来刻画其在无信号灯路段中部的决策演化。

**[核心方法]**：基于扩展决策场理论（extended decision field theory），构建行人乱穿行为的动态决策模型，将效率、安全、公平三维感知与动态交通环境（如车流密度、汽车到达率、公交站距离、人行横道位置）耦合；通过问卷调查采集被试决策数据，用于模型参数估计与效度检验。

**[创新与效果]**：模型揭示：乱穿偏好与交通密度成反比；偏好阈值升高时，选择乱穿概率逐渐降低；乱穿地点与公交站间距的概率分布近似正态，且平均间距随汽车到达率增大而增大；人行横道位置对乱穿行为影响显著。该模型可预测路网中乱穿事件概率分布，辅助交通管理部门优化潜在路段基础设施设置并制定有效监管措施。

### 2. [Pedestrian crossing decision during flashing green-countdown signal for urban signalized intersection](https://doi.org/10.1080/19439962.2021.1949415)
`相关度 92` · 行人过街决策模型直接相关

*Journal of Transportation Safety & Security* | Xue-Qin Long, Meng Zhou, Huan Zhao, Yanan Song | 2021-07-12

**TLDR:** During the signal-transition period for the signalized intersection, pedestrians will face a dilemma of whether to cross or wait, and this study establishes a decision model of pedestrian-crossing based on a choice between crossing or waiting.

> Abstract During the signal-transition period for the signalized intersection, pedestrians will face a dilemma of whether to cross or wait. The study establishes a decision model of pedestrian-crossing based on risk-cost and time-utility during flashing green-countdown signal. First, the calculation methods of potential and direct risk in each direction of the intersection are proposed according to the traffic operation status. Then, analyze the relationship among the arrival time, remaining duration of flashing green signal and the actual crossing behavior, pedestrians are divided into adventurous, ordinary and conservative types respectively. Finally, introducing the time utility function, crossing decision models of three types pedestrians are established respectively based on comparing the risk cost and time utility. Take two signalized intersections as examples, parameters of decision models are calibrated through field investigation. Three types of models’ accuracy rates for the adventurous, ordinary and conservative pedestrians are 80%, 90% and 60% respectively, which denotes that the models can accurately predict the decision behavior of adventurous and ordinary pedestrians. Additionally, pedestrians are less sensitive to risk and more sensitive to time when they are faced with urgent time.

**[试图解决的问题]**：信号交叉口闪烁绿色倒计时信号期间，行人面临过街或等待的两难决策，缺乏针对该过渡期行人过街行为的预测模型。

**[核心方法]**：构建基于风险成本与时间效用的行人过街决策模型。依据交通运行状态提出交叉口各方向潜在风险和直接风险的计算方法；结合行人到达时间、闪烁绿剩余时长与实际过街行为，将行人划分为冒险型、普通型和保守型；引入时间效用函数，分别建立三类行人的过街决策模型，比较风险成本与时间效用以判定是否过街。

**[创新与效果]**：通过两处信号交叉口的实地调查标定模型参数，三类行人决策模型准确率分别为冒险型80%、普通型90%、保守型60%，可准确预测冒险型和普通型行人的决策行为；并发现行人在时间紧迫时对风险敏感度降低、对时间敏感度升高。

### 3. [LiDAR-Based Vehicle–Pedestrian Interaction Study on Midblock Crossing Using Trajectory-Based Modified Post-Encroachment Time](https://doi.org/10.1177/03611981221083295)
`相关度 90` · LiDAR行人过街轨迹与交互研究

*Transportation Research Record* | V. Vasudevan, R. Agarwala, Akhil Tiwari | 2022-04-02

**TLDR:** 无

> Understanding pedestrian road crossing behavior is crucial in the interests of pedestrian traffic safety. The interaction between pedestrians and approaching vehicles at midblock road crossings is a complex process. The high degree of vehicle heterogeneity and poor lane discipline in low- and middle-income countries further adds to its complexity. Moreover, because of the lack of proper pedestrian facilities, pedestrian crossing behavior is quite different in low- and middle-income countries. It is necessary to have high-resolution data to study pedestrian crossing behavior. At midblock, pedestrians do not always take the shortest route while crossing the road and cross diagonally, moving toward or away from the approaching vehicle. The risk indicator of crossing changes based on the pedestrian path chosen. This study was based on studying vehicle–pedestrian interaction at midblock locations using light detection and ranging (LiDAR) data. This study considered a trajectory-based pedestrian modified post-encroachment time (PET) to account for the various possible pedestrian crossing trajectories. Suitable algorithms were developed to identify and track vehicles and pedestrians from the LiDAR point cloud data. The results showed the trajectory-based pedestrian modified PET to perform better than the traditional PET. The risk-taking appetite of pedestrians was also observed to be high when motorbikes were the approaching vehicle. This study showed the motorbike–pedestrian conflicts to be riskier as compared with car–pedestrian conflicts. The results obtained from this study would be helpful to practitioners in investing higher priority to proper pedestrian-related road infrastructure.

**[试图解决的问题]**：在中低收入国家无信号路段（midblock），由于车辆类型混杂、车道纪律差且缺乏行人设施，行人横穿轨迹多变（斜穿、靠近或远离来车），传统风险指标无法准确反映人车交互风险。

**[核心方法]**：基于激光雷达（LiDAR）点云数据，开发车辆与行人识别及跟踪算法；提出基于轨迹的行人修正后侵入时间（trajectory-based pedestrian modified Post-Encroachment Time, PET），用于量化不同横穿轨迹下的行人与来车交互风险。

**[创新与效果]**：修正PET优于传统PET；发现摩托车逼近时行人冒险意愿更高，摩托车-行人冲突比汽车-行人冲突更危险；结果可为优先改善行人相关道路基础设施提供依据。

### 4. [Autonomous vehicle with communicative driving for pedestrian crossing: Trajectory optimization](https://doi.org/10.1109/ITSC45102.2020.9294325)
`相关度 90` · 自动驾驶与行人过街轨迹交互

*2020 IEEE 23rd International Conference on Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITSC)* | Meng Zhang, Abdeljalil Abbas-Turki, ..., A. Koukam, K. Jo | 2020-09-20

**TLDR:** 无

> Connected and autonomous vehicles (CAV) is a key technology for this century. One of the main challenges is to define a smart interaction behavior of CAV with the other road users. The challenge is mainly raised at conflicting points where path of CAV intersects with the other users. Recent studies show interaction with humans is a big challenge. It not only requires a collision avoidance system but also more communicative behaviors of the CAV. More precisely, pedestrian needs to understand the intention of the incoming CAV whether it will cross first or not according to its speed profile. One way to overcome this issue is to design optimal trajectory control of the CAV that matches with the pedestrian expectation. However, such a design faces the non-linearity of the space sharing constraint. This paper uses sequence modeling based on Petri Net in order to overcome the problem. It also uses Hamiltonian analysis to derive the optimal control. Numerical examples of the communicative behavior of CAV at pedestrian crossing show that the proposed approach provides CAV with a kind of automatic courtesy.

**[试图解决的问题]**：网联自动驾驶车辆（CAV）在人车路径冲突点（如人行横道）需具备“可沟通”的驾驶行为，而不仅是避撞；难点在于轨迹控制需匹配行人对CAV是否先行的速度预期，同时处理空间共享约束带来的非线性。

**[核心方法]**：采用Petri网对CAV与行人交互的共享空间顺序进行序列建模，以处理非线性约束；并基于哈密顿分析（Hamiltonian analysis）推导最优轨迹控制律。

**[创新与效果]**：将行人预期纳入CAV轨迹优化，在过街场景中实现了类似“自动礼让”（automatic courtesy）的沟通式驾驶行为；数值算例验证了该方法能生成符合行人预期的CAV轨迹，解决了仅避撞系统无法表达交互意图的限制。

### 5. [Pedestrian Crossing Decisions in Virtual Environments: Behavioral Validity in CAVEs and Head-Mounted Displays](https://doi.org/10.1177/0018720820987446)
`相关度 88` · 虚拟环境中行人过街决策验证

*Hum. Factors* | S. Schneider, Philipp Maruhn, ..., V. Cavallo, K. Bengler | 2021-02-02

**TLDR:** For a common decision task, the results obtained in virtual environments deviate from those in a nonvirtual test bed, which indicates that restrictions apply when predicting real-world behavior based on VR studies.

> Objective To contribute to the validation of virtual reality (VR) as a tool for analyzing pedestrian behavior, we compared two types of high-fidelity pedestrian simulators to a test track. Background While VR has become a popular tool in pedestrian research, it is uncertain to what extent simulator studies evoke the same behavior as nonvirtual environments. Method An identical experimental procedure was replicated in a CAVE automatic virtual environment (CAVE), a head-mounted display (HMD), and on a test track. In each group, 30 participants were instructed to step forward whenever they felt the gap between two approaching vehicles was adequate for crossing. Results Our analyses revealed distinct effects for the three environments. Overall acceptance was highest on the test track. In both simulators, crossings were initiated later, but a relationship between gap size and crossing initiation was apparent only in the CAVE. In contrast to the test track, vehicle speed significantly affected acceptance rates and safety margins in both simulators. Conclusion For a common decision task, the results obtained in virtual environments deviate from those in a nonvirtual test bed. The consistency of differences indicates that restrictions apply when predicting real-world behavior based on VR studies. In particular, the higher susceptibility to speed effects warrants further investigation, since it implies that differences in perceptual processing alter experimental outcomes. Application Our observations should inform the conclusions drawn from future research in pedestrian simulators, for example by accounting for a higher sensitivity to speed variations and a greater uncertainty associated with crossing decisions.

**[试图解决的问题]**：验证虚拟现实（VR）环境能否诱发与真实非虚拟环境一致的 pedestrian crossing 行为；此前不确定模拟器研究能否推广到真实场景。

**[核心方法]**：在 CAVE 自动虚拟环境、头戴式显示器（HMD）和真实试验跑道三种环境中复现完全相同的实验流程；每组 30 名参与者，要求其在两辆逼近车辆之间的间隔足够安全时向前迈步穿越；比较穿越接受率、穿越发起时间、间距-发起关系、车速影响及安全裕度。

**[创新与效果]**：发现虚拟环境与真实场景存在系统性偏差：真实跑道的穿越接受率最高；两种模拟器中穿越发起更晚；仅 CAVE 中显示出间距大小与穿越发起之间的关系；与真实跑道不同，两种模拟器中车速均显著影响接受率与安全裕度。这表明基于 VR 研究预测真实行人行为存在限制，且模拟器中更强的速度敏感性可能源于感知加工差异，应被后续行人模拟器研究纳入考量。
