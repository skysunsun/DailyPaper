### 2. [Estimating Pedestrian Crossing States Based on Single 2D Body Pose](https://doi.org/10.1109/IROS45743.2020.9341745)
`相关度 95` · 单2D姿态估计过街状态，直接相关

*IEEE/RJS International Conference on Intelligent RObots and Systems* | Zixing Wang, N. Papanikolopoulos | 2020-10-24

**TLDR:** 无

> The Crossing or Not-Crossing (C/NC) problem is important to autonomous vehicles (AVs) for safe vehicle/pedestrian interactions. However, this problem setup often ignores pedestrians walking along the direction of the vehicles' movement (LONG). To enhance the AVs' awareness of pedestrian behavior, we make the first step towards extending the C/NC to the C/NC/LONG problem and recognize them based on single body pose. In contrast, previous C/NC state classifiers depend on multiple poses or contextual information. Our proposed shallow neural network classifier aims to recognize these three states swiftly. We tested it on the JAAD dataset and reported an average 81.23% accuracy.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有行人过街状态识别（C/NC）忽略沿车辆行驶方向行走（LONG）的行人，且以往分类器依赖多帧姿态或场景上下文，难以快速、单帧地扩展行人与车辆交互感知。

**[核心方法]**：提出将 C/NC 二分类扩展为 C/NC/LONG 三分类，使用基于单帧 2D 人体姿态的浅层神经网络分类器，输入仅为单幅 2D 骨架姿态，不依赖多姿态序列或上下文信息，以快速推理三分类状态。

**[创新与效果]**：首次在单个 2D 人体姿态上实现 C/NC/LONG 三分类；在 JAAD 数据集上取得 81.23% 的平均准确率，验证了仅凭单帧姿态即可完成跨类别行人过街状态估计的可行性。

### 3. [Modeling the interaction between vehicle yielding and pedestrian crossing behavior at unsignalized midblock crosswalks](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trf.2020.06.019)
`相关度 95` · 车辆让行与过街交互建模，直接相关

*未知会议/期刊* | J. Zhao, J. Malenje, Jingxian Wu, Ruoming Ma | 2020-08-01

**TLDR:** 无

> Abstract The interaction between pedestrians and vehicles is an inevitable phenomenon at unsignalized midblock crosswalks. This study aims to contribute to a better understanding of the interaction between vehicle yielding and pedestrian gap acceptance (VY and PGA). A microscopic traffic flow model was established to describe the interaction and explores its effect on traffic flow. The VY and PGA behaviors were converged into the proposed model. The proposed model was accomplished in a time step simulation. The results stability and descriptive power of the proposed model were analyzed. The proposed model was also validated using empirical data. The effects of the traffic and geometric factors on the operation of the unsignalized midblock crosswalks were discussed based on numerical experiments. Accordingly, the recommendations on choosing the proper control mode of midblock crosswalks (unsignalized or signalized) were proposed.

**[试图解决的问题]**：无信号中间路段人行横道上，车辆让行（VY）与行人可接受间隙（PGA）行为之间的动态交互缺乏微观建模，且该交互对交通流运行的影响及其控制模式选择依据尚不明确。

**[核心方法]**：建立微观交通流模型，将VY和PGA行为纳入统一框架，采用时间步仿真实现人车交互过程，并基于经验数据验证模型稳定性与描述能力，通过数值实验分析交通及几何因素影响。

**[创新与效果]**：模型能稳定复现人车交互对交通流的影响，经实测数据验证具有良好描述力；量化了交通量与几何设计对无信号中间路段人行横道运行效率的作用，并据此提出无信号/信号控制模式的适用性选择建议。

### 4. [Exploring stage-wise pedestrian-crossing behavioral patterns at vulnerable urban midblocks: a perspective under heterogeneous traffic conditions](https://doi.org/10.1080/19439962.2018.1543375)
`相关度 93` · 行人过街阶段行为模式，直接相关

*Journal of Transportation Safety & Security* | Avinash Chaudhari, S. Arkatkar, G. Joshi, M. Parida | 2020-08-08

**TLDR:** 无

> Abstract Due to the rapid increase in the vehicular traffic, pedestrians in urban cities need to look for space for movement on roads, particularly while crossing at midblocks. While accepting the gap, pedestrians have to adjust their crossing speed and behavior that can be influenced by numerous factors in their surroundings while crossing. Generally, in developing countries like India, pedestrians as well as drivers do not follow the traffic regulation, thus, there is a chance of conflict between pedestrians and vehicles due to limited available space on road. The intensity of these conflicts depends on prevailing traffic volume on road, either motorized traffic or nonmotorized, like pedestrians and other surrounding variables. Present study is focused on demonstrating a detailed methodology adopted for data collection at midblock sections of urban roads first, under prevailing crossing patterns observed at nine study sections, and then further describing results pertaining to analysis of patterns emerging out of pedestrian crossing behavior. Different parameters describing pedestrian characteristics like age, gender, crossing times, waiting times and crossing speed of pedestrians, safety margin, type of movement, crossing stage, and approaching vehicle speed have been considered for the analysis. In addition to this, it is proven that safety margin value is significantly lower in oblique crossing (non/partial compliance) pattern than perpendicular (full-compliance) crossing. Study results show that the stages and type of movement/pattern, while crossing, that is, perpendicular or oblique is an important parameter in analyzing behavior of the crossing at midblock in developing countries like India.

**[试图解决的问题]**：在印度等发展中国家城市无信号路段（midblock），机动车与行人混行且双方普遍不遵守交通法规，行人过街冲突频发，缺乏对行人分阶段过街行为模式的系统量化分析。

**[核心方法]**：对9个城市midblock断面采集行人与机动车交互数据，提取行人年龄、性别、过街时间、等待时间、过街速度、安全裕度（safety margin）、移动类型（垂直/斜穿）、过街阶段及来车速度等参数，对比斜穿（非/部分遵守）与垂直过街（完全遵守）模式下的行为差异。

**[创新与效果]**：证明斜穿模式下的安全裕度显著低于垂直过街模式；证实过街阶段与移动类型（垂直或斜穿）是分析发展中国家midblock行人过街行为的关键参数，为异质交通流下行人过街风险评估提供方法学参考。

### 5. [Evaluation of pedestrian critical gap and crossing speed at midblock crossing using image processing.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aap.2021.106127)
`相关度 92` · 直接研究行人过街间隙与速度

*Accident Analysis and Prevention* | Y. Alver, P. Onelcin, A. Cicekli, M. Abdel-Aty | 2021-04-14

**TLDR:** 无

> Pedestrians confront risky situations at midblock sections due to the unyielding behavior of drivers. Thus, pedestrians have to wait for an appropriate gap to cross. This research investigates pedestrians' gap acceptance and crossing speed for midblock crossings by image processing methods in Izmir, Turkey. A total of 498 pedestrians have been tracked at two midblock crossings. The data were collected for one hour at each midblock crossing during the evening peak hour between 5.00-6.00 p.m. Three synchronized cameras were used to record pedestrian crossings. Then, by using image processing, vehicle and pedestrian trajectories have been obtained. Two cameras were mounted on telescopic tripods reaching up to 9 m, and the third camera was used to identify pedestrians' gender better. The parameters extracted from the recordings are; pedestrians' gender, group size, whether they carried items or not, and their accepted/rejected gaps. Pedestrian and item detection has been performed by YOLOv3 and YOLACT models. The accepted and rejected time gaps were extracted for pedestrians, excluding the pedestrians who crossed between stopped vehicles and crossed when an approaching vehicle did not exist within 100 m from the midblock crossing. Raff's method was used to estimate the critical gap using accepted/rejected gaps. The critical gaps ranged between 4.1 s and 6.2 s. The 15th percentile crossing speeds were found to be similar, ranging between 0.78 m/s and 0.80 m/s.

**[试图解决的问题]**：行人在中段过街处因驾驶员不让行而面临危险，需等待合适间隙；缺乏基于图像处理的临界间隙与过街速度实测评估。
**[核心方法]**：在土耳其伊兹密尔两处中段过街点，使用三台同步摄像机（两台升至9米、一台辅助识别性别）录制晚高峰1小时数据；采用 YOLOv3 和 YOLACT 模型检测行人及携带物品，获取车辆与行人轨迹，提取接受/拒绝间隙；用 Raff 法估计临界间隙，并计算过街速度百分位值。
**[创新与效果]**：共追踪498名行人，排除停车间隙及100米内无来车情形后，得出临界间隙范围为4.1 s – 6.2 s；15%分位过街速度范围为0.78 m/s – 0.80 m/s，为路段过街设施设计提供了实测参数。
