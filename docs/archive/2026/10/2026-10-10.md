### 1. [Effects of Pedestrian Behavior, Time Pressure, and Repeated Exposure on Crossing Decisions in Front of Automated Vehicles Equipped with External Communication](https://doi.org/10.1145/3491102.3517571)
`相关度 95` · 自动驾驶前行人过街决策，直接相关

*International Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems* | Mark Colley, Elvedin Bajrovic, E. Rukzio | 2022-04-29

**TLDR:** 无

> Automated vehicles are expected to substitute driver-pedestrian communication via LED strips or displays. This communication is expected to improve trust and the crossing process in general. However, numerous factors such as other pedestrians’ behavior, perceived time pressure, or previous experience influence crossing decisions. Therefore, we report the results of a triply subdivided Virtual Reality study (N=18) evaluating these. Results show that external communication was perceived as hedonically pleasing, increased perceived safety and trust, and also that pedestrians’ behavior affected participants’ behavior. A timer did not alter crossing behavior, however, repeated exposure increased trust and reduced crossing times, showing a habituation effect. Our work helps better to integrate research on external communication in ecologically valid settings.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶汽车用LED/显示屏替代司机与行人沟通，但外部通信效果受行人行为、时间压力、经验等多因素影响，缺乏生态效度下的综合评估。

**[核心方法]**：开展三因素（外部通信、行人行为、时间压力/重复暴露）细分的虚拟现实实验（N=18），测量行人过街决策、信任、安全感和体验。

**[创新与效果]**：发现外部通信提升享乐愉悦感、安全感和信任；其他行人行为显著影响参与者过街行为；计时器未改变行为；但重复暴露产生习惯化效应，增加信任并缩短过街时间。研究为在生态效度情境下整合外部通信研究提供了依据。

### 2. [Pedestrian Trajectory Prediction with MLP-social-GRU](https://doi.org/10.1145/3457682.3457737)
`相关度 95` · 行人轨迹预测，直接核心

*International Conference on Machine Learning and Computing* | Yan-Bo Zhang, Li-Ying Zheng | 2021-02-26

**TLDR:** A data-driven pedestrian trajectory predictor called MLP-social-GRU, which adopts Gated Recurrent Units (GRU) to get hidden features of a pedestrian motion patterns, from which relationships between pedestrians can be simulated and is superior to popular models such as L STM, social-LSTM, and goal- social-array.

> When crossing a crowded area, a person can predict dangers or collisions in advance around him/her, and then makes a suitable decision which direction he/she should take. The pedestrian trajectory prediction aims at simulating such ability of humans in a crowded environment. Most of the existing trajectory predictions are all based on the traditional hand-crafted methods that often ignore critical factors and can only be adapted to specific environments. Based on deep learning technology, this paper proposes a data-driven pedestrian trajectory predictor called MLP-social-GRU. First, the proposed predictor processes a pedestrian trajectory with a Multilayer Perceptron (MLP). Then, it adopts Gated Recurrent Units (GRU) to get hidden features of a pedestrian motion patterns, from which relationships between pedestrians can be simulated. Next, the social-pooling is used to receive and merge the hidden status information to get the mutual influence of adjacent pedestrians. Finally, a unified pedestrian trajectory prediction framework is designed based on abovementioned modules. We evaluate our predictor on two publicly available datasets, ETH and UCY, and the results show that it is superior to popular models such as LSTM, social-LSTM, and goal-social-array.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有行人轨迹预测多依赖传统手工设计方法，容易忽略关键行人交互因素，且只能适应特定场景。

**[核心方法]**：提出数据驱动的 MLP-social-GRU 模型：先用多层感知机（MLP）编码行人轨迹，再用门控循环单元（GRU）提取运动模式的隐藏特征以模拟行人关系，随后通过 social-pooling 接收并融合相邻行人隐藏状态以建模相互影响，最终构成统一的行人轨迹预测框架。

**[创新与效果]**：用端到端深度网络替代人工规则建模；在 ETH 和 UCY 公开数据集上，预测性能优于 LSTM、social-LSTM 和 goal-social-array 等流行模型。

### 3. [Walking Secure: Safe Routing Planning Algorithm and Pedestrian’s Crossing Intention Detector Based on Fuzzy Logic App](https://doi.org/10.3390/s21020529)
`相关度 95` · 行人过街意图检测，直接相关

*Italian National Conference on Sensors* | J. M. Lozano Domínguez, T. de J. Mateo Sanguino | 2021-01-01

**TLDR:** A mobile app based on the integration of the smartphone sensors and a fuzzy logic strategy to determine the pedestrian’s crossing intention around crosswalks is presented, and it has been proven that a smartphone can be successfully used as a crossing intention detector system.

> Improving road safety through artificial intelligence is now crucial to achieving more secure smart cities. With this objective, a mobile app based on the integration of the smartphone sensors and a fuzzy logic strategy to determine the pedestrian’s crossing intention around crosswalks is presented. The app developed also allows the calculation, tracing and guidance of safe routes thanks to an optimization algorithm that includes pedestrian areas on the paths generated over the whole city through a cloud database (i.e., zebra crossings, pedestrian streets and walkways). The experimentation carried out consisted in testing the fuzzy logic strategy with a total of 31 volunteers crossing and walking around a crosswalk. For that, the fuzzy logic approach was subjected to a total of 3120 samples generated by the volunteers. It has been proven that a smartphone can be successfully used as a crossing intention detector system with an accuracy of 98.63%, obtaining a true positive rate of 98.27% and a specificity of 99.39% according to a receiver operating characteristic analysis. Finally, a total of 30 routes were calculated by the proposed algorithm and compared with Google Maps considering the values of time, distance and safety along the routes. As a result, the routes generated by the proposed algorithm were safer than the routes obtained with Google Maps, achieving an increase in the use of safe pedestrian areas of at least 183%.

**[试图解决的问题]**：提升城市行人过街安全，尤其在无信号灯或复杂路口时，缺乏对行人过街意图的实时检测与安全路线规划工具。

**[核心方法]**：开发了一款移动应用，集成智能手机传感器（加速度计、陀螺仪等）数据，采用模糊逻辑（Fuzzy Logic）策略推断行人在人行横道附近的过街意图；同时提出一种优化算法，结合云端数据库中的行人安全区域（斑马线、步行街、人行道）计算、绘制并导航安全路线。

**[创新与效果]**：利用31名志愿者产生的3120个样本进行实验，模糊逻辑模型在过街意图检测上达到98.63%的准确率，真阳性率98.27%，特异性99.39%（基于ROC分析），证明了智能手机可有效用作过街意图检测器。另外，将所提算法生成的30条路线与Google Maps对比，在时间、距离相近的情况下，安全路线使用行人安全区域的比例至少提高183%，显著提升了路径安全性。

### 4. [Effect of pedestrian characteristics and their road crossing behaviors on driver yielding behavior at controlled intersections.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jsr.2022.01.001)
`相关度 90` · 行人过街与驾驶员让行交互

*Journal of Safety Research* | N. Zafri, Tanzila Tabassum, ..., Rashada Sultana, A. Debnath | 2022-02-01

**TLDR:** 无

> INTRODUCTION
Globally, pedestrians are one of the most vulnerable road-user groups. Their vulnerability increases while crossing the road at controlled intersections during the "don't walk" phase. Previous literature shows that driver yielding behavior has an association with pedestrian safety at intersections. Though several studies have explored driver yielding behavior towards pedestrians at conflict points, evidence on how pedestrian actions influence driver yielding behavior at intersections is yet to be investigated.


METHOD
To pursue this end, a binary logistic regression model was developed using the collected data to explore the effect of non-compliant pedestrian characteristics and their road crossing behavior on driver yielding behavior towards pedestrians at six controlled intersections of Dhaka, Bangladesh. The data were collected through videography survey.


RESULTS
Results showed that drivers were more likely to yield to pedestrians who were female, crossing in a group, carrying baggage, not using a mobile, making some hand gesture to the driver, or crossing by rolling gap strategy.


PRACTICAL APPLICATIONS
These findings add new insights for transportation planners into the complex interaction between vehicles and pedestrians at busy controlled intersections, and thus would help to make a pedestrian friendly street.

**[试图解决的问题]**：在受控交叉口“禁止步行”阶段，行人过街行为及特征如何影响驾驶员让行行为，此前缺乏证据。

**[核心方法]**：基于孟加拉达卡六个受控交叉口的视频调查数据，构建二元Logistic回归模型，分析非合规行人的特征与过街行为对驾驶员让行概率的影响。

**[创新与效果]**：发现女性、结群、携带行李、未使用手机、向驾驶员打手势以及采用滚动间隙过街的行人更易获得驾驶员让行；为交通规划者理解人车复杂交互提供了新见解，有助于设计行人友好的街道。

### 5. [Behavior Evolution of Multi-Group in the Process of Pedestrian Crossing Based on Evolutionary Game Theory](https://doi.org/10.3390/SU13042009)
`相关度 90` · 演化博弈建模行人过街行为

*Sustainability* | Ran Zhang, Zhonghua Wei, Heng Gu, Shi Qiu | 2021-02-13

**TLDR:** This paper provides a basis for decision-making for the traffic management department to manage road traffic, offering a new idea from the perspective of evolution for solving the conflict of interest at the crosswalk of the road section.

> The mixed traffic flow has an increasingly impact on the operation of urban traffic. To study the evolution law of multi-group behaviors in pedestrian crossing, we used the evolutionary game theory to establish a multi-group behavior evolution model for pedestrian crossing. The process of concern started from the risk perception and multi-group behavior choice. The evolutionary stability strategies, evolution trends, and factors affecting the evolutionary path of multi-group behaviors are discussed in this paper. This study found that evolutionary strategy equilibrium of pedestrians, drivers, and traffic managers not only relied on their own earning, but also on those of the other two groups. The factors affecting its behavior included the revenue factor and the limiting factor. Evolutionary game theory was used to analyze the multi-group interaction behavior of pedestrians, vehicle drivers, and traffic managers in the process of pedestrian crossing, as well as to analyze the behavior of traffic subjects in the process of pedestrian crossing. This paper provides a basis for decision-making for the traffic management department to manage road traffic, offering a new idea from the perspective of evolution for solving the conflict of interest at the crosswalk of the road section.

**[试图解决的问题]**：混合交通流对城市交通运行影响日益加剧，行人过街过程中行人、驾驶员与交通管理者多群体行为冲突及演化规律不明。

**[核心方法]**：基于演化博弈论建立行人过街多群体行为演化模型，从风险感知与多群体行为选择出发，分析演化稳定策略、演化趋势及影响演化路径的收益因素和限制因素。

**[创新与效果]**：发现三群体演化策略均衡不仅取决于自身收益，还取决于其他两群体收益；从演化视角为交通管理部门治理路段人行横道利益冲突提供了决策依据和新思路。
