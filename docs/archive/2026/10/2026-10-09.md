### 1. [Crosswalk Trajectory Prediction via an Intent-Trajectory Integrated Framework for Autonomous Driving](https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2026.3707642)
`相关度 95` · 直接预测行人过街轨迹与意图

*IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print)* | Yan-Hang Lin, Jun-Heng Yang, ..., X. Zang, Q. Luo | 2026-10-01

**TLDR:** 无

> The increasing complexity of automated urban environments demands accurate modeling of pedestrian crossing behavior. Existing approaches often rely on human annotations or vehicle-centric views, limiting their ability to predict pedestrian intentions in dynamic scenarios. In this paper, we propose an Intent-Trajectory Integrated Pedestrian Crossing Prediction framework (ITI-PCP) that explicitly couples high-level semantic intention inference with multimodal trajectory generation. The proposed framework introduces a Dynamic Bayesian Network to model pedestrian crossing intent in an interpretable and temporally evolving manner, enabling early detection of intention shifts prior to significant trajectory deviations. To effectively capture heterogeneous interaction densities in complex traffic scenes, we further design an Adaptive Spatio-Temporal Encoder with dynamic grid resolution selection, combined with a coarse-to-fine multi-scale grid-based prediction scheme that balances multimodality and spatial continuity. Experiments on the ETH/UCY datasets show that ITI-PCP reduces ADE and FDE by 39.1% and 33.3%, respectively, compared to Trajectron++, and achieves a lower KDE-NLL score (–1.24) than AgentFormer and PECNet. On the NuScenes dataset, the model maintains strong performance over a 4-second prediction horizon, demonstrating robust generalization to realistic autonomous driving scenarios. Ablation studies further confirm the effectiveness of each proposed module.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有行人过街预测依赖人工标注或车端视角，难以在动态场景中早期捕捉意图变化。

**[核心方法]**：提出 Intent-Trajectory Integrated Pedestrian Crossing Prediction (ITI-PCP) 框架，显式耦合高层语义意图推断与多模态轨迹生成；采用动态贝叶斯网络建模时变过街意图，并设计自适应时空编码器（动态网格分辨率选择）与粗到细多尺度网格预测方案。

**[创新与效果]**：在 ETH/UCY 上相比 Trajectron++，ADE 降低 39.1%，FDE 降低 33.3%；KDE-NLL 达到 –1.24，优于 AgentFormer 和 PECNet；在 NuScenes 上实现 4 秒预测时域内稳定性能，验证了泛化性，且消融实验证实各模块有效。

### 2. [Interaction Effects of Pedestrian Behavior, Smartphone Distraction and External Communication of Automated Vehicles on Crossing and Gaze Behavior](https://doi.org/10.1145/3544548.3581303)
`相关度 95` · 行人过街与自动驾驶交互直接相关

*International Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems* | M. Lanzer, I. Koniakowsky, Mark Colley, M. Baumann | 2023-04-19

**TLDR:** 无

> External communication of automated vehicles is proposed to replace driver-pedestrian communication in ambiguous crossing situations. So far, research has focused on simpler scenarios with one attentive pedestrian and one automated vehicle. This virtual reality study (N=115) investigates a more complex scenario with other crossing pedestrians, a distracting task on the smartphone, and external communication by the automated vehicle. Interaction effects were found for crossing duration, gaze behavior, and subjective measures. For attentive pedestrians, the external communication resulted in shorter crossing durations, higher perceived safety, as well as lower perceived criticality, cognitive workload, and effort. These positive effects were not found when pedestrians were distracted. Instead, distracted pedestrians benefited from other crossing pedestrians because they looked less at the stopping vehicle, felt safer, perceived the situation as less critical, and reported lower cognitive workload and effort. Pedestrians initiated crossings earlier with a group or external communication and later with a smartphone.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有自动驾驶外部通信研究多针对单个专注行人，缺乏对多人过街、行人分心（智能手机干扰）等复杂交通情境下人车交互效果的探究。

**[核心方法]**：采用虚拟现实实验（N=115），在存在其他过街行人、智能手机分心任务及自动驾驶车辆外部通信的条件下，测量行人的过街开始时间、过街时长、注视行为及主观评价，并分析这些因素的交互效应。

**[创新与效果]**：发现了外部通信与行人分心/群体环境的交互作用。专注行人受益于外部通信：过街时长更短、安全感更高、感知危险性和认知负荷/努力更低；分心行人则未由此获益，反而依赖其他过街行人：减少注视停车车辆、安全感更高、感知危险性更低、认知负荷/努力更低。此外，行人在有群体或外部通信时更早开始过街，使用智能手机时更晚开始过街。

### 3. [Investigating temporal variations in pedestrian crossing behavior at semi-controlled crosswalks: A Bayesian multilevel modeling approach](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trf.2020.11.002)
`相关度 93` · 直接研究行人过街行为建模

*未知会议/期刊* | Yun-Chang Zhang, J. Fricker | 2021

**TLDR:** A Bayesian multilevel logistic regression model is developed to capture heterogeneities in pedestrian interaction behavior during four different time periods and indicates significant factors, some of which confirm previous research and some that are new ways to explain pedestrian behavior at the individual level.

> Abstract “Semi-controlled” crosswalks are unsignalized, but have clear pavement markings and “yield to pedestrian” signs. At these locations, pedestrians and motorists frequently interact to determine who should proceed first. When interacting with drivers, pedestrian crossing decisions are complex events that involve a variety of human responses, as well as vehicle dynamics, traffic characteristics, and environmental conditions. In addition, these complexities can be subject to temporal effects. Without considering temporal variations in pedestrian-motorist interaction, statistical methods could lead to biased coefficient estimates and inaccurate conclusions. The study developed a Bayesian multilevel logistic regression (BMLR) model to capture heterogeneities in pedestrian interaction behavior during four different time periods. The proposed method incorporates time-specific effects that vary randomly between time-periods based on a weakly informative prior. The results indicate significant factors, some of which confirm previous research and some that are new ways to explain pedestrian behavior at the individual level. The identification of variables such as FlowOn and FlowWait sheds light on the interactions between pedestrians – providing more information than the single GroupSize measure. Some consequent safety implications are discussed from the perspectives of vehicle dynamics, vehicle flow rate and pedestrian volume. The more detailed metrics developed in this paper will provide a valuable starting point. for the design of crosswalk controls that will foster a higher degree of compliance and less delay.

**[试图解决的问题]**：无信号但设有清晰标线和“礼让行人”标志的“半受控”人行横道处，行人-机动车交互决策受时间因素影响；不考虑时间异质性会使统计模型的系数估计有偏，并导致结论失真。

**[核心方法]**：提出了贝叶斯多层逻辑回归（BMLR）模型，用于刻画四个不同时间段内行人交互行为的异质性；模型基于弱先验（weakly informative prior）引入随时间段随机变化的时间特异性效应。

**[创新与效果]**：识别出显著影响行人过街决策的因素，部分验证已有研究结论，部分为个体层面解释行人行为提供了新视角；发现的 FlowOn 与 FlowWait 变量揭示了行人间的交互作用，其信息量优于单一的 GroupSize 指标。模型给出了更细化的度量指标，可从车辆动力学、车流率与行人流量角度提出安全启示，为设计更高合规率、更少延误的人行横道控制方案提供了依据。

### 4. [Analysis of pedestrian second crossing behavior based on physics-informed neural networks](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-72155-y)
`相关度 92` · 直接建模行人二次过街行为

*Scientific Reports* | Yong-Qing Guo, Hai Zou, ..., Dong Guo, Jahongir Pirov | 2024-09-11

**TLDR:** A two-stage pedestrian crossing model based on Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs), incorporating fluid dynamics equations to determine characteristics such as speed, density, acceleration, and Reynolds number during crossings is presented.

> Pedestrian two-stage crossings are common at large, busy signalized intersections with long crosswalks and high traffic volumes. This design aims to address pedestrian operation and safety by allowing navigation in two stages, negotiating each traffic direction separately. Understanding crosswalk behavior, especially during bidirectional interactions, is essential. This paper presents a two-stage pedestrian crossing model based on Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs), incorporating fluid dynamics equations to determine characteristics such as speed, density, acceleration, and Reynolds number during crossings. The study shows that PINNs outperform traditional deep learning methods in calculating and predicting pedestrian fluid properties, achieving a mean squared error as low as 10–8. The model effectively captures dynamic pedestrian flow characteristics and provides insights into pedestrian behavior impacts. The results are significant for designing pedestrian facilities to ensure comfort and optimizing signal timing to enhance mobility and safety. Additionally, these findings can aid autonomous vehicles in better understanding pedestrian intentions in intelligent transportation systems.

**[试图解决的问题]**：大型繁忙信号交叉口长人行横道上的行人二次过街行为，特别是双向交互时的人流特性难以精确刻画。

**[核心方法]**：提出基于物理信息神经网络（PINNs）的两阶段行人过街模型，将流体动力学方程（用于计算速度、密度、加速度、雷诺数）嵌入神经网络训练。

**[创新与效果]**：相比传统深度学习方法，PINNs在计算和预测行人流体特性上更优，均方误差低至10⁻⁸；有效捕捉动态行人流特征，可用于行人设施设计、信号配时优化及自动驾驶对行人意图的理解。

### 5. [Modeling the Influence of Smartphone Distraction and Pedestrian Characteristics on Pedestrian Road Crossing Behavior](https://doi.org/10.1177/03611981231189499)
`相关度 90` · 直接研究行人过街行为与分心影响

*Transportation Research Record* | A. Yadav, N. Pawar, N. Velaga | 2023-08-03

**TLDR:** 无

> With the increase in the use of technology, smartphone distraction has become a significant pedestrian safety issue. Research on pedestrian smartphone distraction has been conducted mainly in western countries, and there is a scarcity of research in low- and middle-income countries (LMICs). This study is the first attempt to investigate the effect of smartphone distraction on pedestrians’ road crossing behavior in India. We conducted a survey of pedestrians, with 560 respondents (63.4% males), about their demographics and walking characteristics, pedestrian behavior (violations, errors, lapses, aggressive behaviors, and positive behaviors), and smartphone distraction. The associations of smartphone distraction and pedestrian characteristics with pedestrians’ road crossing behavior were identified by developing structural equation models (SEMs). The strongest influence of smartphone distraction was found to be on violations (factor loading = 0.50), followed by errors (factor loading = 0.44), lapses (factor loading = 0.41), and aggressive behaviors (factor loading = 0.37). No significant effect of smartphone distraction was observed on the positive behaviors. Male pedestrians were more likely to commit violations, errors, lapses, and aggressive behaviors, whereas female pedestrians showed more positive behaviors. Older pedestrians showed more positive behavior than young pedestrians. Pedestrians who walk at high speeds tend to engage in a high number of violations. Pedestrians with a crash history were more likely to commit violations and show aggressive behaviors than those with no prior crash experience. The study findings can aid policymakers in understanding the smartphone distraction habits of pedestrians, which are required to design behavior-based road safety interventions.

**[试图解决的问题]**：智能手机分心对行人过街行为的影响在低中收入国家（尤其印度）缺乏研究，且行人特征与分心如何共同作用于不安全过街行为尚不明确。

**[核心方法]**：基于560名印度行人（63.4%男性）的问卷调查数据（人口学特征、步行特征、行人行为五维度、智能手机分心），构建结构方程模型（SEM）分析分心与行人特征对过街行为的关联。

**[创新与效果]**：首次在印度量化智能手机分心对过街行为的影响，发现其对违规行为影响最强（因子载荷=0.50），其次为错误（0.44）、疏忽（0.41）和攻击性行为（0.37），对积极行为无显著影响；男性、高步行速度、有事故史者更易出现违规/攻击行为，老年人积极行为更多。结果可为行为导向的道路安全干预提供依据。
