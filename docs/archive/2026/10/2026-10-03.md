### 1. [Analysis Over Vision-Based Models for Pedestrian Action Anticipation](https://doi.org/10.1109/ITSC57777.2023.10422283)
`相关度 95` · 视觉行人动作预测直接相关

*2023 IEEE 26th International Conference on Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITSC)* | Lina Achaji, Julien Moreau, F. Aioun, François Charpillet | 2023-05-27

**TLDR:** In insights on the explainability of vision-based Transformer models in the context of pedestrian action prediction, several spatio-temporal model architectures that utilize standard CNN and Transformer modules to serve as a backbone for pedestrian anticipation are provided.

> Anticipating human actions in front of autonomous vehicles is a challenging task. Several papers have recently proposed model architectures to address this problem by combining multiple input features to predict pedestrian crossing actions. This paper focuses specifically on using images of the pedestrian's context as an input feature. We present several spatio-temporal model architectures that utilize standard CNN and Transformer modules to serve as a backbone for pedestrian anticipation. However, the objective of this paper is not to surpass state-of-the-art benchmarks but rather to analyze the positive and negative predictions of these models. Therefore, we provide insights on the explainability of vision-based Transformer models in the context of pedestrian action prediction. We will highlight cases where the model can achieve correct quantitative results but falls short in providing human-like explanations qualitatively, emphasizing the importance of investing in explainability for pedestrian action anticipation problems.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶场景中行人动作预测的挑战性任务，以及现有视觉模型虽能取得正确的量化结果、却缺乏类人可解释性的问题。

**[核心方法]**：以行人环境上下文图像为唯一输入特征，构建多种结合标准CNN与Transformer模块的时空模型架构作为行人预测骨干网络，并对模型的正确/错误预测进行定性分析。

**[创新与效果]**：不追求超越SOTA基准，而是提供基于视觉Transformer模型在行人动作预测中的可解释性洞察；揭示了模型在定量结果正确但定性解释不类人的典型失败案例，强调在行人预测问题中投入可解释性研究的重要性。

### 2. [Stochastic Model Predictive Control of an Autonomous Vehicle Interacting with Pedestrians at Unsignalized Crosswalks](https://doi.org/10.23919/ECC57647.2023.10178144)
`相关度 92` · 无信号人行横道人车交互控制

*European Control Conference* | B. Škugor, J. Deur, V. Ivanovic, H. Tseng | 2023-06-13

**TLDR:** 无

> This paper proposes a safe speed control strategy for an autonomous vehicle while approaching unsignalized crosswalks with pedestrians, which relies on a receding horizon stochastic model predictive control (SMPC). It is assumed that cruising at a certain speed is a main vehicle preference to provide comfortable driving. A stochastic formulation of MPC is employed to account for uncertainties related to pedestrian crossing decisions. The prediction horizon is divided in two phases, i.e., prior and after pedestrian reaching the crosswalk edge. The deterministic cost related to the first phase is calculated online for different vehicle control trajectory parameters, while the probabilistic cost for the second phase is derived from two offline-calculated maps corresponding to two characteristic scenarios, i.e., pedestrian opting for cross and yield when reaching the crosswalk edge. The probabilities of cross and yield decisions depend on vehicle states, and they are obtained from a binary logistic regression prediction model. SMPC is verified against a baseline control strategy by means of large-scale Monte Carlo simulations over a wide range of randomly generated initial conditions.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶车辆在无信号灯人行横道附近与行人交互时，如何在优先保持巡航速度以提供舒适驾驶的同时，安全控制车速以应对行人穿越决策的不确定性。

**[核心方法]**：采用滚动时域随机模型预测控制（SMPC）。将预测时域划分为行人到达人行横道边缘之前和之后两个阶段：第一阶段针对不同车辆控制轨迹参数的确定性代价在线计算；第二阶段针对行人到达边缘后选择“通过”或“让行”两种情景的概率性代价由离线计算的两张映射图获得。行人通过/让行决策概率取决于车辆状态，由二元逻辑回归预测模型估计。

**[创新与效果]**：提出将 SMPC 与基于逻辑回归的行人决策概率模型结合的安全速度控制策略，并利用大规模蒙特卡洛仿真在随机初始条件范围内与基线控制策略对比验证，表明该策略能有效应对行人决策不确定性并兼顾车辆巡航偏好。

### 3. [Research on pedestrian vehicle collision warning based on path prediction](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICTIS60134.2023.10243767)
`相关度 90` · 行人路径预测用于碰撞预警

*International Conference on Transportation Information and Safety* | Yue Zhang, Xu Wang, ..., Wenjian Jiao, Wenxin Yang | 2023-08-04

**TLDR:** 无

> The vehicle FCW (forward collision warning) system is supposed to considerable reduce the traffic accidents caused by pedestrian crossing. The pedestrian path prediction method in FCW is studied, and the five-freedom-degree vehicle model is analyzed, the limit condition where whether the maximum barking acceleration can avoid collision effectively is researched. The vehicle coordinate is divided according to the psychological safety distance of pedestrians, and an anti-collision warning system is designed on basis of the pedestrian initial position, the movement speed and path. The test results show that the designed anti-collision warning system can effectively warn the driver before the collision accident occurs and avoid pedestrian traffic accidents caused by the negligence of the drivers.

**[试图解决的问题]**：行人横穿道路导致的车辆前方碰撞事故，现有FCW系统在路径预测与有效预警方面存在不足。

**[核心方法]**：采用基于路径预测的防碰撞预警算法；分析五自由度车辆模型，推导最大制动加速度能否避免碰撞的极限条件；依据行人心理安全距离划分车辆坐标区域，并结合行人初始位置、运动速度与路径设计防撞预警系统。

**[创新与效果]**：设计了一套基于行人运动状态与心理安全距离的预警系统，测试表明该系统能在碰撞发生前有效警告驾驶员，从而避免因驾驶员疏忽引发的行人交通事故。

### 4. [Risky Behavior of Pedestrians Crossing the Street in front of New Energy Vehicles](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICTIS60134.2023.10243885)
`相关度 90` · 行人过街风险行为直接研究

*International Conference on Transportation Information and Safety* | Yue Li, Hongmei Zhou, Siyi Fu, Wenwen Wang | 2023-08-04

**TLDR:** 无

> As the number of new energy vehicles equipped with autonomous driving technology increases, the degree of interaction between vehicles and pedestrians is different, which affects the accuracy of pedestrians’ judgment of vehicle behavior and pedestrians’ behavior of crossing the street. Therefore, it is necessary to study road traffic safety. In this paper, SP questionnaire was used to obtain data of pedestrians’ intention to take risks across the street. Based on the theory of extended planned behavior, the formation mechanism model of pedestrians’ intention to take risks across the street was established. On the basis of traditional dimensions, two variables of trust and risk perception were expanded, and a structural equation model was used to verify and improve the model. From the aspect of path relationship and the effect of each factor, analyze the relationship between the variables and the degree of influence. The results show that the extended TPB model can effectively explain pedestrian risk-taking behavior, and the role of risk perception, trust,and perceived behavior control on risk-taking behavior intention is more prominent. The k-means clustering method is adopted to cluster the people who take risks to cross the street, which can be divided into ignoring rules (risk-taking type), flexible following rules (stable type),and strict following rules (prudent type). Therefore, the study of pedestrian risk behavior intention is helpful to traffic safety management and improves traffic efficiency.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶新能源车与行人交互模式变化，导致行人过街风险判断不准、交通事故隐患增加。

**[核心方法]**：基于扩展计划行为理论（TPB）建模，在传统维度上新增“信任”与“风险感知”两个变量；采用SP问卷获取行人冒险过街意图数据，用结构方程模型（SEM）验证并修正模型；并用k-means聚类对冒险人群分型。

**[创新与效果]**：证实扩展TPB能有效解释行人冒险过街行为；风险感知、信任、感知行为控制对冒险意图影响更显著；k-means将行人聚类为“漠视规则型（冒险型）”、“灵活守规型（稳定型）”、“严格守规型（谨慎型）”，为交通安全分类管理和提升通行效率提供依据。

### 5. [Multi-Modal Interaction-Aware Motion Prediction at Unsignalized Intersections](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIV.2023.3254657)
`相关度 80` · 交叉口多模态交互运动预测

*IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Vehicles* | Vinicius Trentin, Antonio Artuñedo, Jorge Godoy, Jorge Villagrá | 2023-05-01

**TLDR:** 无

> Autonomous vehicle technologies have evolved quickly over the last few years, with safety being one of the key requirements for their full deployment. However, ensuring their safety while navigating through highly interactive and complex scenarios remains a critical challenge. To tackle this problem, intention estimation and motion prediction are fundamental. In this work, a method to infer the intentions, based on a Dynamic Bayesian Network (DBN), and predict the motion, using Markov Chains, of the nearby vehicles at unsignalized intersections is proposed. This approach considers all possible corridors of the surrounding traffic participants and takes into account their interactions to infer the probabilities of stopping or crossing the intersection, as well as the probability of being in each of the possible navigable corridors. To achieve this, the DBN is used to model the relationships between the observed states and the unobserved intentions of the nearby agents. The Markov Chain model, obtained from a kinematic model, is used to predict the future motions of the vehicles, taking into account their current state, their inferred intentions, and the uncertainty associated with the prediction. The resulting multi-modal motion predictions are sent to the ego vehicle to navigate through the scene. The proposed method is evaluated in 6 real situations extracted from publicly available datasets and is compared with a model-based and a learn-based baseline models. The results showed that the proposed method outperformed both baselines in terms of accuracy considering the metrics ADE and FDE.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶车辆在无信号交叉口这一高交互复杂场景中，如何利用周围车辆的意图估计与多模态运动预测保障安全。

**[核心方法]**：构建动态贝叶斯网络（DBN）推断周围车辆未观测的意图（停车/通过及所在可行走廊），并结合运动学模型导出的马尔可夫链（Markov Chain）预测未来运动，显式建模交通参与者之间的交互与多模态不确定性。

**[创新与效果]**：提出一种同时考虑所有可行走廊及交互的意图与运动联合预测框架，在6个真实公开数据集场景中，以ADE和FDE为指标，精度优于模型基线和学习基线方法。
