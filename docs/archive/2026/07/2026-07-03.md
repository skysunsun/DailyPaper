**TLDR:** A novel paradigm of Sparse Transformer Networks with Spatial-Temporal Graph (STGSTN), which captures complex spatial-temporal interactions by stacking spatial-temporal Transformer blocks and improves the accuracy of pedestrian trajectory prediction by combining dynamic spatial dependence and long-range temporal dependence.

> Pedestrian trajectory prediction is a key technology in surveillance systems and autonomous driving. However, due to the high uncertainty and dynamic spatial-temporal dependence of pedestrian movement, timely and accurate pedestrian trajectory prediction, especially long-term prediction, is still an open challenge. However, the existing models lack an effective modeling method for temporal dependence and spatial interaction modeling. To solve these problems, in this paper, we propose a novel paradigm of Sparse Transformer Networks with Spatial-Temporal Graph (STGSTN), which captures complex spatial-temporal interactions by stacking spatial-temporal Transformer blocks and improves the accuracy of pedestrian trajectory prediction by combining dynamic spatial dependence and long-range temporal dependence. We propose a new variant of sparse spatial transformer combined with graph neural networks, which uses the self-attention mechanism to dynamically model spatial dependencies to capture the state of pedestrian movement. The multi-head attention mechanism is also used to jointly model various patterns of spatial dependence. In addition, the sparse temporal transformer is used to learn the sparse attention map for time modeling, and then the long-range temporal dependence between pedestrians is modeled. Compared with the existing work, STGSTN can effectively and efficiently train long-range spatial-temporal dependencies. Experimental results show that STGSTN is competitive with state-of-the-art techniques on the ETH-UCY dataset, especially for long-term prediction. The performance of STGSTN may be limited by the quality of the training data, and its generalization to highly dynamic or crowded environments remains a challenge. Future work will focus on addressing these limitations and exploring additional contextual information to further enhance prediction accuracy.

**[核心方法]**：提出STGSTN（稀疏时空Transformer网络），堆叠时空Transformer块：稀疏空间Transformer结合图神经网络，通过自注意力动态建模空间依赖；稀疏时间Transformer学习稀疏注意力图，建模行人间的长程时间依赖。

**[创新与效果]**：在ETH-UCY数据集上达到与SOTA竞争的性能，尤其显著提升长期预测精度；但模型性能受训练数据质量限制，在高度动态或拥挤场景中泛化性仍有挑战。

[Goal-Oriented Pedestrian Trajectory Prediction Considering Spatial–Temporal Interactions](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIM.2024.3472774)
*IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement* | Hongyan Guo, Yanran Liu, ..., Jialin Li, Hong Chen | 2024

**TLDR:** 

> Accurately predicting pedestrian trajectories in complex and dynamic scenarios is vital for the success of intelligent transportation systems (ITSs) and autonomous vehicles (AVs). Given the flexible and purpose-driven nature of pedestrian behavior, not only the interaction in time and space needs to be considered in dense scenes, but also the guidance and restraint effects of pedestrian moving goals need to be considered. However, previous approaches ignore the spatial–temporal interactions with neighboring pedestrians when estimating goals. Accordingly, a goal-oriented spatial–temporal (GOST) pedestrian trajectory prediction network is proposed. The GOST algorithm models temporal dependence and spatial interactivity separately. It then extracts spatial–temporal interaction features to estimate goals and predict trajectories. Based on this, a novel temporal pattern attention (TPA) mechanism based on the gated recurrent unit (GRU), named TPA-GRU, is introduced to capture the dependence of historical temporal information on a single pedestrian. In addition, a two-layer multihead graph attention network (GAT) is devised to effectively aggregate direct and indirect spatial interaction features. After fusing spatial–temporal interaction features, a conditional variational autoencoder (CVAE) is employed to estimate more reasonable social movement goals, thereby improving the accuracy of predicted trajectories. Experiments on ETH and UCY datasets validate the superior performance of our method, showing a reduction of 25.71% in average displacement error (ADE) and 18.64% in final displacement error (FDE).

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有行人轨迹预测方法在估计行人运动目标时，忽略了与邻近行人的时空交互作用，导致密集场景下预测精度不足。  
**[核心方法]**：提出GOST网络，采用TPA-GRU（基于门控循环单元的时间模式注意力）捕获单一行人的历史时间依赖，使用两层多头图注意力网络（GAT）聚合直接与间接空间交互特征，并通过条件变分自编码器（CVAE）融合时空交互特征来估计合理的社会运动目标。  
**[创新与效果]**：在ETH和UCY数据集上，平均位移误差（ADE）降低25.71%，最终位移误差（FDE）降低18.64%。
