### 1. [Pedestrian Safety by Intent Prediction: A Lightweight LSTM-Attention Architecture and Experimental Evaluations with Real-World Datasets](https://doi.org/10.1109/IV55156.2024.10588868)
`相关度 95` · 直接研究行人过街意图预测

*2024 IEEE Intelligent Vehicles Symposium (IV)* | Afnan Alofi, Ross Greer, Akshay Gopalkrishnan, Mohan M. Trivedi | 2024-06-02

**TLDR:** 无

> Autonomous vehicles face significant challenges in understanding pedestrian behavior, particularly in urban environments. In such settings, the system must recognize pedestrian intentions and anticipate their actions to achieve safe and intelligent driving. This paper focuses on predicting pedestrian crossings, enabling oncoming vehicles to react to pedestrians in a traffic scene in a timely manner. We investigate the effectiveness of various input features for pedestrian crossing prediction, including human poses, bounding boxes, and ego vehicle speed features. We propose a novel lightweight architecture based on LSTM and attention to accurately identify crossing pedestrians. Our methods are evaluated on two widely used public datasets for pedestrian behavior, PIE and JAAD datasets, and our algorithm achieves a state-of-the-art performance in both datasets by reaching a prediction accuracy of 91% and an F1-score of 84% on the PIE dataset and an accuracy of 67% and an F1-score of 77% on the JAAD Behavior (BEH) split. We make our code available at https://github.com/afnan29alofi/Pedestrian-Intent-Prediction.git

**[试图解决的问题]**：城市环境中自动驾驶车辆难以准确预测行人过马路意图，导致安全反应延迟。  
**[核心方法]**：基于LSTM和注意力机制的轻量级架构，融合人体姿态、边界框和自车速度等多模态输入特征进行行人过街预测。  
**[创新与效果]**：在PIE数据集上达到91%准确率和84% F1分数，在JAAD Behavior分割上达到67%准确率和77% F1分数，实现当前最优性能，模型轻量高效且代码开源。

### 2. [RNN-based Pedestrian Crossing Prediction using Activity and Pose-related Features](https://doi.org/10.1109/IV47402.2020.9304652)
`相关度 95` · RNN行人过街预测

*2020 IEEE Intelligent Vehicles Symposium (IV)* | J. Lorenzo, I. Parra, ..., D. F. Llorca, M. Sotelo | 2020-08-26

**TLDR:** 无

> Pedestrian crossing prediction is a crucial task for autonomous driving. Numerous studies show that an early estimation of the pedestrian's intention can decrease or even avoid a high percentage of accidents. In this paper, different variations of a deep learning system are proposed to attempt to solve this problem. The proposed models are composed of two parts: a CNN-based feature extractor and an RNN module. All the models were trained and tested on the JAAD dataset. The results obtained indicate that the choice of the features extraction method, the inclusion of additional variables such as pedestrian gaze direction and discrete orientation, and the chosen RNN type have a significant impact on the final performance.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶中行人过街意图的早期预测，以减少事故发生率。  
**[核心方法]**：采用CNN特征提取器与RNN模块结合的深度学习架构，输入包含行人活动、姿态、注视方向及离散朝向等特征。  
**[创新与效果]**：在JAAD数据集上验证了特征提取方式、额外变量（注视方向、离散朝向）及RNN类型对预测性能的显著影响，实现更准确的早期意图估计。

### 3. [A Real-Time Predictive Pedestrian Collision Warning Service for Cooperative Intelligent Transportation Systems Using 3D Pose Estimation](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1aee84b8c12ae3da10fb9a764e9f97f2ac3a0e67)
`相关度 90` · 行人过街意图实时预测

*未知会议/期刊* | Ue-Hwan Kim, Dongho Ka, H. Yeo, Jong-Hwan Kim | 2020-09-23

**TLDR:** 无

> Minimizing traffic accidents between vehicles and pedestrians is one of the primary research goals in intelligent transportation systems. To achieve the goal, pedestrian orientation recognition and prediction of pedestrian's crossing or not-crossing intention play a central role. Contemporary approaches do not guarantee satisfactory performance due to limited field-of-view, lack of generalization, and high computational complexity. To overcome these limitations, we propose a real-time predictive pedestrian collision warning service (P2CWS) for two tasks: pedestrian orientation recognition (100.53 FPS) and intention prediction (35.76 FPS). Our framework obtains satisfying generalization over multiple sites because of the proposed site-independent features. At the center of the feature extraction lies 3D pose estimation. The 3D pose analysis enables robust and accurate recognition of pedestrian orientations and prediction of intentions over multiple sites. The proposed vision framework realizes 89.3% accuracy in the behavior recognition task on the TUD dataset without any training process and 91.28% accuracy in intention prediction on our dataset achieving new state-of-the-art performance. To contribute to the corresponding research community, we make our source codes public which are available at https://github.com/Uehwan/VisionForPedestrian

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有行人方向识别与过街意图预测方法受限于视野有限、缺乏泛化性及高计算复杂度，难以保证实时性和跨站点性能。  
**[核心方法]**：提出实时预测性碰撞预警服务（P2CWS），基于3D姿态估计提取站点无关特征，分别以100.53 FPS和35.76 FPS完成行人方向识别与意图预测。  
**[创新与效果]**：在TUD数据集上无需训练即达89.3%行为识别准确率，在自建数据集上意图预测准确率91.28%，创下新SOTA。

### 4. [Using Graph Convolutional Networks Skeleton-Based Pedestrian Intention Estimation Models for Trajectory Prediction](https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-6596/1621/1/012047)
`相关度 90` · 骨架意图估计用于轨迹预测

*Journal of Physics: Conference Series* | Dong Cao, Yunbin Fu | 2020-08-01

**TLDR:** 无

> In autonomous driving scenarios, pedestrian trajectory prediction is an important research direction. Based on the spatio-temporal graph convolutional neural network, we propose a new pedestrian trajectory prediction algorithm. The new algorithm constructs a series of new models around pedestrian intention estimation. The construction of the estimation algorithm considers the following aspects: the contextual information of pedestrians and the surrounding environment, the “pedestrian ego-vehicle” interaction combined with the vehicle speed estimation, the pedestrian’s own skeletal structure information and body language estimation, which includes head joints and the relative structural relationship of the torso joints, including whether it is out of the same plane, is rotated, and so on. Skeleton information feature extraction and construction adopts the method of graph convolutional neural network to structure pedestrians into joints in the form of graphs in non-Euclidean space, and further adopts spatial temporal graph convolutional network for feature extraction and learning. The new method is named a “head-torso”-based spatial temporal graph convolutional network (HT-STGCN). On the dataset PID, the novel method achieves substantial improvements over mainstream methods. Experimental results show that combining HT-STGCN with observed action can improve trajectory prediction.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶场景下，现有行人轨迹预测方法对行人意图（如身体语言、与自车交互）的建模不足，导致预测精度受限。  
**[核心方法]**：提出基于头部-躯干时空图卷积网络（HT-STGCN），将行人骨架关节（头部、躯干节点及相对结构关系）构建为图，采用时空图卷积网络（ST-GCN）提取特征，并融合行人-环境上下文、行人-自车交互（含车速估计）等多模态信息。  
**[创新与效果]**：在PID数据集上，HT-STGCN结合观测动作显著提升轨迹预测精度，性能超越主流方法。

### 5. [Long-Term Pedestrian Trajectory Prediction Using Mutable Intention Filter and Warp LSTM](https://doi.org/10.1109/LRA.2020.3047731)
`相关度 90` · 长期轨迹预测结合意图滤波

*IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters* | Zhe Huang, Aamir Hasan, ..., Ruohua Li, K. Driggs-Campbell | 2020-06-30

**TLDR:** 无

> Trajectory prediction is one of the key capabilities for robots to safely navigate and interact with pedestrians. Critical insights from human intention and behavioral patterns need to be integrated to effectively forecast long-term pedestrian behavior. Thus, we propose a framework incorporating a mutable intention filter and a Warp LSTM (MIF-WLSTM) to simultaneously estimate human intention and perform trajectory prediction. The mutable intention filter is inspired by particle filtering and genetic algorithms, where particles represent intention hypotheses that can be mutated throughout the pedestrian’s motion. Instead of predicting sequential displacement over time, our Warp LSTM learns to generate offsets on a full trajectory predicted by a nominal intention-aware linear model, which considers the intention hypotheses during filtering process. Through experiments on a publicly available dataset, we show that our method outperforms baseline approaches and demonstrate the robust performance of our method under abnormal intention-changing scenarios.

**[试图解决的问题]**：长期行人轨迹预测需融合人类意图与行为模式，现有方法在意图动态变化场景下预测精度不足。  
**[核心方法]**：提出MIF-WLSTM框架，包含基于粒子滤波与遗传算法的可变意图过滤器（粒子代表可变异的意图假设），以及Warp LSTM（学习生成对名义意图感知线性模型预测的完整轨迹的偏移量）。  
**[创新与效果]**：在公开数据集上超越基线方法，并在意图异常变化场景下展现鲁棒性能。
