The input of FSP is the information in the two frames of a panoramic camera. First, the free pedestrian prediction network (FPPN) is used to predict the free movement of pedestrians. **[创新与效果]**：采用全景相机消除视野盲区，仅利用两帧图像预测以提升实时性；在自建全景视频数据集上，FSP的预测性能优于时空编码器-解码器（STED）和未来人物定位（FPL）模型。

*AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence* | Inhwan Bae, Hae-Gon Jeon | 2023-06-26

**TLDR:** A graph convolutional network-based trajectory prediction that divides the future path into three sections and infers the intermediate destinations of pedestrians to reduce the accumulated error and achieves state-of-the-art performance on various real-world trajectory prediction benchmarks.

> Predicting the trajectories of pedestrians in crowded conditions is an important task for applications like autonomous navigation systems. Previous studies have tackled this problem using two strategies. They (1) infer all future steps recursively, or (2) predict the potential destinations of pedestrians at once and interpolate the intermediate steps to arrive there. However, these strategies often suffer from the accumulated errors of the recursive inference, or restrictive assumptions about social relations in the intermediate path. In this paper, we present a graph convolutional network-based trajectory prediction. Firstly, we propose a control point prediction that divides the future path into three sections and infers the intermediate destinations of pedestrians to reduce the accumulated error. To do this, we construct multi-relational weighted graphs to account for their physical and complex social relations. We then introduce a trajectory refinement step based on a spatio-temporal and multi-relational graph. By considering the social interactions between neighbors, better prediction results are achievable. In experiments, the proposed network achieves state-of-the-art performance on various real-world trajectory prediction benchmarks.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有行人轨迹预测方法中，递归推理导致累积误差，或一次性预测终点但中间路径的社交关系假设受限。
**[核心方法]**：基于图卷积网络，提出控制点预测将未来路径分为三段并推断中间目的地；构建多关系加权图建模物理与复杂社交关系；引入基于时空与多关系图的轨迹细化步骤。
**[创新与效果]**：在多个真实世界轨迹预测基准上达到最先进性能，显著降低递归推理的累积误差。

**TLDR:** A deep learning-based method for pedestrian trajectory prediction and risk assessment is proposed, using trajectory data extracted from roadside LiDAR data and corresponding signal phasing information at MLK and Georgia Avenue in Chattanooga, TN, demonstrating the efficacy of the proposed LSTM-based methodology.

> Abstract In recent years, rapid advancements in the Autonomous Vehicles (AVs) industry have greatly motivated the research and development in pedestrian trajectory prediction and risk assessment. One of the critical requirements for AVs is to predict the future trajectories of pedestrians and provide collision warnings in an accurate and prompt manner. Nevertheless, accurate prediction of pedestrian trajectories remains a technical challenge, mainly caused by the heterogeneity of pedestrian crossing behavior and uncertainties in vehicle-pedestrian interactions. This paper proposes a deep learning-based method for pedestrian trajectory prediction and risk assessment, using trajectory data extracted from roadside LiDAR data and corresponding signal phasing information at MLK and Georgia Avenue in Chattanooga, TN. Meanwhile, a set of criteria referred to as the risk factor is established to quantitatively evaluate the risk of the pedestrian crossing behavior, which also serves as a learnable feature. A Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) network is proposed, which takes the following data as the input: the pedestrian trajectory data, signal phasing data, and risk factors from the past 10 steps. Meanwhile, the network predicts the pedestrian trajectory and risk factor at the future time step. In the experimental study, the root-mean-square errors between the predicted and ground truth x and y coordinates are 0.225 meters and 0.377 meters, respectively, and the F1 score value for the risk factor is 99.6%, demonstrating the efficacy of the proposed LSTM-based methodology on pedestrian trajectory prediction and risk assessment.

**[试图解决的问题]**：信号交叉口行人轨迹预测准确度低，因行人过街行为异质性与车辆-行人交互不确定性导致的技术挑战。  
**[核心方法]**：提出基于LSTM的深度学习模型，以过去10步的行人轨迹、信号相位数据和风险因子作为输入，同时预测未来时间步的行人轨迹和风险因子。风险因子作为可学习特征，用于定量评估行人过街风险。  
**[创新与效果]**：建立了风险因子评估标准并融入预测框架；实验表明轨迹坐标预测RMSE为x=0.225米、y=0.377米，风险因子F1分数达99.6%。
