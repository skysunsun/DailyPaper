### 1. [TAMformer: Multi-Modal Transformer with Learned Attention Mask for Early Intent Prediction](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICASSP49357.2023.10095781)
`相关度 95` · 直接预测行人过街早期意图

*IEEE International Conference on Acoustics, Speech, and Signal Processing* | Nada Osman, Guglielmo Camporese, Lamberto Ballan | 2022-10-26

**TLDR:** This work focuses on pedestrians’ early intention prediction in which, from a current observation of an urban scene, the model predicts the future activity of pedestrians that approach the street, based on a multi-modal transformer that encodes past observations and produces multiple predictions at different anticipation times.

> Human intention prediction is a growing area of research where an activity in a video has to be anticipated by a vision-based system. To this end, the model creates a representation of the past, and subsequently, it produces future hypotheses about upcoming scenarios. In this work, we focus on pedestrians’ early intention prediction in which, from a current observation of an urban scene, the model predicts the future activity of pedestrians that approach the street. Our method is based on a multi-modal transformer that encodes past observations and produces multiple predictions at different anticipation times. Moreover, we propose to learn the attention masks of our transformer-based model (Temporal Adaptive Mask Transformer) in order to weigh differently present and past temporal dependencies. We investigate our method on several public benchmarks for early intention prediction, improving the prediction performances at different anticipation times compared to the previous works.

**[试图解决的问题]**：从城市场景的当前观测中，早期预测即将过马路的行人的未来活动，以提升不同提前预测时间下的表现。

**[核心方法]**：基于多模态Transformer（Temporal Adaptive Mask Transformer），通过可学习的注意力掩码来差异化地加权当前与过去的时间依赖关系，编码历史观测并生成多个提前时间点的预测。

**[创新与效果]**：在多个行人早期意图预测公开基准上，相比以往方法，在不同提前预测时间下均提升了预测性能。

### 2. [Predicting Pedestrian Intention to Cross the Road](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2020.2987777)
`相关度 95` · 直接预测行人过马路意图

*IEEE Access* | Karam M. Abughalieh, Shadi G. Alawneh | 2020-04-13

**TLDR:** A Convolutional Neural Network model combined with depth sensing camera to estimate the pedestrian orientation and distance from the vehicle and any change in the pedestrian moving pattern toward the road is translated to a warning for the driver.

> The goal of this research is the development of a driver assistant feature, which can warn the driver in case a pedestrian is in a potential risk due to sudden intention to cross the road. The process of crossing pedestrian is defined as the changing of pedestrian orientation on the curb toward the road. We built a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) model combined with depth sensing camera to estimate the pedestrian orientation and distance from the vehicle. The model detects the higher human body keypoints in 2D space while the depth info make it possible to translate the points into a 3D space. These info are tracked per pedestrian and any change in the pedestrian moving pattern toward the road is translated to a warning for the driver. The CNN model is end-end trained using different datasets presenting pedestrian in different configurations and scenes.

**[试图解决的问题]**：驾驶员辅助系统需要提前预警行人可能突然横穿道路的风险。
**[核心方法]**：结合深度摄像头的CNN模型，在2D空间检测人体关键点，并利用深度信息将其映射到3D空间，追踪行人相对于车辆的朝向和距离，监测行人从路边向道路的运动模式变化。
**[创新与效果]**：实现端到端训练，使用多个不同场景数据集；通过识别行人朝向改变触发预警，提升了行人横穿意图的预测准确性。

### 3. [The 'invisible gorilla' during pedestrian-AV interaction: Effects of secondary tasks on pedestrians' reaction to eHMIs.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aap.2023.107246)
`相关度 90` · 行人-AV交互与过街决策

*Accident Analysis and Prevention* | Xiaoyuan Zhao, X. Li, ..., D. Gruyer, P. Delhomme | 2023-08-17

**TLDR:** It was found that the presence of a warning eHMI on AVs contributes to a clearer understanding of pedestrians' inferences about the intention of AVs and helps deter late and dangerous crossing decisions made by pedestrians, however, the eH MI fail to help pedestrians avoid such decisions when they face a high mental workload induced by secondary task engagement.

> In road traffic, mental overload often leads to a failure to notice new and distinctive stimuli. Such phenomenon is known as 'inattentional blindness'. Safe and efficient interaction between automated vehicles (AVs) and pedestrians is expected to rely heavily on external human-machine interfaces (eHMIs), a tool AVs are equipped with to communicate their intentions to pedestrians. This study seeks to explore the phenomenon of 'inattentional blindness' in the context of pedestrian-AV interactions. Specifically, the aim is to understand the effects of a warning eHMI on pedestrians' crossing decisions when they are engaged in a secondary task. In an experiment study with videos of pedestrian crossing scenarios filmed from the perspective of the crossing pedestrian, participants had to decide the latest point at which they would be willing to cross the road in front of an AV with an eHMI vs. an AV without an eHMI. Participants were also asked to predict the future behavior of the AV. 125 female and 9 male participants aged between 18 and 25 completed the experiment and a follow-up questionnaire. It was found that the presence of a warning eHMI on AVs contributes to a clearer understanding of pedestrians' inferences about the intention of AVs and helps deter late and dangerous crossing decisions made by pedestrians. However, the eHMI fail to help pedestrians avoid such decisions when they face a high mental workload induced by secondary task engagement.

**[试图解决的问题]**：行人在与自动驾驶车辆交互时，因执行次要任务导致心理负荷过高而忽略eHMI警告信号（非注意盲视），从而做出危险过街决策。  
**[核心方法]**：通过视频模拟行人过街场景，比较有/无警告型eHMI的AV，要求被试决定最晚安全过街时机并预测AV行为，同时施加次要任务以操纵心理负荷。  
**[创新与效果]**：警告型eHMI能帮助行人更清晰推断AV意图并减少延迟/危险过街决策，但在高心理负荷（次要任务）下，eHMI无法有效防止此类危险决策。

### 4. [Where are you heading? Dynamic Trajectory Prediction with Expert Goal Examples](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCV48922.2021.00753)
`相关度 90` · 多行人轨迹预测，直接相关

*IEEE International Conference on Computer Vision* | He Zhao, Richard P. Wildes | 2021-10-01

**TLDR:** 无

> Goal-conditioned approaches recently have been found very useful to human trajectory prediction, when adequate goal estimates are provided. Yet, goal inference is difficult in itself and often incurs extra learning effort. We propose to predict pedestrian trajectories via the guidance of goal expertise, which can be obtained with modest expense through a novel goal-search mechanism on already seen training examples. There are three key contributions in our study. First, we devise a framework that exploits nearest examples for high-quality goal position inquiry. This approach naturally considers multi-modality, physical constraints, compatibility with existing methods and is nonparametric; it therefore does not require additional learning effort typical in goal inference. Second, we present an end-to-end trajectory predictor that can efficiently associate goal retrievals to past motion information and dynamically infer possible future trajectories. Third, with these two novel techniques in hand, we conduct a series of experiments on two broadly explored datasets (SDD and ETH/UCY) and show that our approach surpasses previous state-of-the-art performance by notable margins and reduces the need for additional parameters. Code can be found at our project page1.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有目标条件轨迹预测方法依赖昂贵且困难的目标推断，且需额外学习成本。  
**[核心方法]**：提出非参数化目标搜索机制，从训练样本中检索最近邻专家示例作为目标引导，结合端到端轨迹预测器动态关联历史运动与目标检索结果。  
**[创新与效果]**：在SDD和ETH/UCY数据集上超越先前最优方法，显著提升预测性能，同时减少额外参数需求。

### 5. [Trajectory Prediction with Latent Belief Energy-Based Model](https://doi.org/10.1109/CVPR46437.2021.01164)
`相关度 90` · 基于能量模型的行人轨迹预测

*Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition* | Bo Pang, Tianyang Zhao, Xujun Xie, Y. Wu | 2021-04-07

**TLDR:** The LB-EBM model is able to make accurate, multi-modal, and social compliant trajectory predictions and improves over prior state-of-the-arts performance on the Stanford Drone trajectory prediction benchmark by 10.9% and on the ETH-UCY benchmark by 27.6%.

> Human trajectory prediction is critical for autonomous platforms like self-driving cars or social robots. We present a latent belief energy-based model (LB-EBM) for diverse human trajectory forecast. LB-EBM is a probabilistic model with cost function defined in the latent space to account for the movement history and social context. The low-dimensionality of the latent space and the high expressivity of the EBM make it easy for the model to capture the multi-modality of pedestrian trajectory distributions. LB-EBM is learned from expert demonstrations (i.e., human trajectories) projected into the latent space. Sampling from or optimizing the learned LB-EBM yields a belief vector which is used to make a path plan, which then in turn helps to predict a long-range trajectory. The effectiveness of LB-EBM and the two-step approach are supported by strong empirical results. Our model is able to make accurate, multi-modal, and social compliant trajectory predictions and improves over prior state-of-the-arts performance on the Stanford Drone trajectory prediction benchmark by 10.9% and on the ETH-UCY benchmark by 27.6%.

**[试图解决的问题]**：人类轨迹预测需同时处理多模态分布和社会交互约束，现有方法在复杂场景下精度不足。

**[核心方法]**：提出潜在信念能量模型（LB-EBM），在低维潜在空间定义能量函数以编码运动历史与社会上下文，通过从专家轨迹投影中学习并采样/优化得到信念向量，再由信念向量驱动路径规划以生成长期轨迹。

**[创新与效果]**：在Stanford Drone数据集上较先前最优提升10.9%，在ETH-UCY数据集上提升27.6%，实现准确、多模态且符合社会规范的轨迹预测。
