### 1. [From Recognition to Prediction: Analysis of Human Action and Trajectory Prediction in Video](https://doi.org/10.1184/r1/21692891.v1)
`相关度 92` · 行人轨迹与动作预测，直接

*未知会议/期刊* | Junwei Liang | 2020-11-20

**TLDR:** To enable optimal future human behavioral forecasting, it is crucial for the system to be able to detect and analyze human activities as well as scene semantics, passing informative features to the subsequent prediction module for context understanding.

> With the advancement in computer vision deep learning, systems now are able to analyze an unprecedented amount of rich visual information from videos to enable applications such as autonomous driving, socially-aware robot assistant and public safety monitoring. Deciphering human behaviors to predict their future paths/trajectories and what they would do from videos is important in these applications. However, human trajectory prediction still remains a challenging task, as scene semantics and human intent are difficult to model. Many systems do not provide high-level semantic attributes to reason about pedestrian future. This design hinders prediction performance in video data from diverse domains and unseen scenarios. To enable optimal future human behavioral forecasting, it is crucial for the system to be able to detect and analyze human activities as well as scene semantics, passing informative features to the subsequent prediction module for context understanding.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有轨迹预测系统缺乏对场景语义和人类意图的高层语义建模，导致在多样化视频域和未见场景中预测性能受限。  
**[核心方法]**：构建一个端到端系统，先进行人类活动识别与场景语义分析，再将提取的信息特征传递至后续轨迹预测模块，实现上下文感知的预测。  
**[创新与效果]**：通过融合动作识别与场景语义，使预测模块获得更丰富的上下文信息，从而提升轨迹预测的准确性和泛化能力，克服了传统方法仅依赖低层视觉特征的局限。

### 2. [Deciding when to cross in front of an autonomous vehicle: How child and adult pedestrians respond to eHMI timing and vehicle kinematics.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aap.2024.107567)
`相关度 90` · 行人过街决策与eHMI，直接

*Accident Analysis and Prevention* | Lakshmi Devi Subramanian, E. O'Neal, ..., J. Plumert, Joseph K. Kearney | 2024-04-25

**TLDR:** 无

> How autonomous vehicles (AVs) communicate their intentions to vulnerable road users (e.g., pedestrians) is a concern given the rapid growth and adoption of this technology. At present, little is known about how children respond to external Human Machine Interface (eHMI) signals from AVs. The current study examined how adults and children respond to the combination of explicit (eHMI signals) and implicit information (vehicle deceleration) to guide their road-crossing decisions. Children (8- to 12-year-olds) and adults made decisions about when to cross in front of a driverless car in an immersive virtual environment. The car sometimes stopped, either abruptly or gradually (manipulated within subjects), to allow participants to cross. When yielding, the car communicated its intent via a dome light that changed from red to green and varied in its timing onset (manipulated between subjects): early eHMI onset, late eHMI onset, or control (no eHMI). As expected, we found that both children and adults waited longer to enter the roadway when vehicles decelerated abruptly than gradually. However, adults responded to the early eHMI signal by crossing sooner when the cars decelerated either gradually or abruptly compared to the control condition. Children were heavily influenced by the late eHMI signal, crossing later when the eHMI signal appeared late and the vehicle decelerated either gradually or abruptly compared to the control condition. Unlike adults, children in the control condition behaved similarly to children in the early eHMI condition by crossing before the yielding vehicle came to a stop. Together, these findings suggest that early eHMI onset may lead to riskier behavior (initiating crossing well before a gradually decelerating vehicle comes to a stop), whereas late eHMI onset may lead to safer behavior (waiting for the eHMI signal to appear before initiating crossing). Without an eHMI signal, children show a concerning overreliance on gradual vehicle deceleration to judge yielding intent.

**[试图解决的问题]**：儿童和成人行人在自动驾驶汽车（AV）前过马路时，如何受外部人机界面（eHMI）信号时机（早/晚/无）与车辆减速方式（急刹/缓刹）的交互影响，尤其是儿童对eHMI的响应模式尚不明确。

**[核心方法]**：在沉浸式虚拟环境中，被试（8-12岁儿童与成人）面对无人驾驶车辆，车辆以被试内方式呈现急刹或缓刹让行，同时以被试间方式操纵车顶灯eHMI从红变绿的时机（早触发、晚触发、无信号），记录被试进入车道的时机。

**[创新与效果]**：早eHMI使成人在两种减速下均更早过马路（冒险行为），而晚eHMI使儿童在两种减速下均更晚过马路（更安全）；无eHMI时儿童表现出过度依赖车辆缓刹判断让行意图的危险倾向。

### 3. [Communication between Autonomous Vehicles and Pedestrians: An Experimental Study Using Virtual Reality](https://doi.org/10.3390/s23031049)
`相关度 88` · VR实验行人过街反应，强相关

*Italian National Conference on Sensors* | Symbat Zhanguzhinova, Emese Makó, ..., Ágoston Pál Sándor, C. Koren | 2023-01-17

**TLDR:** This research composed a virtual reality experiment for a pedestrian crossing in an urban environment in order to test pedestrians' reactions on an LED light display mounted on a virtual AV and found that explicit communication patterns are self-explaining.

> One of the major challenges of autonomous vehicles (AV) is their interaction with pedestrians. Unofficial interactions such as gestures, eye contact, waving, and flashing lights are very common behavioral patterns for drivers to express their intent to give priority. In our research we composed a virtual reality experiment for a pedestrian crossing in an urban environment in order to test pedestrians’ reactions on an LED light display mounted on a virtual AV. Our main research interest was to investigate whether communication patterns influence the decision making of pedestrians when crossing the road. In a VR environment, four scenarios were created with a vehicle approaching a pedestrian crossing with different speeds and displaying a special red/green sign to pedestrians. Here, 51 persons participating in the experiment had to decide when crossing is safe. Results show that the majority of people indicated they would cross in the time windows when it was actually safe to cross. Male subjects made their decision to cross slightly faster but no significant differences were found in the decision making by gender. It was found that age is not an influencing factor, either. Overall, a quick learning process was experienced proving that explicit communication patterns are self-explaining.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶汽车（AV）与行人之间缺乏非正式交互（如手势、眼神、闪光灯）导致的路权沟通障碍。  
**[核心方法]**：在VR环境中构建城市路口场景，让51名受试者面对不同速度接近的虚拟AV，该AV装有红/绿LED信号灯，测试行人自行判断安全过街时机。  
**[创新与效果]**：多数受试者在安全时间窗口内选择过街；性别和年龄无显著影响；显式通信模式（LED灯）具有自解释性，行人快速学会识别。

### 4. [Pedestrians’ understanding of fully autonomous vehicles (FAV) intent to stop](https://doi.org/10.1177/1071181320641465)
`相关度 88` · 行人理解AV停车意图，直接

*未知会议/期刊* | M. Hochman, T. Oron-Gilad | 2020-12-01

**TLDR:** Walkers’ understanding of Fully Autonomous Vehicle intention and what influences their decision to cross are explored, suggesting that pedestrians’ decision making to cross depends on a combination of the e-HMI implementation and the car distance.

> This study explored pedestrians’ understanding of Fully Autonomous Vehicle (FAV) intention and what influences their decision to cross. Twenty participants saw fixed simulated urban road crossing scenes with a FAV present on the road. The scenes differed from one another in the FAV’s messages: the external Human-Machine Interfaces (e-HMI) background color, message type and modality, the FAV’s distance from the crossing place, and its size. Eye-tracking data and objective measurements were collected. Results revealed that pedestrians looked at the e-HMI before making their decision; however, they did not always make the decision according to the e-HMIs’ color, instructions (in advice messages), or intention (in status messages). Moreover, when they acted according to the e-HMI proposition, for certain distance conditions, they tended to hesitate before making the decision. Findings suggest that pedestrians’ decision making to cross depends on a combination of the e-HMI implementation and the car distance. Future work should explore the robustness of the findings in dynamic and more complex crossing environments.

**[试图解决的问题]**: 行人如何理解完全自动驾驶车辆（FAV）的停车意图，以及影响其过街决策的关键因素。  
**[核心方法]**: 通过固定模拟城市道路场景，操控FAV的e-HMI背景颜色、消息类型与模态、车辆距离和尺寸，采集20名参与者的眼动数据与客观测量。  
**[创新与效果]**: 发现行人在决策前会注视e-HMI，但并非总是依据e-HMI的色码、指令或意图做出决策；特定距离下即使遵从e-HMI建议，也会出现犹豫；表明过街决策取决于e-HMI实现与车辆距离的耦合。

### 5. [From Camera-Based Sensing to Reasoning: A Comprehensive Review Toward Proactive Vulnerable Road User Safety](https://doi.org/10.1109/TITS.2026.3707876)
`相关度 85` · VRU安全综述，直接相关

*IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print)* | Shucheng Zhang, Yan Shi, ..., Mehrdad Nasri, Yinhai Wang | 2025-09-30

**TLDR:** A comprehensive review of camera-based methods for proactive VRU safety, with a focus on developments over the past five years, and provides a unified foundation for the development of reliable, scalable, and deployable VRU safety systems.

> Ensuring the safety of vulnerable road users (VRUs), such as pedestrians and cyclists, remains a critical challenge, as conventional infrastructure-based measures are often insufficient in dynamic urban environments. Recent advances in learning-based visual sensing systems have created new opportunities for more adaptive and context-aware VRU protection. This paper presents a comprehensive review of camera-based methods for proactive VRU safety, with a focus on developments over the past five years. Unlike prior surveys that primarily emphasize detection, we organize the literature into three interconnected components: visual perception (detection and classification), motion modeling (tracking and trajectory prediction), and behavior understanding (intent recognition and reasoning). These components form a unified hierarchical pipeline that enables early risk anticipation and timely intervention. Furthermore, this survey systematically incorporates emerging AI paradigms, including Vision Transformers (ViTs), Large Language Models (LLMs), and diffusion models, highlighting their roles in representation learning, uncertainty modeling, and semantic reasoning. Finally, we identify four key challenges specific to VRU safety, including data scarcity, behavioral uncertainty, edge deployment efficiency, and real-world sensing constraints, and discuss corresponding research directions. This work provides a unified foundation for the development of reliable, scalable, and deployable VRU safety systems.

**[试图解决的问题]**：传统基础设施措施在动态城市环境中难以有效保护行人、自行车等弱势道路使用者（VRU），需更自适应、上下文感知的主动安全系统。

**[核心方法]**：构建从视觉感知（检测与分类）、运动建模（跟踪与轨迹预测）到行为理解（意图识别与推理）的三级分层管道，并系统纳入Vision Transformers（ViT）、Large Language Models（LLM）、扩散模型等新兴AI范式用于表示学习、不确定性建模和语义推理。

**[创新与效果]**：首次将VRU安全文献按统一层次管道组织，实现早期风险预测与及时干预；明确数据稀缺、行为不确定性、边缘部署效率、真实传感约束四大挑战，为开发可靠、可扩展、可部署的VRU安全系统提供统一基础。
