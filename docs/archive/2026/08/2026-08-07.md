### 1. [Sparse Instance Conditioned Multimodal Trajectory Prediction](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCV51070.2023.00895)
`相关度 95` · 行人轨迹预测，多模态条件

*IEEE International Conference on Computer Vision* | Yonghao Dong, Le Wang, Sanpin Zhou, Gang Hua | 2023-10-01

**TLDR:** 无

> Pedestrian trajectory prediction is critical in many vision tasks but challenging due to the multimodality of the future trajectory. Most existing methods predict multi-modal trajectories conditioned by goals (future endpoints) or instances (all future points). However, goal-conditioned methods ignore the intermediate process and instance-conditioned methods ignore the stochasticity of pedestrian motions. In this paper, we propose a simple yet effective Sparse Instance Conditioned Network (SICNet), which gives a balanced solution between goal-conditioned and instance-conditioned methods. Specifically, SICNet learns comprehensive sparse instances, i.e., representative points of the future trajectory, through a mask generated by a long short-term memory encoder and uses the memory mechanism to store and retrieve such sparse instances. Hence SICNet can decode the observed trajectory into the future prediction conditioned on the stored sparse instance. Moreover, we design a memory refinement module that refines the retrieved sparse instances from the memory to reduce memory recall errors. Extensive experiments on ETH-UCY and SDD datasets show that our method outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods. In addition, ablation studies demonstrate the superiority of our method compared with goal-conditioned and instance-conditioned approaches.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有行人轨迹预测方法中，目标条件方法忽略轨迹中间过程，实例条件方法忽略运动随机性，无法平衡多模态预测的准确性与随机性。  
**[核心方法]**：提出SICNet，通过LSTM编码器生成掩码学习稀疏实例（未来轨迹的代表点），并利用记忆机制存储和检索稀疏实例，再基于检索到的稀疏实例解码观测轨迹为未来预测；同时设计记忆精炼模块修正检索误差。  
**[创新与效果]**：在ETH-UCY和SDD数据集上超越现有最先进方法，消融实验证明相比纯目标条件或实例条件方法具有更优性能。

### 2. [Multimodal Hybrid Pedestrian: A Hybrid Automaton Model of Urban Pedestrian Behavior for Automated Driving Applications](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2021.3058307)
`相关度 95` · 行人行为混合自动机模型

*IEEE Access* | Suresh Kumaar Jayaraman, L. Robert, X. J. Yang, D. Tilbury | 2021

**TLDR:** A hybrid automaton model of multimodal pedestrian behavior called Multimodal Hybrid Pedestrian (MHP) is developed that is applicable to a wide variety of urban scenarios—midblocks, intersections, one-way, and two-way streets, etc., and the probabilistic predictions from the model can be utilized for AV motion planning.

> For automated vehicles (AVs) to navigate safely, they must be able to anticipate and predict the behavior of pedestrians. This is particularly critical in urban driving environments where risks of collisions are high. However, a major challenge is that pedestrian behavior is inherently multimodal in nature, i.e., pedestrians can plausibly take multiple paths. This is because, in large part, pedestrian behaviors are driven by unique intentions and decisions made by each pedestrian walking along a particular sidewalk or crosswalk. As described in this paper, we developed a hybrid automaton model of multimodal pedestrian behavior called Multimodal Hybrid Pedestrian (MHP). We account for multimodal pedestrian behavior by identifying pedestrian decision-making points and developing decision-making models to predict pedestrian behaviors in a probabilistic hybrid automaton framework. The resulting MHP model is more likely to predict the ground truth trajectory compared to two baseline models—a baseline hybrid automaton model and a constant velocity model. The MHP model is applicable to a wide variety of urban scenarios—midblocks, intersections, one-way, and two-way streets, etc., and the probabilistic predictions from the model can be utilized for AV motion planning.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶汽车在城市环境中难以预测行人行为的多模态性（即行人有多种可能路径），导致碰撞风险高。

**[核心方法]**：提出一种概率混合自动机模型（Multimodal Hybrid Pedestrian, MHP），通过识别行人决策点并建立决策模型，在混合自动机框架内进行概率性行为预测。

**[创新与效果]**：相比基线混合自动机模型和恒定速度模型，MHP模型对真实轨迹的预测概率更高；适用于路段、交叉口、单双向街道等多种城市场景，其概率预测可直接用于自动驾驶运动规划。

### 3. [Pedestrian Trajectory Prediction with Learning-based Approaches: A Comparative Study](https://doi.org/10.1109/IVS.2019.8814183)
`相关度 95` · 行人轨迹预测比较研究

*2019 IEEE Intelligent Vehicles Symposium (IV)* | Yang Li, Long Xin, ..., Jianqiang Wang, S. Li | 2019-06-01

**TLDR:** 无

> To enable safe and efficient navigations through the urban environment, autonomous vehicles need to anticipate the future motions of the walking pedestrians who might collide with them. The dynamic and stochastic behavior characteristics of the pedestrians make the trajectory prediction challengeable for most kinematics-based approaches. This paper presents a comparative study of six state-of-the-art learning-based methods for pedestrian trajectory prediction, including Gaussian Process (GP), LSTM, GP-LSTM, Character-based LSTM, Sequence-to-Sequence (Seq2Seq), and attention-based Seq2Seq. The trajectory prediction is formulated as the regression task or sequence generation problem that predicts future trajectories based on observed trajectories. We evaluate the performance of the learning-based methods on a public real-world pedestrian dataset. To address the concern of data scarcity, we employ three forms of data augmentation (i.e., translation, rotation, and stretch) to enlarge the dataset, which produce the transformed trajectories from the original trajectories. By comparison, those learning-based approaches are ranked based on prediction accuracy from high to low as Seq2Seq, attention-based Seq2Seq, C-LSTM, LSTM, GP, and GP-LSTM. Particularly, Seq2Seq model outperforms all baseline approaches, with the mean and final point errors less than 15cm in normal scenarios when predicting 1s ahead.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶中，行人运动的动态与随机性使得基于运动学的方法难以准确预测其未来轨迹。  
**[核心方法]**：比较六种学习型方法（高斯过程GP、LSTM、GP-LSTM、基于字符的LSTM、序列到序列Seq2Seq、注意力Seq2Seq），将轨迹预测视为回归或序列生成任务，并采用平移、旋转、拉伸三种数据增强扩充数据集。  
**[创新与效果]**：Seq2Seq在公开行人数据集上预测1秒时均值和终点误差低于15cm，精度排名最高（Seq2Seq > 注意力Seq2Seq > C-LSTM > LSTM > GP > GP-LSTM）。

### 4. [Understanding Pedestrian-Vehicle Interactions with Vehicle Mounted Vision: An LSTM Model and Empirical Analysis](https://doi.org/10.1109/IVS.2019.8813798)
`相关度 95` · 行人-车辆交互LSTM模型

*2019 IEEE Intelligent Vehicles Symposium (IV)* | Daniela A. Ridel, Nachiket Deo, D. Wolf, M. Trivedi | 2019-05-14

**TLDR:** A data-driven approach to implicitly model pedestrians' interactions with vehicles, to better predict pedestrian behavior is presented and a Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) model is proposed that takes as input the past trajectories of the pedestrian and ego-vehicle, and pedestrian head orientation, and predicts the future positions of the pedestrians.

> Pedestrians and vehicles often share the road in complex inner city traffic. This leads to interactions between the vehicle and pedestrians, with each affecting the other's motion. In order to create robust methods to reason about pedestrian behavior and to design interfaces of communication between self-driving cars and pedestrians we need to better understand such interactions. In this paper, we present a data-driven approach to implicitly model pedestrians' interactions with vehicles, to better predict pedestrian behavior. We propose a Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) model that takes as input the past trajectories of the pedestrian and ego-vehicle, and pedestrian head orientation, and predicts the future positions of the pedestrian. Our experiments based on a real-world, inner city dataset captured with vehicle mounted cameras, show that the usage of such cues improve pedestrian prediction when compared to a baseline that purely uses the past trajectory of the pedestrian.

**[试图解决的问题]**：城市复杂交通中行人与车辆相互影响，需要更精确地预测行人未来位置以支持自动驾驶决策与人机交互设计。

**[核心方法]**：提出一种基于LSTM的隐式交互建模方法，输入行人过去轨迹、自车过去轨迹及行人头部朝向，输出行人未来位置。

**[创新与效果]**：基于车载相机采集的真实城市数据集，相比仅使用行人历史轨迹的基线模型，引入交互线索（自车轨迹、头部朝向）显著提升了行人位置预测精度。

### 5. [TPNet: Trajectory Proposal Network for Motion Prediction](https://doi.org/10.1109/cvpr42600.2020.00683)
`相关度 85` · 通用运动预测，含行人

*Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition* | Liangji Fang, Qinhong Jiang, Jianping Shi, Bolei Zhou | 2020-04-26

**TLDR:** This work proposes a novel two-stage motion prediction framework, Trajectory Proposal Network (TPNet), which first generates a candidate set of future trajectories as hypothesis proposals, then makes the final predictions by classifying and refining the proposals which meets the physical constraints.

> Making accurate motion prediction of the surrounding traffic agents such as pedestrians, vehicles, and cyclists is crucial for autonomous driving. Recent data-driven motion prediction methods have attempted to learn to directly regress the exact future position or its distribution from massive amount of trajectory data. However, it remains difficult for these methods to provide multimodal predictions as well as integrate physical constraints such as traffic rules and movable areas. In this work we propose a novel two-stage motion prediction framework, Trajectory Proposal Network (TPNet). TPNet first generates a candidate set of future trajectories as hypothesis proposals, then makes the final predictions by classifying and refining the proposals which meets the physical constraints. By steering the proposal generation process, safe and multimodal predictions are realized. Thus this framework effectively mitigates the complexity of motion prediction problem while ensuring the multimodal output. Experiments on four large-scale trajectory prediction datasets, i.e. the ETH, UCY, Apollo and Argoverse datasets, show that TPNet achieves the state-of-the-art results both quantitatively and qualitatively.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有数据驱动的运动预测方法难以提供多模态预测并整合物理约束（如交通规则和可行驶区域）。

**[核心方法]**：提出两阶段框架TPNet，首先生成候选未来轨迹作为假设提议，再通过分类和细化符合物理约束的提议做出最终预测。

**[创新与效果]**：通过引导提议生成过程实现安全且多模态的预测，降低了问题复杂度；在ETH、UCY、Apollo和Argoverse四个数据集上取得定量和定性SOTA结果。
