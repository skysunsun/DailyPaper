### 1. [Learning From Past and Future: A Unified Instantaneous Pedestrian Intent Prediction Framework Based on Privileged Knowledge Distillation for Autonomous Driving](https://doi.org/10.1109/TASE.2026.3713196)
`相关度 95` · 直接研究行人过街意图预测

*IEEE Transactions on Automation Science and Engineering* | Xiaobo Chen, Wei Xu, Jianjun Qian | 2026

**TLDR:** 无

> Accurately predicting pedestrian crossing intent is paramount for the safety and reliability of autonomous driving systems in complex urban environments. Despite tremendous progress in the past decade, existing methods often fail to make reliable predictions from instantaneous observations. To address this limitation, we propose a novel privileged knowledge distillation (PKD) framework that can be seamlessly integrated into existing prediction models to improve their performance under instantaneous observation scenarios. Specifically, we propose a unique training paradigm where a privileged branch (teacher) learns temporal trend features from bidirectional privileged sequences, encompassing both extended historical observation sequences and future behavioral sequences in the time-to-event (TTE) period. This bidirectional trend knowledge is then distilled into the instantaneous branch (student) via a meticulously designed composite loss, enabling the student to effectively simulate and infer complex pedestrian intent from only a few frames of input at test time. The framework exploits specialized components, including a Gated Multi-scale Trend Module (GMTM) and a bidirectional trend simulation module (BTSM), to capture and mimic privileged knowledge explicitly. Extensive experiments on the public PIE and JAAD datasets demonstrate that our PKD framework significantly enhances the performance of multiple state-of-the-art baseline models, confirming its effectiveness and broad applicability. Note to Practitioners—In real-world autonomous driving, vehicles often need to predict a pedestrian’s intent to cross the road using only a few visual frames, especially in sudden or complex traffic situations. However, short and incomplete observations frequently lead to unreliable intent predictions. This study aims to improve the reliability of such instantaneous pedestrian intent prediction when limited sensory data are available. We propose a Privileged Knowledge Distillation (PKD) framework that is flexible and can be plugged into existing prediction systems without additional sensing hardware. During training, the privileged branch learns from both extended past and future sequences to form a deeper understanding of how pedestrians’ intent evolves over time. The knowledge of the privileged branch is distilled into an instantaneous branch that only relies on short observations. At runtime, the instantaneous branch can reason about pedestrians’ intent using only brief observations. In practice, this approach can enhance the safety and responsiveness of autonomous vehicles by allowing them to make early and reliable decisions even with minimal visual input. Therefore, our approach has broad application prospects in autonomous driving and automatic robotics.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有行人过街意图预测方法在仅依赖瞬时短时观测（少量视觉帧）时预测结果不可靠，难以应对突发或复杂交通场景。  
**[核心方法]**：提出特权知识蒸馏（PKD）框架，包含教师（特权分支）从双向特权序列（扩展历史观测序列+未来行为序列）学习时间趋势特征，通过复合损失蒸馏到学生（瞬时分支）；并设计门控多尺度趋势模块（GMTM）和双向趋势模拟模块（BTSM）显式捕获与模拟特权知识。  
**[创新与效果]**：在PIE和JAAD数据集上，PKD框架显著提升多个SOTA基线模型的预测性能，使瞬时分支仅用少量输入帧即可可靠推断行人意图，且无需额外传感硬件，具备广泛适用性。

### 2. [Vision-based recognition of pedestrian crossing intention in an urban environment](https://doi.org/10.1109/CYBER46603.2019.9066706)
`相关度 95` · 直接行人过街意图识别

*Cyber ..* | Biao Yang, Rongrong Ni | 2019-07-01

**TLDR:** 无

> Autonomous moving platforms (like self-driving cars and mobile robots) need to understand the behaviors of pedestrians for better performance. Recognizing pedestrians' crossing intention is one of the most critical capabilities of self-driving cars to guarantee safe operations in an urban environment. Some researchers utilize pedestrians' trajectories to identify their crossing intention, whereas ignoring the informative appearance. This work studies two vision cues which reveal pedestrians' crossing intention. A looking/not looking classifier is built to capture eye contacts between pedestrians and self-driving cars. Such eye contacts are considered to be highly related to pedestrians' crossing intention. A crossing/not crossing classifier is designed to catch early actions before pedestrians starting to cross, like limb movements. Data augmentation is used before training both classifiers due to limited training samples. Meanwhile, both classifiers are optimized with focal loss, which can relieve the issue of easy/hard samples. Experimental evaluations verify the effectiveness of the proposed vision cues in recognizing pedestrians' crossing intention. Comparisons with the baseline indicate the superiorities of the proposed strategies in training the two classifiers.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有行人过街意图识别方法仅依赖轨迹，忽略了行人外观（如眼神接触、肢体动作）所蕴含的信息，导致识别准确率受限。

**[核心方法]**：构建两个视觉线索分类器——① 眼神接触检测（looking/not looking）分类器，捕捉行人与自车之间的目光接触；② 过街动作检测（crossing/not crossing）分类器，捕捉行人起始过街前的早期肢体动作（如手臂摆动）。训练中对有限样本进行数据增强，并使用Focal Loss优化以缓解难易样本不平衡问题。

**[创新与效果]**：首次将眼神接触与早期肢体动作作为视觉线索用于行人过街意图识别，实验验证了该方法的有效性；与基线模型相比，两个分类器在识别准确率上均表现出显著优势。

### 3. [Pedestrians’ intent to cross in a fully autonomous vehicle environment; looking at crossing opportunity, eHMI message and wait time](https://doi.org/10.1080/15389588.2024.2372801)
`相关度 90` · 研究行人过街意图与eHMI

*Traffic Injury Prevention* | Michael E. Hochman, Y. Parmet, T. Oron-Gilad | 2024-11-01

**TLDR:** It is observed that pedestrians may be more likely to follow the eHMI’s suggestion to cross than their self-skill, especially under time pressure, and indices and metrics reflect the intricate interaction aspects of pedestrian behavior with eHMIs.

> Abstract Objectives Currently, pedestrians’ road-crossing decisions depend on the traffic at the crossing point, crossing opportunities, and circumstantial elements. Longer wait times on the curb and time pressure raise the number of traffic violations among pedestrians. The era of fully autonomous vehicles (FAVs) promises new interactions. External Human-Machine Interfaces (eHMIs) aim to increase the understanding of FAVs’ intentions and will influence pedestrians’ intent to cross. Yet, how pedestrian behaviors will change in the FAV era remains unclear. Further, there are no good metrics for evaluating the intricate interaction of crossing-related factors. Methods In a laboratory study, sixty participants observed crossing scenarios of a typical one-lane road with 10–12 FAVs driving along, two possible eHMI messages (“Cross!”/“Stop!”), and varying crossing opportunities (safe, risky, and unsafe). Participants had to indicate when they intended to cross the road using a designated button. Half of the participants were induced to feel time pressure. We developed index scores to convey the complexity of such crossing situations from the perspective of a pedestrian and used them to analyze the experimental data. Results In total, 48% of participants’ indications were to cross in safe crossing opportunities, 34% risky, and 18% unsafe; 69% were made when the eHMI proposition was “Cross!” and 31% for “Stop!”. High, significant, and negative correlations were found for compatible responses, e.g., with a safe crossing gap and an eHMI sign to “Cross!”. The opposite was true for incompatibility, e.g., a safe crossing gap and an eHMI sign to “Stop!” or an unsafe crossing gap and an eHMI sign to “Cross!”. The time pressure group’s median times to cross were shorter, making riskier decisions. Conclusion Our findings reflect a tendency to comply with the eHMI. In more complex scenarios where the crossing opportunity was uncertain or waiting times were longer, we observed that pedestrians were more inclined to follow the eHMI’s guidance to cross. These findings suggest that pedestrians may be more likely to follow the eHMI’s suggestion to cross than their self-skill, especially under time pressure. Our indices and metrics reflect the intricate interaction aspects of pedestrian behavior with eHMIs.

**[试图解决的问题]**：在全自动驾驶车辆（FAV）环境中，eHMI信息与时间压力如何影响行人的过街决策，以及现有缺乏评估过街相关因素复杂交互的指标。
**[核心方法]**：实验室研究，60名参与者观看典型单车道场景（10-12辆FAV），设置两种eHMI信息（“Cross!”/“Stop!”）与三种过街机会（安全/风险/不安全），通过按键记录过街意图，并对一半参与者施加时间压力；开发指数分数量化过街情境的复杂性。
**[创新与效果]**：发现69%的过街意图发生在eHMI提示“Cross!”时，安全机会下过街比例为48%，时间压力组决策更快且更冒险；行人倾向于遵循eHMI建议而非自身判断，尤其在过街机会不确定或等待时间较长时；提出的指数和指标有效反映了行人行为与eHMI的复杂交互。

### 4. [Should automated vehicles communicate their state or intent? Effects of eHMI activations and non-activations on pedestrians’ trust formation and crossing behavior](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11042-024-20497-0)
`相关度 85` · 行人过街行为与eHMI交互

*Multimedia tools and applications* | Daniel Eisele, J. Kraus, Magdalena Maria Schlemer, Tibor Petzoldt | 2024-12-18

**TLDR:** The results show that the activation of both the state and intent eHMI was effective in communicating the AV’s intent to yield and both eHMIs drew attention to a failure to yield when they did not activate, however, the two eHMIs differed in their potential to mislead pedestrians.

> In recent years, there has been a debate on whether automated vehicles (AVs) should be equipped with novel external human–machine interfaces (eHMIs). Many studies have demonstrated how eHMIs influence pedestrians’ attitudes (e.g., trust in AVs) and behavior when they activate (e.g., encourage crossing by lighting up). However, very little attention has been paid to their effects when they do not activate (e.g., discourage crossing by not lighting up). We conducted a video-based laboratory study with a mixed design to explore the potential of two different eHMI messages to facilitate pedestrian-AV interactions by means of activating or not activating. Our participants watched videos of an approaching AV equipped with either a state eHMI (“I am braking”) or intent eHMI (“I intend to yield to you”) from the perspective of a pedestrian about to cross the road. They indicated when they would initiate crossing and repeatedly rated their trust in the AV. Our results show that the activation of both the state and intent eHMI was effective in communicating the AV’s intent to yield and both eHMIs drew attention to a failure to yield when they did not activate. However, the two eHMIs differed in their potential to mislead pedestrians, as decelerations accompanied by the activation of the state eHMI were repeatedly misinterpreted as an intention to yield. Despite this, user experience ratings did not differ between the eHMIs. Following a failure to yield, trust declined sharply. In subsequent trials, crossing behavior recovered quickly, while trust took longer to recover.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶车辆的外部人机界面（eHMI）的激活与未激活对行人信任和过马路行为的影响，尤其关注两种eHMI类型（状态 vs. 意图）在误导行人方面的差异。  
**[核心方法]**：基于视频的实验室混合设计实验，被试从行人视角观看配备状态eHMI（“我正在刹车”）或意图eHMI（“我打算让行”）的接近车辆，记录其开始过马路的时间并重复评估信任度。  
**[创新与效果]**：两种eHMI激活时均有效传达让行意图，未激活时均能提示让行失败；但状态eHMI的减速伴随激活被反复误认为让行意图，而意图eHMI误导性更小。让行失败后信任急剧下降，但过马路行为恢复快于信任恢复。

### 5. [The effect of cognitive load on the driver's recognition of
 pedestrian road-crossing intention](https://doi.org/10.1037/tms0000132)
`相关度 80` · 驾驶员识别行人过街意图

*TMS Proceedings 2021* | Wenjia Guo, Shiqi Wang, Jun Yin | 2021

**TLDR:** As the cognitive load increased, the accuracy of identifying pedestrian intentions decreased; the shorter the TTA, the faster the driver correctly identified pedestrian intentions, and the more easily the driver is able to recognize their intentions.

> Correctly recognizing pedestrian intentions is very important to ensure the safety of drivers and pedestrians. In the driving process, divers may need to complete other tasks that are not related to driving (e.g., remembering phone numbers), resulting in varying degrees of cognitive load. Therefore, this study explored whether cognitive load affects the driver’s recognition of pedestrian intentions, and meanwhile considered whether the Time to Arrival (TTA; the time when the car reaches the position of the pedestrian at the current driving speed) has an effect in this influential relation. Based on the driving simulation platform (including Logitech G29, Carla), the cognitive load was manipulated by the auditory n-back task, and the recognition of pedestrian intention was measured by the situation present awareness method (SPAM), so as to explore the influence of cognitive load and TTA on the driver's recognition of pedestrian intention. The results found that: (1) As the cognitive load increased, the accuracy of identifying pedestrian intentions decreased; (2) The shorter the TTA, the faster the driver correctly identified pedestrian intentions. Accordingly, in order to improve driving safety, the drivers should keep their cognitive load as low as possible during driving, and for pedestrians, the earlier they express their intention to cross the road, the more easily the driver is able to recognize their intentions.

**[试图解决的问题]**：驾驶过程中非驾驶任务（如记忆电话号码）引起的认知负荷如何影响驾驶员识别行人过马路意图的准确性，以及车辆到达时间（TTA）在此关系中的作用。

**[核心方法]**：基于驾驶模拟平台（Logitech G29 + Carla），通过听觉n-back任务操纵认知负荷，采用情境呈现意识法（SPAM）测量驾驶员对行人意图的识别。

**[创新与效果]**：发现认知负荷增加降低行人意图识别准确率；TTA越短，驾驶员正确识别行人意图的速度越快。据此提出驾驶时应保持低认知负荷、行人应尽早表达过马路意图以提升安全性。
