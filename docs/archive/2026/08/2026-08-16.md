### 1. [SDATNet: Social Diverse Adaptive TrajectorNet for Pedestrian Trajectory Prediction Under Long-Tail Behavior Distributions](https://doi.org/10.1109/TITS.2026.3676929)
`相关度 95` · 直接研究行人轨迹预测，长尾行为

*IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print)* | Zihan Jiang, Ruonan Liu, ..., Yafeng Guo, Weidong Zhang | 2026-08-01

**TLDR:** 无

> Pedestrian trajectory prediction is vital for safety-critical applications such as autonomous driving and video surveillance. While recent advances in deep learning have significantly improved predictive accuracy, most models struggle with underrepresented and diverse behaviors present in the long-tail distribution of pedestrian trajectories. To address this challenge, we propose Social Diverse Adaptive TrajectorNet (SDATNet), a novel deep learning framework designed to enhance prediction performance on rare and atypical trajectory patterns. SDATNet introduces three key innovations: 1) the ResGhost module, which incorporates residual connections and ghost operations to enrich multi-scale social feature diversity; 2) a Dynamic Weights Variety Loss (DWVL) that adaptively emphasizes rare behaviors by weighting loss based on prediction uncertainty; and 3) an Adaptive Gaussian Distribution module that stabilizes the modeling of outlier trajectories by aligning prediction distributions with behavioral variance. The results demonstrate that the SDATNet outperforms existing methods in predicting pedestrian trajectories, offering a reliable and versatile solution for real-world applications.

**[试图解决的问题]**：行人轨迹预测中，长尾分布下的罕见、非典型轨迹模式预测性能差。  
**[核心方法]**：提出SDATNet框架，包含三部分：1) ResGhost模块（残差连接+幻象操作，增强多尺度社交特征多样性）；2) 动态权重多样性损失DWVL（基于预测不确定性自适应加权，强调稀有行为）；3) 自适应高斯分布模块（对齐预测分布与行为方差，稳定异常轨迹建模）。  
**[创新与效果]**：在预测罕见和异常轨迹上优于现有方法，提升了长尾行为分布下的预测可靠性。

`相关度 95` · 直接研究行人过街意图识别

*2026 7th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Electromechanical Automation (AIEA)* | Yuli Lu | 2026-06-01

**TLDR:** 无

> Pedestrian crossing intention recognition is critical for autonomous driving safety. Existing methods often rely on a single feature extractor and therefore capture either local spatial patterns or global temporal context insufficiently. To address this limitation, this paper proposes a dual-stream framework that combines ResNet local features with ViT global representations through bidirectional cross-attention. The fusion module enables mutual enhancement between fine-grained pedestrian cues and scene-level context, while temporal attention aggregates frame-level evidence for final classification. Experiments on JAAD demonstrate competitive performance and real-time deployment potential. The revision further adds a PIE/TITAN validation protocol, complexity and inference-speed discussion, backbone rationale, and failure-case analysis, indicating that the method is suitable for accelerator-based real-time perception while lightweight variants remain necessary for stricter resource budgets.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有行人过街意图识别方法依赖单一特征提取器，无法同时充分捕捉局部空间模式与全局时间上下文。

**[核心方法]**：提出双流框架，通过双向交叉注意力机制融合ResNet提取的局部特征与ViT提取的全局表示，并利用时间注意力聚合帧级证据进行最终分类。

**[创新与效果]**：在JAAD上取得竞争性性能并具备实时部署潜力；在PIE/TITAN上验证了方法有效性，讨论了复杂度与推理速度，表明该方法适用于基于加速器的实时感知，但严格资源预算下仍需轻量变体。

### 3. [A Hierarchical Forecasting Model of Pedestrian Crossing Behavior for Autonomous Vehicle](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2024.3352499)
`相关度 95` · 直接行人过街行为预测模型

*IEEE Access* | Guolin Yang, Erwin Jose López Pulgarín, Guido Herrmann | 2024

**TLDR:** The resulting HFPM model can produce more accurate simulation results than previously developed policy-based models, as demonstrated through qualitative and quantitative comparisons with a baseline pedestrian model obtained from the CITR data set.

> Simulation of pedestrians in shared spaces poses a significant challenge in autonomous driving virtual testing. The simulation pedestrian model can respond to autonomous vehicle behaviour changes. We present HFPM: a Hierarchical Forecasting Pedestrian Model to imitate pedestrian behaviour. The model has three layers: the dynamics model layer, the path planning layer, and the decision layer. In the dynamics model layer, an improved force model with the heading direction of the pedestrian is developed based on the Social Force Model, which can model pedestrian-pedestrian interaction. In the path planning layer, an Artificial Potential Field model is modified to plan a feasible path to the individual goals. The planning layer has a prediction module to predict the trajectory of vehicles on the road in order to choose the best route with no collision. The decision layer is a finite state machine with five states: the pedestrian can approach, walk, wait, run and reach the goal. The resulting HFPM model can produce more accurate simulation results than previously developed policy-based models, as demonstrated through qualitative and quantitative comparisons with a baseline pedestrian model obtained from the CITR data set.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶虚拟测试中，缺乏能准确模拟行人响应车辆行为变化的仿真模型，已有基于策略的模型精度不足。

**[核心方法]**：提出分层预测行人模型（HFPM），包含三层：动力学模型层（基于社会力模型改进，引入行人航向方向以建模行人-行人交互）、路径规划层（改进人工势场法，集成车辆轨迹预测模块以规划无碰撞最优路径）、决策层（有限状态机，含接近、行走、等待、奔跑、到达目标五个状态）。

**[创新与效果]**：与CITR数据集基线模型相比，HFPM在定性和定量对比中均生成更准确的行人仿真结果，优于此前基于策略的模型。

### 4. [Predicting Pedestrian Crossing Behavior in Germany and Japan: Insights Into Model Transferability](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIV.2024.3506727)
`相关度 95` · 跨国行人过街行为预测可迁移

*IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Vehicles* | Chi Zhang, Janis Sprenger, Zhongjun Ni, Christian Berger | 2024-12-04

**TLDR:** A transferable model is developed using an unsupervised clustering method, which improves prediction accuracy for gap selection and trajectory prediction, and improves prediction accuracy for gap selection and trajectory prediction.

> Predicting pedestrian crossing behavior is important for intelligent traffic systems to avoid pedestrian-vehicle collisions. Most existing pedestrian crossing behavior models are trained and evaluated on datasets collected from a single country, overlooking differences between countries. To address this gap, we compared pedestrian road-crossing behavior at unsignalized crossings in Germany and Japan. We presented four types of machine learning models to predict gap selection behavior, zebra crossing usage, and their trajectories using simulator data collected from both countries. When comparing the differences between countries, pedestrians from the study conducted in Japan are more cautious, selecting larger gaps compared to those in Germany. We evaluate and analyze model transferability. Our results show that neural networks outperform other machine learning models in predicting gap selection and zebra crossing usage, while random forest models perform best on trajectory prediction tasks, demonstrating strong performance and transferability. We develop a transferable model using an unsupervised clustering method, which improves prediction accuracy for gap selection and trajectory prediction. These findings provide a deeper understanding of pedestrian crossing behaviors in different countries and offer valuable insights into model transferability.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有行人过街行为模型多基于单一国家数据训练，忽略了国家间差异（如德国与日本行人行为差异），导致模型跨地域迁移性不足。

**[核心方法]**：使用四种机器学习模型（神经网络、随机森林等）预测间隙选择、斑马线使用及轨迹；并开发基于无监督聚类方法的可迁移模型。

**[创新与效果]**：神经网络在间隙选择和斑马线使用预测上最优，随机森林在轨迹预测上最优；无监督聚类方法使间隙选择与轨迹预测准确率提升，并揭示了模型跨国家迁移的规律。

### 5. [Causal Discovery and Deep Learning-based Interaction-aware Pedestrian Trajectory Prediction](https://doi.org/10.5194/isprs-annals-xi-4-2026-275-2026)
`相关度 90` · 因果发现与交互感知轨迹预测

*ISPRS Annals of the Photogrammetry, Remote Sensing and Spatial Information Sciences* | Wen-Xin Qiu, T. Fuse | 2026-07-10

**TLDR:** The experimental results demonstrate that the proposed pedestrian trajectory modeling approach achieves a good balance between prediction accuracy and interpretability, while also identifying limitations, including at low-density scenes and due to causal model assumptions.

> Abstract. Understanding pedestrian behaviors is the foundation of simulation for space planning. However, conventional behavior modeling methods are insufficient for learning detailed interactions, and deep learning methods often lack interpretability. This study aims to develop a pedestrian trajectory modeling approach based on discovering causal relationships among pedestrians. The proposed method consists of two parts: analyzing causal relationships among pedestrians using statistical causal discovery methods and predicting trajectories using attention-based deep learning methods. The first part employs a semi-parametric method to identify the causal relationships underlying observed pedestrian behavior and construct a spatial-temporal graph based on these causal relationships. The second part primarily uses the graph attention network to learn interactions among pedestrians. The experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method achieves a good balance between prediction accuracy and interpretability, while also identifying limitations, including at low-density scenes and due to causal model assumptions.

**[试图解决的问题]**：传统行为建模无法学习详细交互，而深度学习方法缺乏可解释性，导致行人轨迹预测在准确性与可解释性之间难以平衡。  
**[核心方法]**：采用半参数统计因果发现方法识别行人间的因果关系，构建时空图；然后使用图注意力网络（GAT）学习交互并预测轨迹。  
**[创新与效果]**：在预测准确性与可解释性之间取得良好平衡，但指出在低密度场景及因果模型假设下存在局限性。
