### 1. [Spatio-temporal DenseNet for real-time intent prediction of pedestrians in urban traffic environments](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neucom.2019.12.091)
`相关度 95` · 实时行人意图预测，直接相关

*Neurocomputing* | Khaled Saleh, M. Hossny, S. Nahavandi | 2020-04-01

**TLDR:** This paper proposes a real-time framework that can accurately detect, track and predict the intended actions of pedestrians based on a tracking-by-detection technique in conjunction with a novel spatio-temporal DenseNet model and has shown resilient and competitive results in comparison to other baseline approaches.

> Abstract Autonomous ground vehicles are increasingly finding their way into real-life applications, ranging from food/parcel delivery to self-driving vehicles. Given that, understanding the behaviours and intentions of humans are still one of the main challenges autonomous ground vehicles faced with. More specifically, when it comes to complex environments such as urban traffic scenes, inferring the intentions and actions of vulnerable road users such as pedestrians become even harder. In this paper, we address the problem of intent action prediction of pedestrians in urban traffic environments using only image sequences from a monocular RGB camera. We propose a real-time framework that can accurately detect, track and predict the intended actions of pedestrians based on a tracking-by-detection technique in conjunction with a novel spatio-temporal DenseNet model. We trained and evaluated our framework based on real data collected from urban traffic environments. Our framework has shown resilient and competitive results in comparison to other baseline approaches. Overall, we achieved an average precision score of 84.76% with a real-time performance at 20 FPS.

**[试图解决的问题]**：城市交通场景中，自动驾驶车辆仅凭单目RGB图像序列难以实时推断行人的意图动作。
**[核心方法]**：基于跟踪-检测（tracking-by-detection）技术，结合新颖的时空密集连接网络（Spatio-temporal DenseNet）模型，从单目RGB图像序列中实现行人检测、跟踪与意图预测。
**[创新与效果]**：在真实城市交通数据上，该框架以20 FPS实时运行，平均精度达84.76%，相比其他基线方法表现出鲁棒性。

### 3. [How should external human-machine interfaces behave? Examining the effects of colour, position, message, activation distance, vehicle yielding, and visual distraction among 1,434 participants.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apergo.2021.103450)
`相关度 90` · 直接研究eHMI对行人过街影响

*Applied Ergonomics* | P. Bazilinskyy, L. Kooijman, Dimitra Dodou, J. D. de Winter | 2021-05-07

**TLDR:** People adopt an egocentric perspective, the windshield is an ineffective position, the often-recommended colour cyan may have to be avoided, and that eHMI activation distance has intricate effects related to onset saliency.

> External human-machine interfaces (eHMIs) may be useful for communicating the intention of an automated vehicle (AV) to a pedestrian, but it is unclear which eHMI design is most effective. In a crowdsourced experiment, we examined the effects of (1) colour (red, green, cyan), (2) position (roof, bumper, windshield), (3) message (WALK, DON'T WALK, WILL STOP, WON'T STOP, light bar), (4) activation distance (35 or 50 m from the pedestrian), and (5) the presence of visual distraction in the environment, on pedestrians' perceived safety of crossing the road in front of yielding and non-yielding AVs. Participants (N = 1434) had to press a key when they felt safe to cross while watching a random 40 out of 276 videos of an approaching AV with eHMI. Results showed that (1) green and cyan eHMIs led to higher perceived safety of crossing than red eHMIs; no significant difference was found between green and cyan, (2) eHMIs on the bumper and roof were more effective than eHMIs on the windshield, (3) for yielding AVs, perceived safety was higher for WALK compared to WILL STOP, followed by the light bar; for non-yielding AVs, a red bar yielded similar results to red text, (4) for yielding AVs, a red bar caused lower perceived safety when activated early compared to late, whereas green/cyan WALK led to higher perceived safety when activated late compared to early, and (5) distraction had no significant effect. We conclude that people adopt an egocentric perspective, that the windshield is an ineffective position, that the often-recommended colour cyan may have to be avoided, and that eHMI activation distance has intricate effects related to onset saliency.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶车辆的外部人机界面（eHMI）如何设计才能有效传达意图给行人，现有设计缺乏系统比较。

**[核心方法]**：众包实验（N=1434），操纵5个自变量：颜色（红/绿/青）、位置（车顶/保险杠/挡风玻璃）、消息（WALK/DON'T WALK/WILL STOP/WON'T STOP/光条）、激活距离（35m/50m）、视觉干扰（有/无），测量行人对横穿安全性的感知。

**[创新与效果]**：发现绿色和青色比红色更安全，但青色可能需避免（因与绿色无显著差异且存在混淆风险）；挡风玻璃位置无效，保险杠和车顶更有效；激活距离产生复杂效应（早期激活的红色光条降低安全性，晚期激活的绿色WALK提高安全性）；视觉干扰无显著影响。

### 4. [EigenTrajectory: Low-Rank Descriptors for Multi-Modal Trajectory Forecasting](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCV51070.2023.00919)
`相关度 85` · 行人轨迹多模态预测

*IEEE International Conference on Computer Vision* | Inhwan Bae, Jean Oh, Hae-Gon Jeon | 2023-07-18

**TLDR:** EigenTrajectory is presented, a trajectory prediction approach that uses a novel trajectory descriptor to form a compact space, known here as EigenTrajectory space, in place of Euclidean space, for representing pedestrian movements and proposes a trajectory anchor-based refinement method to cover all possible futures in the proposed space.

> Capturing high-dimensional social interactions and feasible futures is essential for predicting trajectories. To address this complex nature, several attempts have been devoted to reducing the dimensionality of the output variables via parametric curve fitting such as the Bézier curve and B-spline function. However, these functions, which originate in computer graphics fields, are not suitable to account for socially acceptable human dynamics. In this paper, we present EigenTrajectory ($\mathbb{E}\mathbb{T}$), a trajectory prediction approach that uses a novel trajectory descriptor to form a compact space, known here as $\mathbb{E}\mathbb{T}$ space, in place of Euclidean space, for representing pedestrian movements. We first reduce the complexity of the trajectory descriptor via a low-rank approximation. We transform the pedestrians' history paths into our $\mathbb{E}\mathbb{T}$ space represented by spatio-temporal principle components, and feed them into off-the-shelf trajectory forecasting models. The inputs and outputs of the models as well as social interactions are all gathered and aggregated in the corresponding $\mathbb{E}\mathbb{T}$ space. Lastly, we propose a trajectory anchor-based refinement method to cover all possible futures in the proposed $\mathbb{E}\mathbb{T}$ space. Extensive experiments demonstrate that our EigenTrajectory predictor can significantly improve both the prediction accuracy and reliability of existing trajectory forecasting models on public benchmarks, indicating that the proposed descriptor is suited to represent pedestrian behaviors. Code is publicly available at https://github.com/inhwanbae/EigenTrajectory.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有参数曲线拟合（如贝塞尔曲线、B样条函数）降维方法源自计算机图形学，不适用于建模社会可接受的人类动力学，导致多模态轨迹预测精度和可靠性不足。  
**[核心方法]**：提出EigenTrajectory（ET）描述符，通过低秩近似将行人历史路径映射到由时空主成分构成的ET空间；在该空间内使用现成轨迹预测模型进行输入输出及社交交互的聚合，并设计基于轨迹锚点的细化方法覆盖所有可能未来。  
**[创新与效果]**：在公共基准上显著提升现有轨迹预测模型的预测准确性和可靠性，证明ET描述符适合表征行人行为。

### 5. [Peeking Into the Future: Predicting Future Person Activities and Locations in Videos](https://doi.org/10.1109/CVPR.2019.00587)
`相关度 85` · 行人未来轨迹和活动预测

*Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition* | Junwei Liang, Lu Jiang, ..., A. Hauptmann, Li Fei-Fei | 2019-02-11

**TLDR:** An end-to-end, multi-task learning system utilizing rich visual features about human behavioral information and interaction with their surroundings is proposed, providing the first empirical evidence that joint modeling of paths and activities benefits future path prediction.

> Deciphering human behaviors to predict their future paths/trajectories and what they would do from videos is important in many applications. Motivated by this idea, this paper studies predicting a pedestrian's future path jointly with future activities. We propose an end-to-end, multi-task learning system utilizing rich visual features about human behavioral information and interaction with their surroundings. To facilitate the training, the network is learned with an auxiliary task of predicting future location in which the activity will happen. Experimental results demonstrate our state-of-the-art performance over two public benchmarks on future trajectory prediction. Moreover, our method is able to produce meaningful future activity prediction in addition to the path. The result provides the first empirical evidence that joint modeling of paths and activities benefits future path prediction.

**[试图解决的问题]**：从视频中联合预测行人未来路径（轨迹）和未来活动，缺乏联合建模的实证研究。

**[核心方法]**：端到端多任务学习系统，利用关于人类行为信息和与周围环境交互的丰富视觉特征，并引入辅助任务——预测未来活动发生的位置。

**[创新与效果]**：首次实证证明路径与活动的联合建模有益于未来路径预测，在两个公开基准上达到未来轨迹预测的当前最优性能，同时能输出有意义的未来活动预测。
