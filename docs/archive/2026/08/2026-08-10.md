### 1. [Real-time Intent Prediction of Pedestrians for Autonomous Ground Vehicles via Spatio-Temporal DenseNet](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICRA.2019.8793991)
`相关度 95` · 实时行人意图预测

*IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation* | Khaled Saleh, M. Hossny, S. Nahavandi | 2019-04-22

**TLDR:** A real-time framework that can accurately detect, track and predict the intended actions of pedestrians based on a tracking-by-detection technique in conjunction with a novel spatio-temporal DenseNet model that has shown resilient and competitive results in comparison to other baseline approaches.

> Understanding the behaviors and intentions of humans are one of the main challenges autonomous ground vehicles still faced with. More specifically, when it comes to complex environments such as urban traffic scenes, inferring the intentions and actions of vulnerable road users such as pedestrians become even harder. In this paper, we address the problem of intent action prediction of pedestrians in urban traffic environments using only image sequences from a monocular RGB camera. We propose a real-time framework that can accurately detect, track and predict the intended actions of pedestrians based on a tracking-by-detection technique in conjunction with a novel spatio-temporal DenseNet model. We trained and evaluated our framework based on real data collected from urban traffic environments. Our framework has shown resilient and competitive results in comparison to other baseline approaches. Overall, we achieved an average precision score of 84.76% with real-time performance at 20 FPS.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶车辆在复杂城市交通场景中，仅凭单目RGB图像序列实时预测行人意图动作的难题。
**[核心方法]**：基于跟踪-检测（tracking-by-detection）技术，结合新型时空DenseNet（Spatio-Temporal DenseNet）模型进行行人检测、跟踪与意图预测。
**[创新与效果]**：在真实城市交通数据上，平均精度达84.76%，同时实现20 FPS的实时性能，相比基线方法表现出鲁棒性与竞争力。

### 2. [Pedestrian Head and Body Pose Estimation with CNN in the Context of Automated Driving](https://doi.org/10.5220/0009410903530360)
`相关度 90` · 行人姿态估计用于意图预测

*International Conference on Vehicle Technology and Intelligent Transport Systems* | M. Steinhoff, D. Goehring | 2020

**TLDR:** This paper presents a framework with which reference measurements of head and upper body poses for the generation of training data can be carried out and is extended in a semi-supervised manner which optimizes and generalizes the detector, so that it is applicable to the prediction of pedestrian intention.

> : The challenge of determining pedestrians head poses in camera images is a topic that has already been re-searched extensively. With the ever-increasing level of automation in the ﬁeld of Advanced Driver Assistance Systems, a robust head orientation detection is becoming more and more important for pedestrian safety. The fact that this topic is still relevant, however, indicates the complexity of this task. Recently, trained classiﬁers for discretized head poses have recorded the best results. But large databases, which are essential for an appropriate training of neural networks meeting the special requirements of automatic driving, can hardly be found. Therefore, this paper presents a framework with which reference measurements of head and upper body poses for the generation of training data can be carried out. This data is used to train a convolutional neural network for classifying head and upper body poses. The result is extended in a semi-supervised manner which optimizes and generalizes the detector, so that it is applicable to the prediction of pedestrian intention.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶中缺乏大规模标注数据用于行人头部和上半身姿态估计，限制了基于CNN的检测器对行人意图的预测能力。

**[核心方法]**：提出一个框架，通过参考测量（reference measurements）生成头部和上半身姿态的训练数据；训练卷积神经网络（CNN）进行姿态分类；并采用半监督学习（semi-supervised manner）优化和泛化检测器。

**[创新与效果]**：半监督方式扩展了检测器，使其在训练数据稀缺条件下仍能泛化，并适用于行人意图预测任务。

### 3. [Road use as conversation: a Multimodal Multi-scale Coordinated Action Model (MCAM) for immersive and generative traffic safety applications](https://doi.org/10.3389/fcomp.2026.1882606)
`相关度 85` · 多尺度VRU交互模型，强相关

*Frontiers of Computer Science* | Merve Keskin, Rebekah Wegener, M. Moser | 2026-07-17

**TLDR:** The MCAM is introduced, a theoretical framework that reconceptualises urban mobility as structured communicative interaction governed by shared behavioral conventions across three spatiotemporal scales—micro, meso, and macro (longer-term contextual)—operationalising conflict as a five-phase gradient progression from coordinated flow to conflict event.

> Understanding how VRUs—pedestrians, cyclists, and micromobility users—coordinate social action and resolve conflict in mixed-mode urban traffic is not merely a technological but a fundamental human factors problem. The challenge concerns how people perceive, attend to, and act upon the behavioral signals of others under real-world conditions of unpredictability and uncertainty. Intention prediction is a key mechanism through which traffic safety applications attempt to manage this complexity, yet existing frameworks treat coordination as a binary classification task and fail to capture its multi-scale, gradient nature. Current research has major gaps related to data, robustness, and contextual understanding. XR and digital twin systems are deployed for urban traffic safety, yet a critical validity problem persists: these systems are designed and evaluated without ground-truth knowledge of how people look, move, and experience cognitive load in real mixed-mode traffic. Simulator-based studies offer control but sacrifice ecological validity; existing real-world datasets are vehicle-centric, single-modality, and restricted to pedestrian-crossing scenarios. This paper introduces the MCAM, a theoretical framework that reconceptualises urban mobility as structured communicative interaction governed by shared behavioral conventions across three spatiotemporal scales—micro (1–5 s), meso (5–30 s), and macro (longer-term contextual)—operationalising conflict as a five-phase gradient progression from
 coordinated flow
 to
 conflict event
 . MCAM is operationalised through a multimodal field study using a BIBD at three ecologically distinct urban sites (a signalized intersection, a shared space, and a cycling corridor), observed across peak hours and seasonal conditions. Thirty gender- and age-balanced participants each navigate all three sites as pedestrians, cyclists, and e-scooter riders—a within-subject cross-modal design is often absent from the majority of existing intention detection datasets— would give approximately 60 h of usable recording and 1,500 hours of annotation. Three contributions are presented: (1) a reusable multimodal field methodology for ecologically valid human factors data collection; (2) an MCAM-grounded annotation scheme applicable to both real and simulated environments; and (3) a methodological bridge between real-world human behavior and the design of immersive and generative traffic safety applications.

**[核心方法]**：提出MCAM理论框架，将城市交通重构为结构化交流互动，按微(1-5s)、中(5-30s)、宏(长期上下文)三时空尺度组织，冲突定义为五阶段渐变梯度（从协调流到冲突事件）；通过多模态实地研究（BIBD设计，3个生态各异地点，30名参与者跨模式作为行人、自行车、电动滑板车）收集约60小时可用记录和1500小时标注。  
**[创新与效果]**：①提供可复用的多模态实地方法论；②提出基于MCAM的标注方案（适用于真实与模拟环境）；③建立真实人类行为与沉浸式/生成式交通安全应用之间的方法论桥梁，解决数据、鲁棒性和上下文理解的关键空白。

### 4. [RobustTP: End-to-End Trajectory Prediction for Heterogeneous Road-Agents in Dense Traffic with Noisy Sensor Inputs](https://doi.org/10.1145/3359999.3360495)
`相关度 85` · 包含行人的轨迹预测

*Computer Science in Cars Symposium* | Rohan Chandra, Uttaran Bhattacharya, ..., Aniket Bera, Dinesh Manocha | 2019-07-20

**TLDR:** This work presents RobustTP, an end-to-end algorithm for predicting future trajectories of road-agents in dense traffic with noisy sensor input trajectories obtained from RGB cameras through a tracking algorithm that outperforms state-of-the-art methods for end- to-end trajectory prediction using sensor inputs.

> We present RobustTP, an end-to-end algorithm for predicting future trajectories of road-agents in dense traffic with noisy sensor input trajectories obtained from RGB cameras (either static or moving) through a tracking algorithm. In this case, we consider noise as the deviation from the ground truth trajectory. The amount of noise depends on the accuracy of the tracking algorithm. Our approach is designed for dense heterogeneous traffic, where the road agents corresponding to a mixture of buses, cars, scooters, bicycles, or pedestrians. RobustTP is an approach that first computes trajectories using a combination of a non-linear motion model and a deep learning-based instance segmentation algorithm. Next, these noisy trajectories are trained using an LSTM-CNN neural network architecture that models the interactions between road-agents in dense and heterogeneous traffic. Our trajectory prediction algorithm outperforms state-of-the-art methods for end-to-end trajectory prediction using sensor inputs. We achieve an improvement of upto 18% in average displacement error and an improvement of up to 35.5% in final displacement error at the end of the prediction window (5 seconds) over the next best method. All experiments were set up on an Nvidia TiTan Xp GPU. Additionally, we release a software framework, TrackNPred. The framework consists of implementations of state-of-the-art tracking and trajectory prediction methods and tools to benchmark and evaluate them on real-world dense traffic datasets.

**[试图解决的问题]**：在密集异质交通场景下，利用RGB相机（静态或移动）通过跟踪算法获取的含噪传感器轨迹进行端到端轨迹预测。

**[核心方法]**：先结合非线性运动模型与深度学习实例分割算法计算轨迹，再采用LSTM-CNN神经网络架构建模密集异质交通中道路代理之间的交互。

**[创新与效果]**：在5秒预测窗口上，平均位移误差提升最高达18%，最终位移误差提升最高达35.5%，优于现有基于传感器输入的端到端轨迹预测方法。

### 5. [ECAM: A Contrastive Learning Approach to Avoid Environmental Collision in Trajectory Forecasting](https://doi.org/10.1109/IJCNN64981.2025.11229361)
`相关度 75` · 轨迹预测方法，含行人意图

*IEEE International Joint Conference on Neural Network* | Giacomo Rosin, Muhammad Rameez Ur Rahman, S. Vascon | 2025-06-11

**TLDR:** This paper introduces ECAM (Environmental Collision Avoidance Module), a contrastive learning-based module to enhance collision avoidance ability with the environment and shows that state-of-the-art methods significantly reduce the collision rate when integrated with the proposed module.

> Human trajectory forecasting is crucial in applications such as autonomous driving, robotics and surveillance. Accurate forecasting requires models to consider various factors, including social interactions, multi-modal predictions, pedestrian intention and environmental context. While existing methods account for these factors, they often overlook the impact of the environment, which leads to collisions with obstacles. This paper introduces ECAM (Environmental Collision Avoidance Module), a contrastive learning-based module to enhance collision avoidance ability with the environment. The proposed module can be integrated into existing trajectory forecasting models, improving their ability to generate collision-free predictions. We evaluate our method on the ETH/UCY dataset and quantitatively and qualitatively demonstrate its collision avoidance capabilities. Our experiments show that state-of-the-art methods significantly reduce (-40/50%) the collision rate when integrated with the proposed module. The code is available at https://github.com/CVML-CFU/ECAM.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有轨迹预测模型忽略环境障碍物，导致预测轨迹与障碍物发生碰撞。  
**[核心方法]**：提出ECAM（环境碰撞避免模块），基于对比学习框架，可集成至现有轨迹预测模型。  
**[创新与效果]**：在ETH/UCY数据集上，集成ECAM后SOTA方法的碰撞率显著降低40-50%。
