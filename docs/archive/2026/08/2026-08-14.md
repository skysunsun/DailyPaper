### 1. [Long-Term Recurrent Predictive Model for Intent Prediction of Pedestrians via Inverse Reinforcement Learning](https://doi.org/10.1109/DICTA.2018.8615854)
`相关度 95` · 行人意图预测，逆强化学习

*International Conference on Digital Image Computing: Techniques and Applications* | Khaled Saleh, M. Hossny, S. Nahavandi | 2018-12-01

**TLDR:** This work is proposing a data-driven framework based on Inverse Reinforcement Learning (IRL) and the bidirectional recurrent neural network architecture (B-LSTM) for long-term prediction of pedestrians' trajectories and has outperformed other baseline models with the lowest margin of average displacement error.

> Recently, the problem of intent and trajectory prediction of pedestrians in urban traffic environments has got some attention from the intelligent transportation research community. One of the main challenges that make this problem even harder is the uncertainty exists in the actions of pedestrians in urban traffic environments, as well as the difficulty in inferring their end goals. In this work, we are proposing a data-driven framework based on Inverse Reinforcement Learning (IRL) and the bidirectional recurrent neural network architecture (B-LSTM) for long-term prediction of pedestrians' trajectories. We evaluated our framework on real-life datasets for agent behavior modeling in traffic environments and it has achieved an overall average displacement error of only 2.93 and 4.12 pixels over 2.0 secs and 3.0 secs ahead prediction horizons respectively. Additionally, we compared our framework against other baseline models based on sequence prediction models only. We have outperformed these models with the lowest margin of average displacement error of more than 5 pixels.

**[试图解决的问题]**：城市交通环境中行人的不确定性及最终目标难推断，导致长期轨迹与意图预测困难。

**[核心方法]**：基于逆强化学习（IRL）与双向递归神经网络（B-LSTM）的数据驱动框架，用于行人长期轨迹预测。

**[创新与效果]**：在真实数据集上，2.0秒预测时平均位移误差仅2.93像素，3.0秒预测时误差4.12像素；相比仅基于序列预测的基线模型，误差降低超过5像素。

### 2. [GD-GAN: Generative Adversarial Networks for Trajectory Prediction and Group Detection in Crowds](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-20887-5_20)
`相关度 90` · 行人轨迹预测与群体检测

*Asian Conference on Computer Vision* | Tharindu Fernando, Simon Denman, S. Sridharan, C. Fookes | 2018-12-02

**TLDR:** A generative adversarial pipeline is introduced which preserves the spatio-temporal structure of the pedestrian’s neighbourhood, enabling them to extract relevant attributes describing their social identity.

> This paper presents a novel deep learning framework for human trajectory prediction and detecting social group membership in crowds. We introduce a generative adversarial pipeline which preserves the spatio-temporal structure of the pedestrian’s neighbourhood, enabling us to extract relevant attributes describing their social identity. We formulate the group detection task as an unsupervised learning problem, obviating the need for supervised learning of group memberships via hand labeled databases, allowing us to directly employ the proposed framework in different surveillance settings. We evaluate the proposed trajectory prediction and group detection frameworks on multiple public benchmarks, and for both tasks the proposed method demonstrates its capability to better anticipate human sociological behaviour compared to the existing state-of-the-art methods (This research was supported by the Australian Research Council’s Linkage Project LP140100282 “Improving Productivity and Efficiency of Australian Airports”).

**[试图解决的问题]**：如何在无监督条件下实现人群中的轨迹预测与社会群体成员检测，避免依赖手工标注的群体标签。

**[核心方法]**：采用生成对抗网络（GAN）流水线，通过保留行人邻域的时空结构来提取描述其社会身份的特征，并将群体检测构建为无监督学习问题。

**[创新与效果]**：在多个公开基准上，该方法在轨迹预测和群体检测两项任务中均优于现有最先进方法，且无需监督式群体标注即可直接适用于不同监控场景。

### 3. [Pedestrian trajectory prediction via the Social‐Grid LSTM model](https://doi.org/10.1049/JOE.2018.8316)
`相关度 90` · 行人轨迹预测，Social-Grid LSTM

*Jurnal Engineering* | Bang Cheng, Xin Xu, ..., Junkai Ren, Seul Jung | 2018-11-01

**TLDR:** The authors present a new method for predicting the pedestrian's trajectory, which is called Social-Grid LSTM based on RNN architecture, which combines the human–human interaction model called social pooling and the Grid L STM network model.

> In the design of intelligent driving systems, reliable and accurate trajectory prediction of pedestrians is necessary. With the prediction of pedestrians’ trajectory, the possible collisions can be avoided or warned as early as possible by changing the behaviour of intelligent vehicles. The trajectory prediction problem can be considered as a sequence learning problem, in which one of the recurrent neural network (RNN) models called long short term memory (LSTM) has been regarded as a promising method. The authors present a new method for predicting the pedestrian's trajectory, which is called Social-Grid LSTM based on RNN architecture. The proposed method combines the human–human interaction model called social pooling and the Grid LSTM network model. The performance of the proposed method is demonstrated on two available public datasets, and compared with two baseline methods (LSTM and Social LSTM). The experimental results indicate that the authors’ proposed method outperforms previous prediction approaches.

**[试图解决的问题]**：智能驾驶系统中需要可靠准确的行人轨迹预测以提前规避碰撞，现有序列学习方法（如标准LSTM）预测精度不足。
**[核心方法]**：基于RNN架构的Social-Grid LSTM模型，融合了Social Pooling（人-人交互模型）与Grid LSTM网络。
**[创新与效果]**：在两个公开数据集上，相比LSTM和Social LSTM基线方法，预测性能更优。

### 4. [Social Intelligence for Autonomous Vehicles Interacting With Vulnerable Road Users](https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2024.3376872)
`相关度 85` · VRU交互，直接相关

*IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print)* | Yaobin Chen, Renran Tian | 2024-04-01

**TLDR:** 无

> Protecting vulnerable road users (VRUs) has always been a top priority in intelligent transportation research. Safety-oriented pre-collision systems have been developed and implemented for vehicles with lower automation levels to protect vulnerable road users via kinematics-based crash calculations. Many research efforts, including naturalistic driving study and standardized system evaluation, have been made to ensure the performance of such safety functionalities. Although the warnings and automatic emergency braking systems can efficiently reduce crash injuries, they cannot support higher-level autonomous vehicles interacting with VRUs and driving efficiently without human interventions in urban settings. Numerous algorithms predicting pedestrian trajectories and future actions have been developed in the past several years, which still cannot fully solve the VRU interaction dilemma. In this presentation, we discuss the importance of social intelligence for autonomous vehicles, emphasizing the capability to estimate and negotiate intentions with VRUs for effective and safe interactions. We will highlight the differences between intention and existing behavior predictions, especially regarding autonomous vehicles’ motion planning during vehicle-VRU interactions. A series of research efforts conducted in the Transportation & Autonomous Systems Institute (TASI) at IUPUI will be presented related to developing intention prediction algorithms and benchmark datasets for all types of VRUs. We will propose and discuss open challenges for developing autonomous vehicles with social intelligence.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有安全系统（如碰撞预警、自动紧急制动）无法支持高级别自动驾驶汽车在无人类干预下与弱势道路使用者（VRU）进行高效、安全的交互，行为预测无法完全解决VRU交互困境。

**[核心方法]**：提出社会智能（Social Intelligence）框架，强调自动驾驶汽车需具备估计和协商VRU意图的能力，区别于传统行为预测；开发了针对所有类型VRU的意图预测算法及基准数据集。

**[创新与效果]**：将意图预测与行为预测区分，并应用于自动驾驶运动规划中，以提升车辆与VRU交互的有效性和安全性；通过构建专用数据集和算法，为高级别自动驾驶在复杂城市环境中与VRU交互提供解决方案。

### 5. [A Literature Review on the Prediction of Pedestrian Behavior in Urban Scenarios](https://doi.org/10.1109/ITSC.2018.8569415)
`相关度 85` · 行人行为预测综述

*International Conference on Intelligent Transportation Systems* | Daniela A. Ridel, Eike Rehder, ..., C. Stiller, D. Wolf | 2018-11-01

**TLDR:** 无

> The ability to anticipate pedestrian actions on streets is a safety issue for intelligent cars and has increasingly drawn the attention of the automotive industry. Estimating when pedestrians will cross streets has proved a challenging task, since they can move in many different directions, suddenly change motion, be occluded by a variety of obstacles and distracted while talking to other pedestrians or typing on a mobile phone. Moreover, their decisions can also be affected by several factors. This paper explores the ways pedestrians' intention estimation has been studied, evaluated, and evolved. It provides a literature review on pedestrian behavior prediction, addresses available solutions, state-of-the-art developments, and hurdles to be overcome towards reaching a solution that is closer to the human ability to predict and interpret such scenarios. Although many studies can precisely estimate pedestrians' positioning one second before they cross a street, most of them cannot precisely predict when they will stop at a curb.

**[试图解决的问题]**：智能汽车在城市场景中难以精确预测行人何时会在路边停下，而非仅能估计过街前一秒的位置。  
**[核心方法]**：对行人意图估计的研究方法、评估方式和演进进行了文献综述，涵盖现有解决方案与最新进展，包括应对行人突然变向、遮挡、分心等挑战的各类方法。  
**[创新与效果]**：指出现有研究虽能精确估计行人过街前一秒的位置，但多数无法精确预测行人在路边停止的行为，并指出需向接近人类预测能力的方向发展。
