### 1. [Social-VRNN: One-Shot Multi-modal Trajectory Prediction for Interacting Pedestrians](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/5ed808f79e4846b5f3b50d9196557ed50ae9145b)
`相关度 95` · 行人交互轨迹预测，多模态

*Conference on Robot Learning* | B. Brito, Hai Zhu, Wei Pan, Javier Alonso-Mora | 2020-10-18

**TLDR:** This paper presents a variational learning approach for interaction-aware and multi-modal trajectory prediction based on deep generative neural networks that can achieve faster convergence and requires significantly fewer samples comparing to state-of-the-art methods.

> Prediction of human motions is key for safe navigation of autonomous robots among humans. In cluttered environments, several motion hypotheses may exist for a pedestrian, due to its interactions with the environment and other pedestrians. 
Previous works for estimating multiple motion hypotheses require a large number of samples which limits their applicability in real-time motion planning. In this paper, we present a variational learning approach for interaction-aware and multi-modal trajectory prediction based on deep generative neural networks. 
Our approach can achieve faster convergence and requires significantly fewer samples comparing to state-of-the-art methods. Experimental results on real and simulation data show that our model can effectively learn to infer different trajectories. We compare our method with three baseline approaches and present performance results demonstrating that our generative model can achieve higher accuracy for trajectory prediction by producing diverse trajectories.

**[试图解决的问题]**：传统多模态轨迹预测方法需要大量样本，限制了其在实时运动规划中的适用性。

**[核心方法]**：基于深度生成神经网络的变分学习框架（Social-VRNN），引入交互感知机制，实现单次推理的多模态轨迹预测。

**[创新与效果]**：相比现有方法，收敛速度更快且所需样本量显著减少；在真实与仿真数据上，通过生成多样化轨迹提升了轨迹预测精度。

### 2. [A heuristic model for pedestrian intention estimation](https://doi.org/10.1109/ITSC.2019.8917195)
`相关度 95` · 行人过街意图估计模型

*International Conference on Intelligent Transportation Systems* | F. Camara, N. Merat, Charles W. Fox | 2019-10-01

**TLDR:** A simple heuristic model of intent is tested on pedestrian-vehicle trajectory data for the first time and can predict pedestrian crossing intent, crossing or stopping, with 96% accuracy by the time the pedestrian reaches the curbside, on the standard Daimler pedestrian dataset.

> Understanding pedestrian behaviour and controlling interactions with pedestrians is of critical importance for autonomous vehicles, but remains a complex and challenging problem. This study infers pedestrian intent during possible road-crossing interactions, to assist autonomous vehicle decisions to yield or not yield when approaching them, and tests a simple heuristic model of intent on pedestrian-vehicle trajectory data for the first time. It relies on a heuristic approach based on the observed positions of the agents over time. The method can predict pedestrian crossing intent, crossing or stopping, with 96% accuracy by the time the pedestrian reaches the curbside, on the standard Daimler pedestrian dataset. This result is important in demarcating scenarios which have a clear winner and can be predicted easily with the simple heuristic, from those which may require more complex game-theoretic models to predict and control.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶汽车在接近行人时，难以准确推断其过马路意图（过或停），从而影响决策是否让行。
**[核心方法]**：基于行人-车辆轨迹数据中两者的历史观察位置，首次采用简单启发式模型（Heuristic model）进行意图预测。
**[创新与效果]**：在标准Daimler行人数据集上，当行人到达路边时，过马路意图预测准确率达96%，并厘清了可通过简单启发式解决的场景与需复杂博弈论模型的场景边界。

### 3. [Uncertainty Estimation of Pedestrian Future Trajectory Using Bayesian Approximation](https://doi.org/10.1109/OJITS.2022.3205504)
`相关度 90` · 行人未来轨迹不确定性估计

*IEEE Open Journal of Intelligent Transportation Systems* | Anshul Nayak, A. Eskandarian, Zachary R. Doerzaph | 2022-05-04

**TLDR:** The authors compared the predictions between the probabilistic neural network (NN) models with the standard deterministic models and found that the mean predicted path of Probabilistic models was closer to the ground truth when compared with the deterministic prediction.

> Past research on pedestrian trajectory forecasting mainly focused on deterministic predictions which provide only point estimates of future states. These future estimates can help an autonomous vehicle plan its trajectory and avoid collision. However, under dynamic traffic scenarios, planning based on deterministic predictions is not trustworthy. Rather, estimating the uncertainty associated with the predicted states with certain level of confidence can lead to robust path planning. Hence, the authors propose to quantify this uncertainty during forecasting using stochastic approximation which deterministic approaches fail to capture. The current method is simple and applies Bayesian approximation during inference to standard neural network architectures for estimating uncertainty. The authors compared the predictions between the probabilistic neural network (NN) models with the standard deterministic models. The results indicate that the mean predicted path of probabilistic models was closer to the ground truth when compared with the deterministic prediction. Further, the effect of stochastic dropout of weights and long-term prediction on future state uncertainty has been studied. It was found that the probabilistic models produced better performance metrics like average displacement error (ADE) and final displacement error (FDE). Finally, the study has been extended to multiple datasets providing a comprehensive comparison for each model.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有行人轨迹预测多为确定性点估计，在动态交通场景下不可靠，需量化预测不确定性以实现鲁棒路径规划。  
**[核心方法]**：在标准神经网络推理过程中应用贝叶斯近似（随机丢弃权重）进行随机逼近，实现不确定性估计。  
**[创新与效果]**：概率模型预测的平均路径相比确定性模型更接近真实轨迹，且平均位移误差（ADE）和最终位移误差（FDE）指标更优；研究还分析了随机丢弃权重及长期预测对未来状态不确定性的影响，并在多个数据集上进行了综合对比。

### 4. [Agent-Based Modeling for Predicting Pedestrian Trajectories Around an Autonomous Vehicle](https://doi.org/10.1613/jair.1.13425)
`相关度 90` · 行人轨迹与AV交互建模

*Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research* | Manon Prédhumeau, L. Mancheva, J. Dugdale, A. Spalanzani | 2022-04-19

**TLDR:** An expert pedestrian model is proposed that combines the social force model and a new decision model for anticipating pedestrian–vehicle interactions that reproduces observed behaviors that have not been replicated by the socialForce model and outperforms the socialforce model at predicting pedestrian behavior around the vehicle on the used dataset.

> This paper addresses modeling and simulating pedestrian trajectories when interacting with an autonomous vehicle in a shared space. Most pedestrian–vehicle interaction models are not suitable for predicting individual trajectories. Data-driven models yield accurate predictions but lack generalizability to new scenarios, usually do not run in real time and produce results that are poorly explainable. Current expert models do not deal with the diversity of possible pedestrian interactions with the vehicle in a shared space and lack microscopic validation. We propose an expert pedestrian model that combines the social force model and a new decision model for anticipating pedestrian–vehicle interactions. The proposed model integrates different observed pedestrian behaviors, as well as the behaviors of the social groups of pedestrians, in diverse interaction scenarios with a car. We calibrate the model by fitting the parameters values on a training set. We validate the model and evaluate its predictive potential through qualitative and quantitative comparisons with ground truth trajectories. The proposed model reproduces observed behaviors that have not been replicated by the social force model and outperforms the social force model at predicting pedestrian behavior around the vehicle on the used dataset. The model generates explainable and real-time trajectory predictions. Additional evaluation on a new dataset shows that the model generalizes well to new scenarios and can be applied to an autonomous vehicle embedded prediction.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有行人-车辆交互模型无法准确预测个体轨迹，数据驱动模型泛化性差、非实时且不可解释，专家模型未涵盖交互多样性且缺乏微观验证。

**[核心方法]**：提出结合社会力模型（Social Force Model）与新型决策模型的专家模型，整合行人个体及社会群体在多种交互场景下的行为，并通过训练集拟合参数进行标定。

**[创新与效果]**：模型复现了社会力模型未能复现的观察行为，在所用数据集上预测行人轨迹表现优于社会力模型，生成可解释的实时轨迹预测，且在新数据集上泛化良好，适用于自动驾驶车辆嵌入式预测。

### 5. [Contextual Recurrent Predictive Model for Long-Term Intent Prediction of Vulnerable Road Users](https://doi.org/10.1109/TITS.2019.2927770)
`相关度 90` · VRU长期意图预测，直接相关

*IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print)* | Khaled Saleh, M. Hossny, S. Nahavandi | 2020-08-01

**TLDR:** A data-driven framework based on inverse reinforcement learning (IRL) and the bidirectional recurrent neural network architecture (B-LSTM) for long-term prediction of VRUs’ intention is proposed and it achieves robust results with higher generalization capabilities.

> Recently, the problem of intent and trajectory prediction of vulnerable road users (VRUs) in urban traffic environments has got some attention from the intelligent transportation research community. One of the main challenges that make this problem even harder is the uncertainty exists in the actions of pedestrians in urban traffic environments, as well as the difficulty in inferring their end goals. In this paper, we are proposing a data-driven framework based on inverse reinforcement learning (IRL) and the bidirectional recurrent neural network architecture (B-LSTM) for long-term prediction of VRUs’ intention. We evaluated our framework on real-life datasets for agent behavior modeling in traffic environments, and it has achieved an overall average displacement error of only 2.93 and 4.12 pixels over 2.0 and 3.0 s ahead prediction horizons, respectively. In addition, we compared our framework against other baseline models based on sequence prediction models and planning-based approaches. We have outperformed these approaches with the lowest margin of average displacement error of more than 5 pixels. Furthermore, the performance of the proposed framework was evaluated on an additional vehicle-based video sequence dataset for path prediction of pedestrians and it continued to achieve robust results with higher generalization capabilities.

**[试图解决的问题]**：城市交通中弱势道路使用者（VRUs）的行为不确定性及终点推断困难，导致长期意图预测精度低。  
**[核心方法]**：基于逆强化学习（IRL）与双向长短期记忆网络（B-LSTM）的数据驱动框架，用于长期意图预测。  
**[创新与效果]**：在2.0秒和3.0秒预测视界上，平均位移误差分别仅为2.93和4.12像素，相比基线模型（误差>5像素）显著降低；在额外车辆数据集上验证了泛化能力和鲁棒性。
