### 1. [MMCF-Net: A Micro-Motion Context Fusion Framework for Pedestrian Crossing Intention Recognition in Urban Scenes](https://doi.org/10.1109/TCE.2025.3605791)
`相关度 95` · 直接研究行人过街意图识别

*IEEE transactions on consumer electronics* | Menghao Li, Wei-Wei Zhang, ..., JieJie Xu, Jun Li | 2026-08-01

**TLDR:** 无

> As advanced vision sensors and edge computing platforms become increasingly integrated into modern vehicles as consumer technologies and electronics, how to accurately recognize pedestrian crossing intentions in urban environments is a noteworthy consideration. Existing research largely relies on skeletal features or trajectory prediction, which demonstrates limited performance in complex scenarios. A Micro-Motion Context Fusion Network (MMCF-Net) is proposed to address these limitations. First, a high-resolution spatiotemporal attention module is constructed that captures sub-pixel-level micro-motion features such as head direction shifts, center of gravity adjustments, and limb pre-activation through deformable convolution and dual attention mechanisms. Second, a hierarchical multi-modal context encoder is designed that adaptively fuses traffic element semantics, vehicle dynamics, and perspective-corrected spatial relationships. Finally, a dynamic weight allocation network is introduced that implements optimal combination of micro-motion features and contextual information based on scene complexity. Experiments demonstrate that MMCF-Net achieves an F1 score of 92.3%, an 7.0% improvement over existing methods like PIE, with enhanced robustness in low-light and occluded scenarios, providing a novel approach to pedestrian safety in complex urban environments.

**[试图解决的问题]**：城市复杂场景中，现有基于骨骼特征或轨迹预测的行人过街意图识别方法性能有限，尤其在低光照、遮挡等条件下鲁棒性不足。

**[核心方法]**：提出MMCF-Net，包含：1) 高分辨率时空注意力模块（可变形卷积+双注意力机制）捕获亚像素级微运动特征（头部方向偏移、重心调整、肢体预激活）；2) 层次化多模态上下文编码器自适应融合交通元素语义、车辆动力学、视角校正空间关系；3) 动态权重分配网络根据场景复杂度对微运动特征与上下文信息进行最优组合。

**[创新与效果]**：F1分数达92.3%，较PIE等方法提升7.0%，在低光照和遮挡场景中鲁棒性显著增强。

### 2. [Pedestrian Crossing Intention Prediction via Temporal Dependency Modeling and Evidential Uncertainty Reasoning](https://doi.org/10.1109/WCCIS70285.2026.11650855)
`相关度 95` · 直接研究行人过街意图预测

*2026 3rd World Conference on Computer and Information Security (WCCIS)* | Hang Qi, Ying Jiang, Lu Xu, Jing-Wei Ke | 2026-07-01

**TLDR:** 无

> Pedestrian crossing intention prediction is a critical component bridging environmental perception and decision-making in autonomous driving systems. While existing methods have achieved progress in prediction accuracy, most treat this task as a deterministic classification problem, overlooking the inherent uncertainty in pedestrian behavior and annotation processes. This limitation leads to overconfident erroneous predictions in complex traffic scenarios. To address this challenge, we propose a pedestrian crossing intention prediction method based on temporal dependency modeling and evidential uncertainty reasoning. The proposed approach takes structured temporal features as input and employs a temporal feature representation module followed by a Transformer-based temporal dependency modeling module to capture behavioral evolution patterns within consecutive observation windows. Building upon this foundation, we introduce a dual-head evidential mechanism that maps behavioral features to supporting evidence for different intention categories and constructs Dirichlet distribution parameters for explicit uncertainty quantification. During inference, the model simultaneously outputs crossing intention predictions and corresponding uncertainty measures, providing a principled basis for risk-aware decision-making. Extensive experiments on the PIE and JAAD benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed method achieves superior prediction accuracy compared to multiple existing approaches. On PIE, the model attains 91.01% accuracy, 91.01% F1-score, and 95.33% AUROC; on JAAD, it achieves 84.97% accuracy, 84.97% F1-score, and 92.24% AUROC. Furthermore, uncertainty analysis reveals that incorrect predictions cluster at high uncertainty regions, and selective prediction by filtering high-uncertainty samples pushes accuracy beyond 97%, validating the effectiveness and practical utility of combining temporal dependency modeling with evidential uncertainty reasoning.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有行人过街意图预测方法忽略行人行为与标注中的固有不确定性，导致复杂场景下过度自信的错误预测。  
**[核心方法]**：采用时间特征表示模块与Transformer时间依赖建模模块提取行为演化模式，并通过双头证据机制将特征映射为狄利克雷分布参数，实现显式不确定性量化，同时输出意图预测与不确定性度量。  
**[创新与效果]**：在PIE/JAAD数据集上达到91.01%/84.97%准确率、95.33%/92.24% AUROC；过滤高不确定性样本后准确率超97%，验证了结合时间依赖建模与证据不确定性推理可有效提升预测可靠性与风险感知决策能力。

### 3. [Vision-Based Real-Time Online Vulnerable Traffic Participants Trajectory Prediction for Autonomous Vehicle](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIV.2022.3227940)
`相关度 90` · 弱势交通参与者轨迹预测

*IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Vehicles* | Hao Chen, Yinhua Liu, ..., Chuanping Hu, Xi Zhang | 2023-03-01

**TLDR:** 无

> Recently, Vulnerable Traffic Participants (VTPs) trajectory prediction has got some attention, which can help autonomous vehicles better understand complex traffic environments. This paper systematically investigates VTPs (pedestrians, cyclists, and electric cyclists) trajectory prediction based on a fresh data-driven stacking model. Firstly, a micro-dynamic Modified Social Force Model (MSFM) is proposed, pedestrian-cyclist-electric cyclist-dynamic vehicle interactions, the effect of zebra crossing and pedestrian heterogeneity (age and gender) are taken into account. Then an Attention-Long Short Term Memory Network (AN-LSTM) is developed, AN is used to achieve the influence weights of pedestrian heterogeneity and road users. Finally, a fresh stacking model by combining the MSFM and AN-LSTM is proposed for VTPs trajectory prediction. The stacking model is evaluated with the current state-of-the-art models. The results indicate that the proposed model achieves an increased accuracy of more than 12%. Moreover, a real-time online integrated vision system which combines target detection, multi-target tracking, pedestrian heterogeneity recognition and distance measurement is developed. The proposed model is tested with the integrated vision system, and high accuracy and good real-time performance (0.030 seconds) are achieved, which can give us great confidence to use the proposed model in the autonomous vehicles for improving VTPs safety and transportation efficiency.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶中弱势交通参与者（行人、自行车、电动车）轨迹预测的精度不足，且缺乏考虑行人异质性、斑马线影响及多类交互的实时在线系统。

**[核心方法]**：提出一种堆叠模型，底层为微动态修正社会力模型（MSFM），融合行人-自行车-电动车-动态车辆交互、斑马线及行人异质性（年龄、性别）；上层为注意力长短期记忆网络（AN-LSTM），通过注意力机制加权异质性与道路使用者影响；并集成实时在线视觉系统（目标检测、多目标跟踪、异质性识别、距离测量）。

**[创新与效果]**：堆叠模型相比当前最优方法精度提升12%以上；集成视觉系统在真实场景下实现0.030秒推理延迟，兼具高精度与实时性，可直接用于提升VTPs安全与交通效率。

### 4. [Communication Strategies for Effective Interaction With Pedestrians and Automated Service Vehicles in Japanese Parking Areas](https://doi.org/10.1080/10447318.2023.2285622)
`相关度 75` · 车-人交互，停车场行人策略

*International journal of human computer interactions* | Jieun Lee, T. Daimon | 2023-12-03

**TLDR:** 无

> Abstract One of the challenges in introducing automated service vehicles (ASVs) to parking areas is guiding pedestrians to make quick and appropriate crossing decisions based on an understanding of what the ASV typically does. This study aims to find effective communication strategies for ASVs for pedestrian crossings in Japanese parking areas. Virtual Reality experiments were conducted with 40 participants facilitating vehicle behavior (Normal Deceleration, Early Deceleration, Early Stop) and text-based external human-machine interfaces (eHMIs; No eHMI, “After you,” “I’ll stop,” “In Automated Driving”) as tools of communication with two types of ASVs (bus and golf cart) in parking areas. The results indicated that vehicle behavior significantly influenced pedestrian crossing decisions, and pedestrians began to cross when the ASV decelerated early and stopped not too far from them (Early Deceleration). Self-reported measure analyses revealed that text messages containing the intent of the ASV's actions or instruction (After you, I’ll stop) with Early Deceleration would probably lead to pedestrians’ favorable attitudes toward automated buses. These findings contribute to communication designs based on implicit communication for pedestrian crossing decision-making in parking areas with a clear interpretation of ASV’s intentions.

**[试图解决的问题]**：在停车场引入自动服务车辆（ASVs）时，如何引导行人快速理解ASV意图并做出合适的横穿决策。  
**[核心方法]**：通过VR实验（40名参与者），测试车辆行为（正常减速、早期减速、早期停止）与文本型外部人机界面（无eHMI、“您先请”、“我会停下”、“自动驾驶中”）的交互组合，针对两种ASV车型（巴士、高尔夫球车）。  
**[创新与效果]**：发现早期减速显著影响横穿决策；结合“您先请”或“我会停下”文本的早期减速能提升行人对自动巴士的积极态度，为基于隐式通信的停车区横穿决策设计提供依据。

### 5. [A Short-Range FMCW Radar-based Approach for Multi-Target Human-Vehicle Detection](https://doi.org/10.1109/tgrs.2021.3138687)
`相关度 70` · 雷达人车检测，行人检测相关

*IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing* | Emanuele Tavanti, Ali Rizik, ..., D. Caviglia, A. Randazzo | 2021

**TLDR:** 无

> In the present paper, a new microwave-radar-based technique for short-range detection and classification of multiple human and vehicle targets crossing a monitored area is proposed. This approach, which can find applications in both security and infrastructure surveillance, relies upon the processing of the scattered-field data acquired by low-cost off-the-shelf components, i.e., a 24 GHz Frequency Modulated Continuous Wave radar module and a Raspberry Pi mini-PC. The developed method is based on an ad-hoc processing chain to accomplish the automatic target recognition task, which consists of blocks performing clutter and leakage removal with an IIR filter, clustering with a DBSCAN approach, tracking using a Benedict-Bordner α-β filter, features extraction, and finally classification of targets by means of a k-Nearest Neighbor algorithm. The approach is validated in real experimental scenarios, showing its capabilities in correctly detecting multiple targets belonging to different classes (i.e., pedestrians, cars, motorcycles, and trucks).

**[试图解决的问题]**：在短距离监控区域内，利用低成本商用雷达组件实现多目标（行人、汽车、摩托车、卡车）的自动检测与分类。

**[核心方法]**：采用24 GHz FMCW雷达模块与树莓派采集散射场数据，通过IIR滤波器去除杂波和泄漏，DBSCAN聚类，Benedict-Bordner α-β滤波器跟踪，提取特征后使用k-NN算法进行分类。

**[创新与效果]**：在真实实验场景中成功检测并区分了多个不同类别的目标（行人、汽车、摩托车、卡车），验证了该方法对多目标分类的有效性。
