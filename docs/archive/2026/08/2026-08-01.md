### 1. [Pedestrian Crossing Intention Prediction Based on Decision Semantic Fusion and Hierarchical Transformer](https://doi.org/10.1109/MRAI70020.2026.11621755)
`相关度 95` · 直接研究行人过街意图预测

*2026 2nd International Conference on Mechatronics, Robotics, and Artificial Intelligence (MRAI)* | Tongying Guo, Xubo Gu | 2026-05-01

**TLDR:** 无

> Pedestrian crossing intention prediction is a critical task for risk perception and early warning in autonomous driving. To address the limited focus on decision-relevant cues and insufficient interpretability of existing methods, this paper proposes a decision-semantic modeling approach. Specifically, the input is represented as five types of high-level decision-semantic tokens, and a hierarchical Transformer is employed to model both the temporal evolution of individual semantic factors and their interactions. In addition, visual enhancement, attribute-semantic enhancement, and semantic-relation enhancement modules are introduced to strengthen the representation of key cues. Experimental results on the JAAD and PIE datasets show that the proposed method achieves accuracies of 90% and 89%, respectively, and obtains superior performance in precision, recall, and F1score.Meanwhile, the main model maintains a compact structure and provides interpretable semantic cues, suggesting its potential for lightweight pedestrian intention prediction.

[试图解决的问题]：现有行人过街意图预测方法对决策相关线索关注不足且可解释性差。
[核心方法]：将输入表示为五种高层决策语义令牌，采用层次Transformer建模单个语义因素的时间演化及其交互，并引入视觉增强、属性语义增强和语义关系增强模块。
[创新与效果]：在JAAD和PIE数据集上分别达到90%和89%准确率，精度、召回率、F1分数均优于对比方法；模型结构紧凑且提供可解释语义线索，适用于轻量化预测。

### 2. [Assessing Cross-dataset Generalization of Pedestrian Crossing Predictors](https://doi.org/10.1109/iv51971.2022.9827083)
`相关度 95` · 行人过街预测泛化评估

*2022 IEEE Intelligent Vehicles Symposium (IV)* | Joseph Gesnouin, Steve Pechberti, B. Stanciulescu, F. Moutarde | 2022-01-29

**TLDR:** 无

> Pedestrian crossing prediction has been a topic of active research, resulting in many new algorithmic solutions. While measuring the overall progress of those solutions over time tends to be more and more established due to the new publicly available benchmark and standardized evaluation procedures, knowing how well existing predictors react to unseen data remains an unanswered question. This evaluation is imperative as serviceable crossing behavior predictors should be set to work in various scenarios without compromising pedestrian safety due to misprediction. To this end, we conduct a study based on direct cross-dataset evaluation. Our experiments show that current state-of-the-art pedestrian behavior predictors generalize poorly in cross-dataset evaluation scenarios, regardless of their robustness during a direct training-test set evaluation setting. In the light of what we observe, we argue that the future of pedestrian crossing prediction, e.g. reliable and generalizable implementations, should not be about tailoring models, trained with very little available data, and tested in a classical train-test scenario with the will to infer anything about their behavior in real life. It should be about evaluating models in a cross-dataset setting while considering their uncertainty estimates under domain shift.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有行人过街预测器在跨数据集场景（即面对未见过的数据分布）下的泛化能力未知，可能导致实际应用中因误判危及行人安全。

**[核心方法]**：直接进行跨数据集评估实验，将模型在一个数据集上训练，在另一个独立数据集上测试，对比其在标准训练-测试集设置与跨数据集设置下的表现。

**[创新与效果]**：实验表明当前最先进的行人行为预测器在跨数据集评估中泛化能力较差，即便在标准训练-测试集评估中表现稳健。据此指出未来研究应转向跨数据集评估框架，并考虑模型在域偏移下的不确定性估计。

### 3. [Pedestrian Stop and Go Forecasting with Hybrid Feature Fusion](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2203.02489)
`相关度 90` · 行人停止前进预测，直接相关

*IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation* | Dongxu Guo, Taylor Mordan, Alexandre Alahi | 2022-03-04

**TLDR:** 无

> Forecasting pedestrians' future motions is essential for autonomous driving systems to safely navigate in urban areas. However, existing prediction algorithms often overly rely on past observed trajectories and tend to fail around abrupt dynamic changes, such as when pedestrians suddenly start or stop walking. We suggest that predicting these highly non-linear transitions should form a core component to improve the robustness of motion prediction algorithms. In this paper, we introduce the new task of pedestrian stop and go forecasting. Considering the lack of suitable existing datasets for it, we release TRANS, a benchmark for explicitly studying the stop and go behaviors of pedestrians in urban traffic. We build it from several existing datasets annotated with pedestrians' walking motions, in order to have various scenarios and behaviors. We also propose a novel hybrid model that leverages pedestrian-specific and scene features from several modalities, both video sequences and high-level attributes, and gradually fuses them to integrate multiple levels of context. We evaluate our model and several baselines on TRANS, and set a new benchmark for the community to work on pedestrian stop and go forecasting.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有行人运动预测算法过度依赖历史轨迹，在行人突然启动或停止等剧烈动态变化时预测失败。  
**[核心方法]**：提出一种混合模型，融合行人专属特征与场景特征（视频序列+高层属性），通过多模态逐级融合机制整合多层次上下文。  
**[创新与效果]**：首次定义行人“停止与启动”预测任务，构建跨数据集基准TRANS；该模型在TRANS上设定新基准，提升对非线性过渡行为的预测鲁棒性。

### 4. [Pedestrian Action Anticipation using Contextual Feature Fusion in Stacked RNNs](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/472b3df8920f0939dda0a80bdc51e293130c1124)
`相关度 90` · 行人动作预测，堆叠RNN

*British Machine Vision Conference* | Amir Rasouli, Iuliia Kotseruba, John K. Tsotsos | 2020-05-13

**TLDR:** 无

> One of the major challenges for autonomous vehicles in urban environments is to understand and predict other road users' actions, in particular, pedestrians at the point of crossing. The common approach to solving this problem is to use the motion history of the agents to predict their future trajectories. However, pedestrians exhibit highly variable actions most of which cannot be understood without visual observation of the pedestrians themselves and their surroundings. To this end, we propose a solution for the problem of pedestrian action anticipation at the point of crossing. Our approach uses a novel stacked RNN architecture in which information collected from various sources, both scene dynamics and visual features, is gradually fused into the network at different levels of processing. We show, via extensive empirical evaluations, that the proposed algorithm achieves a higher prediction accuracy compared to alternative recurrent network architectures. We conduct experiments to investigate the impact of the length of observation, time to event and types of features on the performance of the proposed method. Finally, we demonstrate how different data fusion strategies impact prediction accuracy.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶在城市场景中预测行人过马路动作时，仅凭运动历史轨迹难以捕捉行人高度可变的行为，需要融合视觉上下文。

**[核心方法]**：采用堆叠循环神经网络（Stacked RNN）架构，在不同处理层级逐步融合场景动态特征与视觉特征（上下文特征融合）。

**[创新与效果]**：相比其他循环网络架构，行人过马路动作预测精度更高；通过实验分析了观测长度、事件时间及特征类型对性能的影响，并展示了不同数据融合策略的效果。

### 5. [Euro-PVI: Pedestrian Vehicle Interactions in Dense Urban Centers](https://doi.org/10.1109/CVPR46437.2021.00634)
`相关度 80` · 行人车交互轨迹预测

*Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition* | Apratim Bhattacharyya, Daniel Olmeda Reino, Mario Fritz, B. Schiele | 2021-06-01

**TLDR:** 无

> Accurate prediction of pedestrian and bicyclist paths is integral to the development of reliable autonomous vehicles in dense urban environments. The interactions between vehicle and pedestrian or bicyclist have a significant impact on the trajectories of traffic participants e.g. stopping or turning to avoid collisions. Although recent datasets and trajectory prediction approaches have fostered the development of autonomous vehicles yet the amount of vehicle-pedestrian (bicyclist) interactions modeled are sparse. In this work, we propose Euro-PVI, a dataset of pedestrian and bicyclist trajectories. In particular, our dataset caters more diverse and complex interactions in dense urban scenarios compared to the existing datasets. To address the challenges in predicting future trajectories with dense interactions, we develop a joint inference model that learns an expressive multi-modal shared latent space across agents in the urban scene. This enables our Joint-β-cVAE approach to better model the distribution of future trajectories. We achieve state of the art results on the nuScenes and Euro-PVI datasets demonstrating the importance of capturing interactions between ego-vehicle and pedestrians (bicyclists) for accurate predictions.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有数据集和轨迹预测模型对密集城区中车辆与行人/自行车之间的复杂交互建模稀疏，导致预测精度不足。  
**[核心方法]**：提出Euro-PVI数据集，并开发Joint-β-cVAE联合推理模型，通过跨智能体学习表达性多模态共享潜在空间来建模密集交互下的未来轨迹分布。  
**[创新与效果]**：在nuScenes和Euro-PVI数据集上取得最先进结果，验证了捕捉自车与行人/自行车交互对提升预测准确性的关键作用。
