### 1. [Pedestrian Behavior Prediction for Autonomous Vehicles using Deep Learning](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICDSBS69077.2026.11634987)
`相关度 95` · 直接预测行人过街行为

*2026 International Conference on Data Science and Business Systems (ICDSBS)* | Prathipa K, M. Arthy | 2026-05-01

**TLDR:** 无

> The challenge of ensuring real-time safety during autonomous driving goes beyond merely detecting pedestrians, requiring a detailed analysis of what they plan to do next. In our paper, we focus on mitigating the risks associated with the act of "road-darting" through developing a solution that is able to anticipate intent before any action is undertaken. Rather than relying on simplistic 2D object detection models, we designed an approach that combines a detection algorithm YOLOv8 with a BiLSTM, taking spatial information from the former as an input. We specifically trained the BiLSTM model to analyze 15-frame historical windows to distinguish the intent to cross from mere waiting. We have not only used probabilities in our work but also incorporated them in a mapping scheme that triggers Warning/Active braking actions depending on the level of intent. Running the experiments with the JAAD dataset proved effective as our framework delivered an accuracy of 86.9%. Perhaps most interestingly, the results demonstrated our model’s unique ability to recognize an imminent threat to crossing roughly half a second prior to the actual event.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶中行人突然横穿马路（road-darting）的实时安全风险，需在动作发生前预测意图而非仅检测。  
**[核心方法]**：结合YOLOv8目标检测与BiLSTM时序模型，以15帧历史窗口的空间信息作为输入，区分横穿与等待意图，并基于概率映射触发警告/主动制动。  
**[创新与效果]**：在JAAD数据集上达86.9%准确率，能在实际横穿事件发生前约0.5秒识别出即将发生的威胁。

### 2. [Predicting Road Crossing Behaviour using Pose Detection with Sequence Modelling](https://doi.org/10.1109/INDISCON66021.2025.11252371)
`相关度 95` · 直接预测过街行为

*2025 IEEE 6th India Council International Subsections Conference (INDISCON)* | Subhasis Dasgupta, Preetam Saha, Agniva Roy, Jaydip Sen | 2025-08-21

**TLDR:** 无

> The world is constantly moving towards AI based systems and autonomous vehicles are now reality in different parts of the world. These vehicles require sensors and cameras to detect objects and manoeuvre according to that. It becomes important to for such vehicles to also predict from a distant if a person is about to cross a road or not. The current study focused on predicting the intent of crossing the road by pedestrians in an experimental setup. The study involved working with deep learning models to predict poses and sequence modelling for temporal predictions. The study analysed two different sequence modelling to understand the prediction behaviour and it was found out that GRU was better in predicting the intent compared to LSTM model but 1D CNN was the best model in terms of speed. The study involved video analysis, and the output of pose detection model was integrated later on to sequence modelling techniques for an end-to-end deep learning framework for predicting road crossing intents.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶车辆需从远处预测行人横穿马路的意图，以提升安全性。
**[核心方法]**：采用姿态检测深度学习模型提取行人姿态，再结合序列建模（GRU、LSTM、1D CNN）进行时间序列预测，构建端到端框架。
**[创新与效果]**：对比发现GRU在预测意图上优于LSTM，而1D CNN在推理速度上最优；实现了基于视频的过马路意图预测。

### 3. [Deep learning for pedestrian behavior understanding](https://doi.org/10.1109/ATSIP55956.2022.9805916)
`相关度 95` · 直接行人行为理解

*International Conference on Advanced Technologies for Signal and Image Processing* | Soulayma Gazzeh, A. Douik | 2022-05-24

**TLDR:** 无

> Visual perception and road scene understanding are critical components of autonomous driving (AD). Detecting objects and predicting surrounding road user behavior are important tasks for robust and safe driving systems. With the rise of Machine Learning (ML) and Deep Learning (DL) methods using convolutional neural networks (CNN) , research on road scene understanding and motion recognition have made significant progress. In this paper, a real-time recognition of pedestrian’s intention to cross is presented as a step towards autonomous driving. Our approach is constructed using a real-Time visual pedestrian tracker based on a one-stage object detector that gave a fast and robust learning produces dynamic and static features as inputs of a linear classifier for predicting crossing behavior. We apply our approach to public real-life benchmark - JAAD dataset "Joint Attention in Autonomous Driving." The experimental results have shown that our proposed method is efficient and competitive with other approaches. Overall, our system achieves an accuracy of 92.88%.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶中实时识别行人过街意图，以提升道路场景理解与行车安全。  
**[核心方法]**：基于单阶段目标检测器（one-stage object detector）的实时视觉行人跟踪器，提取动态与静态特征，输入线性分类器进行过街行为预测。  
**[创新与效果]**：在JAAD公开数据集上实现92.88%的准确率，系统高效且具备竞争性。

### 4. [Refined Mixing Strategy for Driver Safety Assistance through Predictive Pedestrian Trajectory Modeling](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCE-Taiwan66881.2025.11207823)
`相关度 85` · 行人轨迹预测，强相关

*IEEE International Conference on Consumer Electronics* | Jing-Tai Chang, Feng-Cheng Lin | 2025-07-16

**TLDR:** 无

> Improving urban transportation safety by preventing pedestrian-vehicle collisions and minimizing traffic disruptions critically depends on accurate pedestrian trajectory prediction. However, many existing methodologies are constrained by their reliance on static, bird's-eye perspectives from surveillance cameras, which diminishes their effectiveness in dynamic, vehicle-mounted scenarios. To overcome these limitations, this study proposes an innovative predictive pedestrian warning system engineered for in-vehicle use. Our system uniquely integrates real-time detection and tracking with advanced trajectory generation and employs fuzzy logic for nuanced dynamic risk assessment. It is designed to dynamically anticipate future pedestrian movements, enabling sophisticated risk evaluations and the delivery of graded alerts, particularly in critical situations such as abrupt road crossings or obscured blind spots. By leveraging an onboard camera for egocentric data capture, the system performs continuous real-time analysis, thereby boosting predictive accuracy and mitigating challenges posed by self-motion. The developed approach demonstrably enhances pedestrian intent recognition accuracy and significantly improves the dependability of safety measures across varied urban environments.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有行人轨迹预测方法依赖静态鸟瞰视角，在车载动态场景中准确性和可靠性不足。
**[核心方法]**：基于车载摄像头获取自我中心（egocentric）数据，集成实时检测与跟踪、轨迹生成，并采用模糊逻辑进行动态风险评估，实现分级预警。
**[创新与效果]**：在动态城市环境中，显著提升了行人意图识别准确性和安全措施的可靠性，解决了自运动带来的预测挑战。

### 5. [Lessons learned from pedestrian-driver communication and yielding patterns](https://doi.org/10.1016/J.TRF.2021.03.011)
`相关度 85` · 行人-驾驶员沟通模式

*未知会议/期刊* | X. Kong, Subasish Das, Yunlong Zhang, Xiao Xiao | 2021-05-01

**TLDR:** 无

> Abstract Understanding the hidden patterns of tacit communication between drivers and pedestrians is crucial for improving pedestrian safety. However, this type of communication is a result of the psychological processes of both pedestrians and drivers, which are very difficult to understand thoroughly. This study utilizes a naturalistic field study dataset and explores the hidden patterns from successful and failed communication events using a pattern recognition method known as Taxicab Correspondence Analysis (TCA). The successful communication scenarios indicate the combinations of variable attributes such as eye contact, facial expression, the assertion of crossing, and effective traffic control devices are strongly associated with successful scenarios. The patterns for failed scenarios are most likely to be on the roadway with a relatively higher speed limit (e.g., 35 mph) and a relatively lower speed limit (e.g., 15 mph) under different conditions. On roadways with a higher speed limit, the failed scenarios are highly associated with passive and undecisive pedestrians, pedestrians far away from the crosswalk, regardless of pedestrian-driver eye contact and facial expression of the pedestrians. Instead of waiting for pedestrians to making a crossing decision, overspeeding drivers are more likely to speed up and pass the crosswalk. On roadways with a lower speed limit, the failed scenarios are often associated with distracted pedestrians, vehicles having the right of way, and the absence of effective traffic control devices. These findings could help transportation agencies identify appropriate countermeasures to reduce pedestrian crashes. The findings on driver-pedestrian communication patterns could provide scopes for improvement in computer vision-based algorithms designed for autonomous vehicle industries.

**[试图解决的问题]**：行人-驾驶员之间隐性沟通（如眼神、表情）的失败模式难以识别，导致行人安全问题。
**[核心方法]**：基于自然驾驶数据集，采用Taxicab Correspondence Analysis (TCA) 模式识别方法，分析成功与失败的沟通事件。
**[创新与效果]**：发现成功场景与眼神接触、面部表情、过街决心及有效交通控制设备强相关；失败场景在高限速（35 mph）道路与被动/犹豫行人、远离人行横道有关（驾驶员超速），低限速（15 mph）道路与分心行人、车辆有路权、无有效控制设备有关。可为交通部门制定对策及自动驾驶视觉算法改进提供依据。
