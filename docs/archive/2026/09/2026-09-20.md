### 1. [Student Research Track: Observing School-Age Child Group Crossings from Bird's-Eye View for Behavioural Research](https://doi.org/10.1145/3828158.3834808)
`相关度 95` · 学龄儿童过街行为直接研究

*未知会议/期刊* | S. Schwertner, Alice Rollwagen, Andreas Riener | 2026-09-20

**TLDR:** 无

> Autonomous vehicles need to respond safely to pedestrians, yet the behavior of school-age children remains difficult to characterize and is only weakly represented in many datasets and prediction-oriented research streams. Prior work has shown that children differ from adults in attention, timing, and decision-making during street crossing, and that current pedestrian detection systems can also perform worse on children than on adults. At the same time, much of the existing literature on pedestrian intention prediction focuses on adult pedestrians, egocentric traffic scenes, or individual behavior rather than small child groups. This work-in-progress examines how school-age child group crossings can be described from drone-based bird’s-eye-view recordings for qualitative behavioural research in automated driving contexts. Based on a controlled field study, the paper presents a structured observational account of single-directional and bidirectional child crossing scenes, focusing on waiting, scanning, initiation, following, cohesion, and lateral movement patterns. Across scenes, crossings often appeared to be socially organized, with a small number of children initiating movement while others followed with limited visible safety checks. Rather than proposing a predictive model, the paper offers an initial descriptive basis for future work on underrepresented pedestrian behaviour, trajectory analysis, and child-centered modelling questions.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶需安全应对行人，但学龄儿童的行人行为难以刻画，在数据集中表征薄弱，且现有行人检测系统对儿童的性能劣于成人；既有意图预测研究多聚焦成人、自我中心视角或个体行为，缺乏对儿童小群体的描述性观察。

**[核心方法]**：基于受控实地实验，利用无人机俯视视角录像，对儿童群体过街场景（单向与双向穿越）进行结构化观察记录，分析等待、扫视、启动、跟随、群体凝聚力和横向运动模式。

**[创新与效果]**：发现过街行为常呈社会性组织——少数儿童发起移动，其余儿童跟随且较少进行可见的安全检查；虽未提出预测模型，但为弱势行人行为表征、轨迹分析及以儿童为中心的建模提供了初步描述性基础。

### 2. [CF-LSTM: Cascaded Feature-Based Long Short-Term Networks for Predicting Pedestrian Trajectory](https://doi.org/10.1609/AAAI.V34I07.6943)
`相关度 94` · 级联特征LSTM行人轨迹预测

*AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence* | Yi Xu, Jing Yang, S. Du | 2020-04-03

**TLDR:** 无

> Pedestrian trajectory prediction is an important but difficult task in self-driving or autonomous mobile robot field because there are complex unpredictable human-human interactions in crowded scenarios. There have been a large number of studies that attempt to understand humans' social behavior. However, most of these studies extract location features from previous one time step while neglecting the vital velocity features. In order to address this issue, we propose a novel feature-cascaded framework for long short-term network (CF-LSTM) without extra artificial settings or social rules. In this framework, feature information from previous two time steps are firstly extracted and then integrated as a cascaded feature to LSTM, which is able to capture the previous location information and dynamic velocity information, simultaneously. In addition, this scene-agnostic cascaded feature is the external manifestation of complex human-human interactions, which can also effectively capture dynamic interaction information in different scenes without any other pedestrians' information. Experiments on public benchmark datasets indicate that our model achieves better performance than the state-of-the-art methods and this feature-cascaded framework has the ability to implicitly learn human-human interactions.

**[试图解决的问题]**：拥挤场景下行人轨迹预测中，现有方法多仅利用上一时刻的位置特征，忽视了速度特征，难以刻画复杂人际交互。

**[核心方法]**：提出特征级联的长短期记忆网络框架（CF-LSTM），提取前两个时间步的特征并级联为融合特征输入LSTM，同时捕捉位置信息与动态速度信息；该场景无关的级联特征可隐式表达人际交互，无需人工规则或他人轨迹信息。

**[创新与效果]**：在公开基准数据集上超越现有最优方法，验证了级联特征框架能隐式学习行人交互并提升轨迹预测精度。

### 3. [SFMGNet: A Physics-Based Neural Network To Predict Pedestrian Trajectories](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d53d8e7b0283d2de9483417652c9507f26d80d02)
`相关度 93` · 物理神经网络预测行人轨迹

*Make* | Sakif Hossain, F. Johora, ..., Sven Hartmann, Andreas Reinhardt | 2022-02-06

**TLDR:** 无

> Autonomous robots and vehicles are expected to soon become an integral part of our environment. Unsatisfactory issues regarding interaction with existing road users, performance in mixed-traffic areas and lack of interpretable behavior remain key obstacles. To address these, we present a physics-based neural network, based on a hybrid approach combining a social force model extended by group force (SFMG) with Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) to predict pedestrian trajectories considering its interaction with static obstacles, other pedestrians and pedestrian groups. We quantitatively and qualitatively evaluate the model with respect to realistic prediction, prediction performance and prediction"interpretability". Initial results suggest, the model even when solely trained on a synthetic dataset, can predict realistic and interpretable trajectories with better than state-of-the-art accuracy.

**[试图解决的问题]**：传统行人轨迹预测在混合交通场景下与现有道路使用者的交互不足、可解释性差。

**[创新与效果]**：该模型即使在仅使用合成数据集训练时，也能生成真实且可解释的轨迹，预测精度优于现有最先进方法（state-of-the-art）。

### 4. [Trajectory Prediction for Robot Navigation using Flow-Guided Markov Neural Operator](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICRA57147.2024.10611154)
`相关度 92` · 机器人导航行人轨迹预测直接相关

*IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation* | Rashmi Bhaskara, Hrishikesh Viswanath, Aniket Bera | 2023-09-17

**TLDR:** 无

> Predicting pedestrian movements remains a complex and persistent challenge in robot navigation research. We must evaluate several factors to achieve accurate predictions, such as pedestrian interactions, the environment, crowd density, and social and cultural norms. Accurate prediction of pedestrian paths is vital for ensuring safe human-robot interaction, especially in robot navigation. Furthermore, this research has potential applications in autonomous vehicles, pedestrian tracking, and human-robot collaboration. Therefore, in this paper, we introduce FlowMNO, an Optical Flow-Integrated Markov Neural Operator designed to capture pedestrian behavior across diverse scenarios. Our paper models trajectory prediction as a Markovian process, where future pedestrian coordinates depend solely on the current state. This problem formulation eliminates the need to store previous states. We conducted experiments using standard benchmark datasets like ETH, HOTEL, ZARA1, ZARA2, UCY, and RGB-D pedestrian datasets. Our study demonstrates that FlowMNO outperforms some of the state-of-the-art deep learning methods like LSTM, GAN, and CNN-based approaches, by approximately 86.46% when predicting pedestrian trajectories. Thus, we show that FlowMNO can seamlessly integrate into robot navigation systems, enhancing their ability to navigate crowded areas smoothly.

**[试图解决的问题]**：机器人导航中行人轨迹预测需综合考虑交互、环境、人群密度及社会文化规范等因素，现有深度学习方法在拥挤动态场景下预测精度不足，威胁人机交互安全。

**[核心方法]**：提出 FlowMNO（Optical Flow-Integrated Markov Neural Operator），将轨迹预测建模为马尔可夫过程，即未来坐标仅依赖当前状态，免去历史状态存储；利用光流（Optical Flow）引导的马尔可夫神经算子捕捉行人运动特征，并在 ETH、HOTEL、ZARA1、ZARA2、UCY 与 RGB-D 行人数据集上验证。

**[创新与效果]**：相比 LSTM、GAN、CNN 等现有深度学习方法，FlowMNO 在行人轨迹预测任务上性能提升约 86.46%，可无缝集成到机器人导航系统中，增强其在拥挤区域的安全导航能力。

### 5. [Discovering and Understanding Algorithmic Biases in Autonomous Pedestrian Trajectory Predictions](https://doi.org/10.1145/3560905.3568433)
`相关度 92` · 自动驾驶行人轨迹预测偏差研究

*ACM International Conference on Embedded Networked Sensor Systems* | Andrew Bae, Susu Xu | 2022-11-06

**TLDR:** 无

> Pedestrian trajectory prediction is an important module in autonomous vehicles (AVs) to ensure safe and effective motion planning. Recently, many deep learning algorithms that achieve near real-time trajectory predictions have been developed. However, people in the artificial intelligence (AI) ethics community have raised critical concerns about the bias and fairness of many general deep learning algorithms. For example, most pedestrian trajectory data is collected from majority populations, and models learned from this data may not generalize well to the heterogeneous needs and behavior patterns of different pedestrian groups, especially for vulnerable pedestrians like the disabled, the elderly, and children. Biases present in trajectory prediction algorithms could mean that pedestrians from certain vulnerable demographics are more likely to be involved in vehicle crashes. In this work, we test two state-of-the-art pedestrian trajectory prediction models for age and gender biases across three different datasets. We design and utilize novel evaluation metrics for comparing model performance. We find that both models perform worse on children and the elderly compared to adults. However, their performance is similar between men and women. We identify potential sources of these biases, as well as discuss several limitations of our study. Our future work will consist of testing more models, refining our evaluation metrics, further differentiating the dataset bias from the algorithmic bias, and mitigating the algorithmic biases.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶中的行人轨迹预测模型可能因训练数据以多数人群为主，而对儿童、老年人、残障者等弱势行人的行为模式泛化不足，导致其更易卷入碰撞事故；现有研究缺乏对轨迹预测算法在年龄、性别维度偏差的系统性测试。

**[核心方法]**：选取两个最先进的行人轨迹预测深度学习模型，在三个公开数据集上，设计并使用新的评估指标，系统比较模型对不同年龄组（儿童、成年人、老年人）与不同性别行人的预测性能，并进一步分析偏差来源、区分数据偏差与算法偏差。

**[创新与效果]**：首次在行人轨迹预测任务中量化了年龄与性别偏差：两个模型对儿童和老年人的预测性能均显著差于成年人，而对男女性别的预测性能无显著差异；定位了偏差的可能来源，并提出了后续缓解算法偏差、完善评估指标的方向。
