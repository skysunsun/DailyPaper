### 1. [Real-Time Pedestrian Crossing Intent Prediction and Risk Assessment Framework Using Skeleton Graph Convolutional Networks](https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics15184106)
`相关度 95` · 直接研究行人过街意图识别与风险评估

*Electronics* | Yi-Xuan Deng, C. Sub-r-pa, Rung-Ching Chen | 2026-09-10

**TLDR:** 无

> Pedestrian safety at urban intersections remains a major challenge in Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITSs). This study investigates whether crossing intention can be reliably inferred directly from temporal body-pose dynamics to drive real-time collision warnings on embedded edge platforms. Existing vision-based approaches that rely primarily on bounding-box proximity or scene-level spatial grids are often prone to false alarms in complex urban environments with motorcycles, stationary pedestrians, and background clutter. To overcome these limitations, we propose an end-to-end framework consisting of four sequential processing stages: (1) a perception layer integrating YOLOv8s, ByteTrack, a displacement filter, and rider suppression to generate reliable pedestrian trajectories; (2) a skeleton extraction layer utilizing YOLOv8s-pose to construct temporal sequences of 17 anatomical keypoints; (3) an ultra-lightweight Skeleton Graph Convolutional Network (SkeletonGCN, comprising 33.8 K parameters, <0.2 MB) that models body-joint kinematics and temporal motion dynamics; and (4) an image-space Time-to-Collision (TTC) risk-fusion module. While this fusion approach avoids explicit geometric camera calibration, it still relies on predefined scene-profile parameters and image-space motion assumptions. Furthermore, while the intention classifier is quantitatively evaluated, the risk-fusion module is procedurally defined, and its resulting four-level collision warnings are demonstrated operationally rather than validated against ground-truth hazard annotations. Evaluated on 49,948 valid sequences from the JAAD and PIE benchmark datasets under a strict video-level partitioning protocol, the unified SkeletonGCN achieves a macro-F1 score of 0.717 (with per-scene subset macro-F1 scores of 0.761 on JAAD/PIE urban and 0.895 on intersections), significantly outperforming baseline models. When deployed on an NVIDIA Jetson Orin NX edge device using TensorRT FP16, the full pipeline achieves an instrumented latency of 70.7 ms per frame (~14 fps) and a sustained wall-clock throughput of 7.4 fps on real-world urban dashcam video. System limitations include sensitivity to 2D printed human imagery and reduced prediction reliability under low-light nighttime conditions.

**[试图解决的问题]**：城市路口行人安全预警中，现有基于边界框接近度或场景网格的视觉方法在摩托车、静止行人及背景杂乱场景下易误报；需在边缘嵌入式平台低延迟条件下，直接从人体姿态时序可靠推断过街意图。

**[核心方法]**：提出四阶段端到端框架：①感知层用YOLOv8s+ByteTrack+位移过滤器+骑行者抑制生成可靠行人轨迹；②骨架提取层用YOLOv8s-pose构建17个解剖关键点的时序序列；③超轻量SkeletonGCN（33.8K参数，<0.2MB）建模骨骼关节运动与时间动态；④图像空间TTC风险融合模块，避免显式相机标定，输出四级碰撞预警。部署采用TensorRT FP16。

**[创新与效果]**：在JAAD/PIE数据集严格视频级划分下，49,948个有效序列上macro-F1达0.717；其中JAAD/PIE urban场景子集macro-F1=0.761，intersections场景子集=0.895，显著优于基线模型。在NVIDIA Jetson Orin NX上全流水线延迟70.7 ms/帧（~14 fps），实际墙钟吞吐7.4 fps。局限包括对2D打印人像敏感、低光夜间可靠性下降；风险融合模块为流程式定义，未用地面真值危险标注验证。

### 2. [Efficient Behavior-aware Control of Automated Vehicles at Crosswalks using Minimal Information Pedestrian Prediction Model](https://doi.org/10.23919/ACC45564.2020.9147248)
`相关度 95` · 过街行为预测用于自动驾驶控制，直接相关

*American Control Conference* | Suresh Kumaar Jayaraman, L. Robert, ..., A. Pradhan, D. Tilbury | 2020-03-08

**TLDR:** 无

> For automated vehicles (AVs) to reliably navigate through crosswalks, they need to understand pedestrians’ crossing behaviors. Simple and reliable pedestrian behavior models aid in real-time AV control by allowing the AVs to predict future pedestrian behaviors. In this paper, we present a Behavior-aware Model Predictive Controller (B-MPC) for AVs that incorporates long-term predictions of pedestrian crossing behavior using a previously developed pedestrian crossing model. The model incorporates pedestrians’ gap acceptance behavior and utilizes minimal pedestrian information, namely their position and speed, to predict pedestrians’ crossing behaviors. The B-MPC controller is validated through simulations and compared to a rule-based controller. By incorporating predictions of pedestrian behavior, the B-MPC controller is able to efficiently plan for longer horizons and handle a wider range of pedestrian interaction scenarios than the rule-based controller. Results demonstrate the applicability of the controller for safe and efficient navigation at crossing scenarios.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶车辆（AVs）在人行横道处需理解行人过街行为，而现有控制器难以在仅凭最小行人信息下实现实时、长时域的安全高效控制。

**[核心方法]**：提出行为感知模型预测控制器（B-MPC），将行人过街模型嵌入MPC中；该模型利用行人位置和速度作为最小输入，并建模行人间隙接受行为，以提供行人过街行为的长期预测。

**[创新与效果]**：相比基于规则的控制器，B-MPC能利用预测信息规划更长时域，处理更多样化的行人交互场景；仿真验证其在人行横道场景中实现安全且高效的导航控制。

### 3. [LSTM-based Pedestrian Trajectory Prediction Model under the General Direction Mechanism: DIR-LSTM](https://doi.org/10.1145/3641343.3641438)
`相关度 90` · 行人轨迹预测，直接相关

*ICEITSA* | Jun-Hong Luo, Qiongfang Fu, ..., Hongbo Xiao, Jiyong Liao | 2023-12-08

**TLDR:** 无

> Accurate prediction of future traffic flow trends is essential to solve urban transportation problems. However, traffic flow prediction faces great challenges due to the multimodal nature of pedestrian behavior and the complexity of the traffic environment. Although a large number of studies have been conducted to investigate these issues in depth, there are still some limitations. In order to address these challenges more effectively, we propose a pedestrian trajectory prediction model based on long-short-term memory networks (LSTMs): the DIR-LSTM. The model introduces an innovative generalized direction mechanism and a self-attention mechanism, which captures pedestrian movement patterns more comprehensively and accurately by predicting overall directional movements first and then gradually subdividing them into individual directions of movement. The DIR-LSTM is designed to address the challenges posed by the diversity of pedestrian behaviors and the complexity of urban environments. To validate the state-of-the-art of the model, we conducted experiments using the publicly available ETH [10] and UCY [9] datasets. The experiments demonstrate that DIR-LSTM performs better in terms of accuracy compared to other models, providing a more reliable prediction tool for future urban traffic management.

**[试图解决的问题]**：行人行为多模态性与交通环境复杂性导致未来交通流趋势预测精度不足，现有模型仍有局限。

**[核心方法]**：提出基于长短期记忆网络的 DIR-LSTM 模型，引入广义方向机制与自注意力机制；先预测整体方向运动，再逐步细分为个体运动方向，以捕捉行人运动模式。

**[创新与效果]**：在 ETH 和 UCY 公开数据集上验证，相比其他模型取得更高预测精度，为城市交通管理提供更可靠的预测工具。

### 4. [Transformer-Based Multi-Modal Probabilistic Pedestrian Prediction for Risk-Aware Autonomous Vehicle Navigation](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICAR58858.2023.10436505)
`相关度 90` · 自动驾驶中多模态行人预测，强相关

*International Conference on Advanced Robotics* | Murad Mebrahtu, Awet Araia, ..., Jorge Dias, Majid Khonji | 2023-12-05

**TLDR:** 无

> Over the past decade, the field of assisted and autonomous driving has experienced significant advancements. However, autonomous driving systems are still challenged by the complexities of dynamic urban environments, especially when it comes to predicting and responding to the often stochastic behavior of pedestrians. Current approaches largely concentrate on most likely predictions but tend to ignore their inherent probabilistic nature. Our research introduces a novel Transformer-based multimodal probabilistic prediction model that utilizes a Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM). This approach is simpler than its predecessors, yet it maintains competitive performance, capable of inferring prediction uncertainties using GMM parameters. Additionally, we demonstrate how our prediction model can be incorporated into a risk-aware behavior planner, based on the Chance-Constrained Stochastic Shortest Path (CC-SSP) framework. This planner uses probabilistic trajectory predictions as a Markov transition function to modulate the speed of the autonomous vehicle, effectively keeping the probability of collision below a defined threshold. Our implementation is available at https://github.com/Murdism/Probabilistic_Pedestrian_Trajectory_Prediction-PPTP.git.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶系统在动态城市环境中对行人随机行为的预测不足，现有方法多聚焦于最可能预测而忽略其内在概率分布。

**[核心方法]**：提出一种基于Transformer的多模态概率预测模型，结合高斯混合模型（GMM）进行轨迹分布建模；并将其嵌入基于机会约束随机最短路径（CC-SSP）的risk-aware行为规划器中，利用概率预测作为马尔可夫转移函数调节自车速度。

**[创新与效果]**：模型结构较先前方法更简洁且性能相当，能直接利用GMM参数推断预测不确定性；规划器可根据预测分布将碰撞概率控制在设定阈值以下，实现风险感知的自主车速调控。

### 5. [Enhanced Pedestrian Trajectory Prediction via the Cross-Modal Feature Fusion Transformer](https://doi.org/10.1109/VCIP59821.2023.10402669)
`相关度 90` · 行人轨迹预测方法，直接相关

*Visual Communications and Image Processing* | Rashid Ali, Hsu-Feng Hsiao | 2023-12-04

**TLDR:** 无

> We address the challenge of predicting pedestrian trajectories in videos, a task inherently complex due to the diverse and intricate nature of human motion and interactions within their environment. The accurate anticipation of trajectories necessitates a holistic comprehension of the temporal evolution of past events in videos. Regrettably, existing methods often neglect the fusion of critical features, such as human behavior, motion, and interaction, thereby limiting their efficacy in tackling these challenges. To overcome these limitations, we propose the Cross-modal Feature Fusion Transformer, a novel approach for pedestrian trajectory prediction. Our model seamlessly integrates multimodal features, including human behavior, position, speed, and interaction with surroundings, to effectively encapsulate the temporal progression of observed frames. It consists of transformer-based cross-modal fusion encoder and decoder modules, adeptly melding the interactions between the multimodal features through a multi-head co-attentional mechanism. This enables the precise prediction of future trajectories. Additionally, we incorporate auxiliary self-supervised future prediction losses to learn the temporal evolution of past and future multimodal features. We evaluate our approach on ETH/UCY and ActEV/VIRAT datasets and demonstrate its superior performance compared to state-of-the-art methods.

**[试图解决的问题]**: 视频中行人轨迹预测因需融合行为、运动、交互等多模态特征而复杂，现有方法常忽略关键特征融合，导致预测精度受限。

**[核心方法]**: 提出 Cross-modal Feature Fusion Transformer，由基于Transformer的跨模态融合编码器与解码器构成，通过多头共注意力机制融合行人行为、位置、速度及环境交互等模态特征，并引入辅助自监督未来预测损失以学习时序演化。

**[创新与效果]**: 在ETH/UCY和ActEV/VIRAT数据集上超越现有最先进方法，验证了跨模态特征融合与自监督时序建模的有效性。
