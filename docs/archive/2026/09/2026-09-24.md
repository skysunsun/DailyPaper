### 1. [Learning a Simple Directional eHMI: A VR Study of AV-Pedestrian Interaction](https://doi.org/10.1145/3828158.3838268)
`相关度 95` · 直接研究eHMI对行人过街行为影响

*Adjunct Proceedings of the 18th International Conference on Automotive User Interfaces and Interactive Vehicular Applications* | C. Janssen, Md Shadab Alam, ..., P. Bazilinskyy, Ling-Han Zhang | 2026-09-19

**TLDR:** Results suggest that the eHMI supported earlier crossing initiation and increased perceived safety and trust, and indicate that the signal is learnable in a controlled dyadic setting and motivate future VR studies of multi-pedestrian interaction, social influence, and traffic complexity.

> External Human-Machine Interfaces (eHMIs) for automated vehicles must remain quickly interpretable if they are to scale to realistic traffic. This study examines a deliberately simple directional light-based windshield eHMI as a first step toward that goal. The eHMI uses a minimal light strip whose highlighted segment shifts based on the pedestrian’s relative position, aiming to convey yielding intent without text or complex visual elements. In a virtual-reality study with 30 participants, pedestrians encountered an automated vehicle with and without the eHMI; half received a brief onboarding video, while the others learnt through repeated exposure. Results suggest that the eHMI supported earlier crossing initiation and increased perceived safety and trust. Onboarding produced immediate benefits, whereas participants without onboarding showed gradual improvements over repeated encounters. These findings indicate that the signal is learnable in a controlled dyadic setting and motivate future VR studies of multi-pedestrian interaction, social influence, and traffic complexity.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶车辆与行人交互中，外部人机界面（eHMI）必须快速可解读，才能扩展到真实交通场景。

**[核心方法]**：设计了一种极简的基于方向光的挡风玻璃 eHMI，即一条光条，其高亮段随行人相对位置移动，用于传达让行意图，不含文本或复杂视觉元素；在 30 人 VR 实验中对比有无 eHMI 的条件，其中一半受试者观看简短入门视频，另一半通过重复暴露自然学习。

**[创新与效果]**：该 eHMI 支持行人更早开始过马路，并提高了感知安全感和信任度；入门视频带来即时收益，无入门视频的参与者随重复遭遇逐步改善；结果表明该信号在受控双人场景中可学习，并为后续多行人交互、社会影响和交通复杂度的 VR 研究奠定基础。

### 2. [Pedestrian Trajectory Prediction Based on Dual Social Graph Attention Network](https://doi.org/10.3390/app15084285)
`相关度 95` · 直接研究行人轨迹预测方法

*Applied Sciences* | Xinhai Li, Yong Liang, Zhenhao Yang, Jie Li | 2025-04-13

**TLDR:** A novel dual social graph attention network (DSGAT) that systematically models multi-level interactions is proposed that consistently surpasses the baseline methods across the key evaluation metrics, including the Average Displacement Error and Final Displacement Error.

> Pedestrian trajectory prediction poses significant challenges for autonomous systems due to the intricate nature of social interactions in densely populated environments. While the existing methods frequently encounter difficulties in effectively quantifying the nuanced social relationships, we propose a novel dual social graph attention network (DSGAT) that systematically models multi-level interactions. This framework is specifically designed to enhance the extraction of pedestrian interaction features within the environment, thereby improving the trajectory prediction accuracy. The network architecture consists of two primary branches, namely an individual branch and a group branch, which are responsible for modeling personal and collective pedestrian behaviors, respectively. For individual feature modeling, we propose the Spatio-Temporal Weighted Graph Attention Network (STWGAT) branch, which incorporates a newly developed directed social attention function to explicitly capture both the direction and intensity of pedestrian interactions. This mechanism enables the model to more effectively represent the fine-grained social dynamics. Subsequently, leveraging the STWGAT’s processing of directed weighted graphs, the network’s ability to aggregate spatiotemporal information and refine individual interaction representations is further strengthened. To effectively account for the critical group dynamics, a dedicated group attention function is designed to identify and quantify the collective behaviors within pedestrian crowds. This facilitates a more comprehensive understanding of the complex social interactions, leading to an enhanced trajectory prediction accuracy. Extensive comparative experiments conducted on the widely used ETH and UCY benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed network consistently surpasses the baseline methods across the key evaluation metrics, including the Average Displacement Error (ADE) and Final Displacement Error (FDE). These results confirm the effectiveness and robustness of the DSGAT-based approach in handling complex pedestrian interaction scenarios.

**[试图解决的问题]**：密集行人场景中现有轨迹预测方法难以有效量化细粒度社会交互（交互方向与强度），导致预测精度受限。

**[核心方法]**：提出双社交图注意力网络（DSGAT），包含个体分支与群体分支。个体分支采用时空加权图注意力网络（STWGAT），通过有向社交注意力函数显式建模交互方向与强度，并在有向加权图上聚合时空信息以细化个体交互表征；群体分支设计群体注意力函数，识别并量化人群中的集体行为。

**[创新与效果]**：在ETH和UCY基准上，DSGAT在平均位移误差（ADE）和最终位移误差（FDE）上一致超过基线方法，验证了对复杂行人交互场景建模的有效性与鲁棒性。

### 3. [DiffWT: Diffusion-Based Pedestrian Trajectory Prediction With Time-Frequency Wavelet Transform](https://doi.org/10.1109/JIOT.2024.3489638)
`相关度 95` · 直接研究行人轨迹预测

*IEEE Internet of Things Journal* | Xin Chen, Li Zeng, ..., Chi Ding, Yougang Bian | 2025-03-01

**TLDR:** 无

> Accurate pedestrian trajectory prediction is a crucial task for ensuring the safety of autonomous driving. However, most of the existing methods only model pedestrian trajectories in the spatial-temporal domain, which results in a lack of analysis of motion at different scales. In this work, we propose a framework based on wavelet transform and diffusion model, which is called DiffWT. Different from previous approaches, our method employs a discrete wavelet transform (DWT) to perform time-frequency analysis of trajectories. The high-frequency component of the DWT indicates local motion details of the trajectory, while the low-frequency one represents overall motion trends. Second, we propose a trajectory decoder comprising a conditional diffusion model and a cross-constrained bidirectional trajectory generator (C2Bid). The diffusion model generates the distribution of implicit pedestrian behaviors by taking the multiscale motion behaviors as conditions. Furthermore, the C2Bid module is designed as a cross-constrained bidirectional structure to decode behavioral distribution into multimodal trajectories. This trajectory decoder can generate precise distribution of trajectories and reduce accumulation of prediction errors. Extensive experimental results on the ETH/UCY and the Stanford drone datasets (SDDs) demonstrate that our method achieves better performance as well as higher efficiency compared to other state-of-the-art approaches.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有行人轨迹预测方法仅在时空域建模，缺乏对不同尺度运动（局部细节与整体趋势）的分析，且预测误差易累积。

**[核心方法]**：提出 DiffWT 框架，使用离散小波变换（DWT）对轨迹进行时频分析（高频表征局部运动细节，低频表征整体运动趋势）；轨迹解码器由条件扩散模型和跨约束双向轨迹生成器（C2Bid）组成，以多尺度运动行为为条件生成隐式行为分布，并通过 C2Bid 的跨约束双向结构解码为多模态轨迹。

**[创新与效果]**：引入小波时频分析结合扩散模型实现多尺度运动建模，C2Bid 双向解码减少误差累积；在 ETH/UCY 和 Stanford Drone 数据集上取得优于现有 SOTA 方法的性能，且效率更高。

### 4. [Crosswalk Cooperation: A Phone-Integrated Driver-Vehicle Cooperation Approach to Predict the Crossing Intentions of Pedestrians in Automated Driving](https://doi.org/10.1145/3409251.3411727)
`相关度 95` · 直接预测行人过街意图

*International Conference on Automotive User Interfaces and Interactive Vehicular Applications* | Marcel Walch, Stacey Li, ..., Natalie Friedman, Wendy Ju | 2020-09-21

**TLDR:** How passengers can be used to disambiguate pedestrian crossing behavior by relying on the users’ interpretation of the situation to clarify ambiguity is explored.

> While the implementation of automated driving under well-defined circumstances such as highways is possible today, more complex and dynamic environments such as urban areas remain challenging. In particular, the behavior prediction of other road users like pedestrians can be difficult for automated vehicles. We suggest a cooperative approach to overcome this system limitation. More specifically, the system can rely on the users’ interpretation of the situation to clarify ambiguity. This study explores how passengers can be used to disambiguate pedestrian crossing behavior. Because passengers likely use their phones while traveling in an automated vehicle, we displayed a live view of the traffic scenes as well as a cooperation request to ask about the intentions of the pedestrian at the crosswalk. A preliminary evaluation of usability shows that this approach provides promising results. Participants also reported trusting the system and showed willingness to help an automated vehicle.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶在城市复杂动态环境中对行人过街意图的预测存在歧义，难以可靠判断。

**[核心方法]**：提出一种手机集成的驾驶员-车辆协作方法：将交通场景实时画面推送至乘客手机，同时发送协作请求，要求乘客基于自身对情境的理解判断人行横道处行人的过街意图，从而消除系统预测的不确定性。

**[创新与效果]**：利用乘客作为“情境解释者”辅助自动驾驶消解行人行为歧义；初步可用性评估显示该方法可行，参与者信任该系统并愿意协助自动驾驶车辆。

### 5. [How Do eHMIs Affect Pedestrians' Crossing Behavior? A Study Using a Head-Mounted Display Combined with a Motion Suit](https://doi.org/10.3390/info10120386)
`相关度 95` · 直接研究eHMI对行人过街行为影响

*Inf.* | L. Kooijman, R. Happee, J. D. de Winter | 2019-12-06

**TLDR:** Results obtained via a setup in which participants can cross the road are similar to results from survey studies, with eHMIs yielding a higher crossing intention compared to no eHMI.

> In future trac, automated vehicles may be equipped with external human-machine interfaces (eHMIs) that can communicate with pedestrians. Previous research suggests that, during first encounters, pedestrians regard text-based eHMIs as clearer than light-based eHMIs. However, in much of the previous research, pedestrians were asked to imagine crossing the road, and unable or not allowed to do so. We investigated the effects of eHMIs on participants’ crossing behavior. Twenty-four participants were immersed in a virtual urban environment using a head-mounted display coupled to a motion-tracking suit. We manipulated the approaching vehicles’ behavior (yielding, nonyielding) and eHMI type (None, Text, Front Brake Lights). Participants could cross the road whenever they felt safe enough to do so. The results showed that forward walking velocities, as recorded at the pelvis, were, on average, higher when an eHMI was present compared to no eHMI if the vehicle yielded. In nonyielding conditions, participants showed a slight forward motion and refrained from crossing. An analysis of participants’ thorax angle indicated rotation towards the approaching vehicles and subsequent rotation towards the crossing path. It is concluded that results obtained via a setup in which participants can cross the road are similar to results from survey studies, with eHMIs yielding a higher crossing intention compared to no eHMI. The motion suit allows investigating pedestrian behaviors related to bodily attention and hesitation.

**[试图解决的问题]**：此前关于车外人机界面（eHMI）对行人过街影响的研究多基于问卷调查，行人只能想象过街而无法真实执行，缺乏基于实际过街行为的验证。

**[核心方法]**：使用头戴式显示器（HMD）结合动作追踪服构建沉浸式虚拟城市环境，操控接近车辆的行为（让行/不让行）与 eHMI 类型（无、文本、前刹车灯），让 24 名受试者在感觉安全时实际过街，记录骨盆处前向步行速度和胸廓旋转角度。

**[创新与效果]**：在车辆让行条件下，有 eHMI 时的骨盆前向步行速度平均高于无 eHMI；在不让行条件下，受试者仅轻微前移并放弃过街。胸廓角度分析显示受试者先转向接近车辆、再转向过街路径。该过街行为实验结果与既往调查研究一致，即 eHMI 相比无 eHMI 能提高过街意向；动作追踪服还可用于考察身体注意与犹豫相关的行人行为。
