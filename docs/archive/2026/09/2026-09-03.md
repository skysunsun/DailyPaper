**TLDR:** 无

### 2. [Context-Aware Pedestrian Motion Prediction In Urban Intersections](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/36702fbc5195a6bafc0aa62aaa2b084f80e59785)
`相关度 95` · 行人运动预测，强相关

*arXiv.org* | Golnaz Habibi, Nikita Jaipuria, J. How | 2018-06-25

**TLDR:** A novel context-based approach for pedestrian motion prediction in crowded, urban intersections, by incorporating semantic features from the environment in the GP formulation for more accurate predictions of pedestrian trajectories over the same timescale is presented.

> This paper presents a novel context-based approach for pedestrian motion prediction in crowded, urban intersections, with the additional flexibility of prediction in similar, but new, environments. Previously, Chen et. al. combined Markovian-based and clustering-based approaches to learn motion primitives in a grid-based world and subsequently predict pedestrian trajectories by modeling the transition between learned primitives as a Gaussian Process (GP). This work extends that prior approach by incorporating semantic features from the environment (relative distance to curbside and status of pedestrian traffic lights) in the GP formulation for more accurate predictions of pedestrian trajectories over the same timescale. We evaluate the new approach on real-world data collected using one of the vehicles in the MIT Mobility On Demand fleet. The results show 12.5% improvement in prediction accuracy and a 2.65 times reduction in Area Under the Curve (AUC), which is used as a metric to quantify the span of predicted set of trajectories, such that a lower AUC corresponds to a higher level of confidence in the future direction of pedestrian motion.

**[试图解决的问题]**: 在城市十字路口等拥挤环境中，现有行人轨迹预测方法未充分利用环境语义特征（如路缘距离、交通灯状态），导致预测精度和置信度不足。

**[核心方法]**: 在基于马尔可夫与聚类学习运动基元、并用高斯过程（GP）建模基元转移的框架基础上，将环境语义特征（相对于路缘的距离和行人交通灯状态）作为额外输入嵌入GP公式。

**[创新与效果]**: 在MIT Mobility on Demand车队真实数据上，预测准确率提升12.5%，轨迹集合的AUC（面积度量）降低2.65倍，即预测轨迹集更集中、对未来方向置信度更高。

### 3. [Requirements on Delay of VRU Context Detection for Cooperative Collision Avoidance](https://doi.org/10.1109/VTC2020-Fall49728.2020.9348627)
`相关度 80` · VRU碰撞避免检测延迟，强相关

*IEEE Vehicular Technology Conference* | Michel Morold, Quang-Huy Nguyen, ..., K. David, Falko Dressler | 2020-11-01

**TLDR:** 无

> Every year, approximately 350 000 vulnerable road users (VRUs) still lose their lives in road traffic accidents world-wide. To reduce this number, cooperative VRU collision avoidance systems aim at complementing current vehicle or infrastructure-based systems for VRU protection. The cooperative approach assumes user equipment (UE) like a smartphone or a smartwatch on the VRU side, which allows to obtain and exchange movement and contextual information with other road users for determining the current risk level for a collision. In recent publications, the usage of additional contextual information, such as the pedestrian’s current activity (e.g., standing, walking or crossing a curb), has been shown to improve the collision detection accuracy. However, those approaches focused on the detection accuracy, but did not investigate how time delays for both detection and communication of VRU contexts affect the ability to detect collisions. We fill this gap by investigating the influence of both activity recognition and communication delays on the collision detection performance. As a baseline, we use the standardized Euro NCAP test protocol. For the evaluation, we exemplarily consider the usage of a curb detection module for position correction, which improves the collision detection accuracy at the moment the pedestrian crosses the curb.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有协同VRU碰撞避免系统未考虑上下文检测（如活动识别）与通信延迟对碰撞检测性能的影响。  
**[核心方法]**：以Euro NCAP测试协议为基线，评估活动识别延迟与通信延迟对碰撞检测性能的影响，并示例性地引入路缘检测模块进行位置校正。  
**[创新与效果]**：填补了延迟影响研究的空白，揭示了延迟会降低碰撞检测能力；路缘检测模块可在行人跨越路缘时提升碰撞检测精度。

### 4. [Automated pedestrian safety analysis using data from traffic monitoring cameras](https://doi.org/10.1145/3236461.3241972)
`相关度 60` · 行人安全分析，含过街热点

*Proceedings of the 1st ACM/EIGSCC Symposium on Smart Cities and Communities* | Weijia Xu, Natalia Ruiz-Juri, ..., J. Duthie, J. Clary | 2018-06-20

**TLDR:** This work provides preliminary results based on data from cameras owned by the City of Austin, and discusses outputs such as pedestrian volume estimation and crossing hot-zones identification in the context of Smart Cities, and identifies potential challenges and limitations.

> Transportation agencies often own extensive networks of monocular traffic cameras, which are typically used for traffic monitoring. However, the information captured by such cameras can also be of great value for transportation planning and operations applications, particularly when large data sets may be systematically analyzed. In this paper, we propose an approach to use data collected by existing monitoring cameras to automatically identify locations where pedestrian safety may be a concern. Our methodology utilizes a convolutional-neural-network-based method to recognize pedestrians in traffic camera feeds. Results are stored and aggregated, and may be queried for further analyses. The proposed computational approach may leverage hardware such as GPUs and distributed computing clusters to enable the analysis of large volumes of data. The post recognition analysis utilizes unsupervised learning methods to identify the spatial and temporal patterns of pedestrian positions, which are then correlated to specific scenarios such as usage of crosswalk, compliance with traffic signals, and pedestrian-vehicle interactions. Applications include the identification of potential safety concerns, measuring the effectiveness of proposed safety strategies, and identifying the need for improvements. This work provides preliminary results based on data from cameras owned by the City of Austin. We also discuss outputs such as pedestrian volume estimation and crossing hot-zones identification in the context of Smart Cities, and identify potential challenges and limitations.

**[试图解决的问题]**：利用交通部门已有的单目监控摄像头拍摄的大量视频数据，自动识别行人安全潜在隐患区域，替代人工排查。

**[核心方法]**：基于卷积神经网络（CNN）的行人检测，结合无监督学习对检测结果进行时空聚类，分析行人位置模式并关联过街使用、信号灯遵守及人车交互等场景。

**[创新与效果]**：初步实现基于奥斯汀市真实监控数据的行人流量估计与过街热点识别，为智能城市中的安全策略评估和设施改进提供自动化分析框架。
