### 1. [MulCPred: Learning Multi-Modal Concepts for Explainable Pedestrian Action Prediction](https://doi.org/10.3390/s24206742)
`相关度 95` · 直接研究行人动作可解释预测

*Italian National Conference on Sensors* | Yan Feng, Alexander Carballo, ..., Ming Ding, K. Takeda | 2024-09-14

**TLDR:** Qualitative and quantitative results demonstrate that MulCPred is promising in improving the explainability of pedestrian action prediction without obvious performance degradation, and improved cross-dataset prediction performance, suggesting its potential for further generalization.

> Pedestrian action prediction is crucial for many applications such as autonomous driving. However, state-of-the-art methods lack the explainability needed for trustworthy predictions. In this paper, a novel framework called MulCPred is proposed that explains its predictions based on multi-modal concepts represented by training samples. Previous concept-based methods have limitations, including the following: (1) they cannot be directly applied to multi-modal cases; (2) they lack the locality needed to attend to details in the inputs; (3) they are susceptible to mode collapse. These limitations are tackled accordingly through the following approaches: (1) a linear aggregator to integrate the activation results of the concepts into predictions, which associates concepts of different modalities and provides ante hoc explanations of the relevance between the concepts and the predictions; (2) a channel-wise recalibration module that attends to local spatiotemporal regions, which enables the concepts with locality; (3) a feature regularization loss that encourages the concepts to learn diverse patterns. MulCPred is evaluated on multiple datasets and tasks. Both qualitative and quantitative results demonstrate that MulCPred is promising in improving the explainability of pedestrian action prediction without obvious performance degradation. Moreover, by removing unrecognizable concepts, MulCPred shows improved cross-dataset prediction performance, suggesting its potential for further generalization.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有行人动作预测方法缺乏可解释性，且已有概念学习方法无法直接处理多模态输入、缺乏局部注意力、易陷入模式崩溃。

**[核心方法]**：提出MulCPred框架，包含：(1) 线性聚合器，整合多模态概念激活结果并建立概念与预测间的先验解释；(2) 通道级重校准模块，关注局部时空区域以赋予概念局部性；(3) 特征正则化损失，促使概念学习多样化模式。

**[创新与效果]**：在多个数据集上实现可解释性提升且无明显性能下降；通过剔除不可识别概念，跨数据集预测性能得到改善，具备更好的泛化潜力。

### 2. [DyGraphformer: Transformer combining dynamic spatio-temporal graph network for multivariate time series forecasting](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neunet.2024.106776)
`相关度 50` · 动态时空图，交通相关但通用

*Neural Networks* | Shuo Han, Yaling Xun, ..., Haifeng Yang, Yanfeng Li | 2024-10-01

**TLDR:** DyGraphformer is proposed, which integrates graph convolution into Transformer to assist Transformer in effectively modeling spatial dependencies, while also dynamically inferring time-varying spatial dependencies by combining historical spatial information.

> Transformer-based models demonstrate tremendous potential for Multivariate Time Series (MTS) forecasting due to their ability to capture long-term temporal dependencies by using the self-attention mechanism. However, effectively modeling the spatial correlation cross series for MTS is a challenge for Transformer. Although Graph Neural Networks (GNN) are competent for modeling spatial dependencies across series, existing methods are based on the assumption of static relationships between variables, which do not align with the time-varying spatial dependencies in real-world series. Therefore, we propose DyGraphformer, which integrates graph convolution into Transformer to assist Transformer in effectively modeling spatial dependencies, while also dynamically inferring time-varying spatial dependencies by combining historical spatial information. In DyGraphformer, decoder module involving complex recursion is abandoned to accelerate model execution. First, the input is embedded using DSW (Dimension Segment Wise) through integrating its position and node level embedding to preserve temporal and spatial information. Then, the time self-attention layer and dynamic graph convolutional layer are constructed to capture temporal dependencies and spatial dependencies of multivariate time series, respectively. The dynamic graph convolutional layer utilizes Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) to obtain historical spatial dependencies, and integrates the series features of the current time to perform graph structure inference in multiple subspaces. Specifically, to fully utilize the spatio-temporal information at different scales, DyGraphformer performs hierarchical encoder learning for the final forecasting. Extensive experimental results on seven real-world datasets demonstrate DyGraphformer outperforms state-of-the-art baseline methods, with comparisons including Transformer-based and GNN-based methods.

**[试图解决的问题]**：Transformer难以有效建模多变量时间序列中跨序列的时变空间依赖，而现有图神经网络基于静态变量关系假设，不符合现实。
**[核心方法]**：提出DyGraphformer，将图卷积集成到Transformer中，利用GRU结合历史空间信息动态推断时变图结构；时间自注意力层和动态图卷积层分别捕获时间与空间依赖，并用层次化编码器学习多尺度时空特征。
**[创新与效果]**：在七个真实数据集上超越包括Transformer和GNN在内的基线方法，有效建模时变空间依赖并提升预测性能。

### 3. [Dynamic Hypergraph Structure Learning for Multivariate Time Series Forecasting](https://doi.org/10.1109/TBDATA.2024.3362188)
`相关度 50` · 超图学习，交通相关但通用

*IEEE Transactions on Big Data* | Shun-Kai Wang, Yong Zhang, ..., Qingming Huang, Baocai Yin | 2024-08-01

**TLDR:** 无

> Multivariate time series forecasting plays an important role in many domain applications, such as air pollution forecasting and traffic forecasting. Modeling the complex dependencies among time series is a key challenging task in multivariate time series forecasting. Many previous works have used graph structures to learn inter-series correlations, which have achieved remarkable performance. However, graph networks can only capture spatio-temporal dependencies between pairs of nodes, which cannot handle high-order correlations among time series. We propose a Dynamic Hypergraph Structure Learning model (DHSL) to solve the above problems. We generate dynamic hypergraph structures from time series data using the K-Nearest Neighbors method. Then a dynamic hypergraph structure learning module is used to optimize the hypergraph structure to obtain more accurate high-order correlations among nodes. Finally, the hypergraph structures dynamically learned are used in the spatio-temporal hypergraph neural network. We conduct experiments on six real-world datasets. The prediction performance of our model surpasses existing graph network-based prediction models. The experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness and competitiveness of the DHSL model for multivariate time series forecasting.

**[试图解决的问题]**：多元时间序列预测中，现有图网络只能捕获节点对间的时空依赖，无法处理序列间的高阶相关性。

**[核心方法]**：提出动态超图结构学习模型（DHSL），先通过K近邻（KNN）从时序数据生成动态超图，再经动态超图结构学习模块优化超图以获取精准的节点高阶关联，最后将动态学习的超图结构用于时空超图神经网络。

**[创新与效果]**：在六个真实数据集上，该模型的预测性能全面超越现有基于图网络的模型，验证了动态超图结构在高阶相关性建模中的有效性与竞争力。

### 4. [FilterTS: Comprehensive Frequency Filtering for Multivariate Time Series Forecasting](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v39i20.35438)
`相关度 40` · 通用时序预测方法，不涉及行人

*AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence* | Yulong Wang, Yushuo Liu, Xiaoyi Duan, Kai Wang | 2025-04-11

**TLDR:** FilterTS is a novel forecasting model that utilizes specialized filtering techniques based on the frequency domain that significantly outperforms existing methods in terms of prediction accuracy and computational efficiency.

> Multivariate time series forecasting is crucial across various industries, where accurate extraction of complex periodic and trend components can significantly enhance prediction performance. However, existing models often struggle to capture these intricate patterns. To address these challenges, we propose FilterTS, a novel forecasting model that utilizes specialized filtering techniques based on the frequency domain. FilterTS introduces a Dynamic Cross-Variable Filtering Module, a key innovation that dynamically leverages other variables as filters to extract and reinforce shared variable frequency components across variables in multivariate time series. Additionally, a Static Global Filtering Module captures stable frequency components, identified throughout the entire training set. Moreover, the model is built in the frequency domain, converting time-domain convolutions into frequency-domain multiplicative operations to enhance computational efficiency. Extensive experimental results on eight real-world datasets have demonstrated that FilterTS significantly outperforms existing methods in terms of prediction accuracy and computational efficiency.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有模型难以从多变量时间序列中精确提取复杂的周期性及趋势成分。  
**[核心方法]**：在频域构建FilterTS模型，包含动态跨变量滤波模块（利用其他变量作为滤波器提取并强化跨变量共享频率分量）与静态全局滤波模块（捕获训练集全局稳定频率分量），并将时域卷积转化为频域乘法运算。  
**[创新与效果]**：在8个真实数据集上预测精度与计算效率均显著优于现有方法。

### 5. [TimePFN: Effective Multivariate Time Series Forecasting with Synthetic Data](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2502.16294)
`相关度 40` · 通用时序预测方法

*AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence* | Ege Onur Taga, M. E. Ildiz, Samet Oymak | 2025-02-22

**TLDR:** This work proposes a novel training scheme and a transformer-based architecture for multivariate time-series (MTS) forecasting that outperforms the existing state-of-the-art models for MTS forecasting in both zero-shot and few-shot settings and facilitates strong zero- and few-shot forecasting performance.

> The diversity of time series applications and scarcity of domain-specific data highlight the need for time-series models with strong few-shot learning capabilities. In this work, we propose a novel training scheme and a transformer-based architecture, collectively referred to as TimePFN, for multivariate time-series (MTS) forecasting. TimePFN is based on the concept of Prior-data Fitted Networks (PFN), which aims to approximate Bayesian inference. Our approach consists of (1) generating synthetic MTS data through diverse Gaussian process kernels and the linear coregionalization method, and (2) a novel MTS architecture capable of utilizing both temporal and cross-channel dependencies across all input patches. We evaluate TimePFN on several benchmark datasets and demonstrate that it outperforms the existing state-of-the-art models for MTS forecasting in both zero-shot and few-shot settings. Notably, fine-tuning TimePFN with as few as 500 data points nearly matches full dataset training error, and even 50 data points yield competitive results. We also find that TimePFN exhibits strong univariate forecasting performance, attesting to its generalization ability. Overall, this work unlocks the power of synthetic data priors for MTS forecasting and facilitates strong zero- and few-shot forecasting performance.

**[试图解决的问题]**: 多元时间序列预测中领域数据稀缺导致模型少样本学习能力不足。  
**[核心方法]**: 采用多样化的高斯过程核与线性区域化方法生成合成多元时间序列数据，并设计基于 Transformer 的新型架构（TimePFN），利用输入补丁内部的时域依赖与跨通道依赖。  
**[创新与效果]**: 在零样本和少样本设定下超越现有最先进模型；仅用500数据点微调即可接近全数据集训练误差，50数据点即能取得有竞争力结果；并展现出色的单变量预测泛化能力。
