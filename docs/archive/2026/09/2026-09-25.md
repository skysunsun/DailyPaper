### 1. [Pedestrian Crossing Direction Prediction at Intersections for Pedestrian Safety](https://doi.org/10.1109/OJITS.2025.3574082)
`相关度 96` · 直接预测路口过街方向与意图

*IEEE Open Journal of Intelligent Transportation Systems* | Younggn Kim, Mohamed A. Abdel-Aty, ..., Dongdong Wang, Shaoyan Zhai | 2025

**TLDR:** 无

> Pedestrians are among the most vulnerable road users, with significant risks arising at intersections due to potential conflicts with vehicular traffic. With advancements in deep learning, many approaches have sought to address this issue by understanding pedestrian intentions based on their behaviors. However, at intersections, a pedestrian's crash risk is highly influenced by their crossing direction, yet no prior work has specifically focused on predicting crossing directions, leaving a critical gap in ensuring pedestrian safety in these high-risk areas. This study introduces a novel camera-invariant framework for predicting pedestrian crossing directions at intersections using keypoints and trajectory data extracted from CCTV footage. To address challenges posed by varying intersection geometries and camera perspectives, we developed a global coordinate system that standardizes spatial features. The framework leverages Transformer-based models, Graph Convolutional Networks (GCNs), and a hybrid Transformer+GCN approach to extract spatial and temporal features from the pedestrian behaviors. Our proposed framework with Transformer-based model achieved accuracy of 94.10% and F1-Score of 92.35%, demonstrating its effectiveness in capturing pedestrian intentions across diverse scenarios. With its demonstrated high accuracy and effectiveness, our framework predicts pedestrians' crossing directions, which not only can enhance pedestrian safety by reducing potential conflicts with vehicular traffic at intersections but also can be applied to signal optimization systems to improve traffic flow. Furthermore, the geometric-invariant model ensures that the system is easily transferable across intersections. It reduces the burden of collecting more data and training it from several camera perspectives. The implementation of this work is publicly available at https://github.com/Kimyounggun99/CrossingDirectionPrediction.

**[试图解决的问题]**：路口行人横穿方向是碰撞风险的关键因素，但此前无专门研究对此进行预测，存在行人安全盲区。
**[核心方法]**：提出相机不变框架，基于CCTV关键点与轨迹数据，建立全局坐标系统一空间特征，采用Transformer、GCN及混合Transformer+GCN提取时空特征，预测路口行人横穿方向。
**[创新与效果]**：首次实现横穿方向预测；Transformer模型达94.10%准确率、92.35% F1-Score；几何不变性使模型可跨路口迁移，减少多视角数据收集与训练负担，可增强行人安全并用于信号优化。

### 2. [HMTraj: A Hybrid Mamba–Transformer Framework for Efficient Pedestrian Trajectory Prediction](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICSE68658.2025.11407580)
`相关度 95` · 行人轨迹预测的高效混合模型

*International Conference on Software Engineering* | Shiyi Tang, Kai Wu | 2025-12-12

**TLDR:** 无

> Accurate and efficient pedestrian trajectory prediction is essential for safe motion planning in autonomous driving and mobile robotic systems. Although deep learning models have advanced this field, achieving a balance between prediction accuracy and computational efficiency remains a significant challenge, especially when jointly modeling long-range historical dependencies lying in pedestrian trajectories and complex social interactions among agents, hindering their practical applications. To address this, we propose HMTraj, a hybrid Mamba–Transformer framework for efficient pedestrian trajectory prediction. HMTraj employs a Motion-Mamba encoder operating on a compact set of representative motion patterns to efficiently capture diverse motion behaviors. Then, a Social-Attention decoder is used to selectively attend social interactions with relevant neighboring pedestrians. Experiments on the Stanford Drone Dataset (SDD) demonstrate that our method achieves competitive accuracy while maintaining superior computational efficiency with fewer parameters and lower inference time, validating its potential for providing reliable and practical trajectory prediction in resource-constrained, real-time applications.

**[试图解决的问题]**：行人轨迹预测中，现有深度模型难以在预测精度与计算效率间取得平衡，尤其在需要同时建模轨迹的长时序历史依赖与智能体间复杂社交交互时。

**[核心方法]**：提出混合架构 HMTraj，包含一个作用于紧凑代表性运动模式集合的 Motion-Mamba 编码器，用于高效捕获多样化运动行为；以及一个 Social-Attention 解码器，用于选择性地关注相关邻近行人的社交交互。

**[创新与效果]**：在 Stanford Drone Dataset（SDD）上，HMTraj 在保持竞争性预测精度的同时，以更少的参数量和更低的推理时间实现了更高的计算效率，验证了其在资源受限、实时应用场景中的实用性。

### 3. [MapCVAE: Probabilistic Prediction of Diverse Pedestrian Behaviors on General Roads](https://doi.org/10.1109/APSIPAASC65261.2025.11249241)
`相关度 95` · 概率建模行人行为多样性

*Asia-Pacific Signal and Information Processing Association Annual Summit and Conference* | Konosuke Kobayashi, Satoru Fujita | 2025-10-22

**TLDR:** 无

> Accurate pedestrian trajectory prediction is crucial for autonomous vehicles, but capturing the diverse and uncertain nature of pedestrian movements remains a significant challenge. This paper introduces MapCVAE, a new model based on Conditional Variational Autoencoders (CVAE), which predicts multiple possible future paths. By integrating semantic map information, MapCVAE produces contextually appropriate trajectories and captures diverse intentions such as changing direction or stopping. Experiments show that MapCVAE significantly outperforms conventional deterministic models in predicting diverse pedestrian behaviors. This research makes self-driving cars safer because it helps them better understand where a person might walk next.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶中行人轨迹预测需应对行人运动的多模态与不确定性，传统确定性模型难以预测转向、停止等多意图行为。

**[核心方法]**：提出 MapCVAE 模型，基于条件变分自编码器（CVAE），融合语义地图信息，输出多条可能未来轨迹，以刻画不同行人意图。

**[创新与效果]**：在一般道路场景下显著优于确定性预测模型，可生成符合上下文语义的多样化轨迹，提升自动驾驶对行人下一步位置的预测能力与安全性。

### 4. [Joint Pedestrian Trajectory Prediction through Posterior Sampling](https://doi.org/10.1109/IROS58592.2024.10802382)
`相关度 95` · 联合行人轨迹预测后验采样

*IEEE/RJS International Conference on Intelligent RObots and Systems* | Hao-Tian Lin, Yi-Xiao Wang, ..., Zhi-Yuan Liu, Masayoshi Tomizuka | 2024-03-30

**TLDR:** 无

> Joint pedestrian trajectory prediction has long grappled with the inherent unpredictability of human behaviors. Recent works employing conditional diffusion models in trajectory prediction have exhibited notable success. Nevertheless, the heavy dependence on accurate historical data results in their vulnerability to noise disturbances and data incompleteness. To improve the robustness and reliability, we introduce the Guided Full Trajectory Diffuser (GFTD), a novel diffusion-based framework that translates prediction as the inverse problem of spatial-temporal inpainting and models the full joint trajectory distribution which includes both history and the future. By learning from the full trajectory and leveraging flexible posterior sampling methods, GFTD can produce accurate predictions while improving the robustness that can generalize to scenarios with noise perturbation or incomplete historical data. Moreover, the pre-trained model enables controllable generation without an additional training budget. Through rigorous experimental evaluation, GFTD exhibits superior performance in joint trajectory prediction with different data quality and in controllable generation tasks. See more results at https://sites.google.com/andrew.cmu.edu/posterior-sampling-prediction.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有基于条件扩散模型的联合行人轨迹预测过度依赖准确历史数据，在噪声扰动或历史数据不完整时鲁棒性差。

**[核心方法]**：提出 Guided Full Trajectory Diffuser（GFTD），将预测转化为时空修复（spatial-temporal inpainting）的逆问题，建模包含历史和未来的完整联合轨迹分布，并利用灵活的后验采样（posterior sampling）生成预测。

**[创新与效果]**：GFTD 通过全轨迹学习与后验采样，在噪声扰动或不完整历史数据下仍能保持准确预测，且预训练模型无需额外训练预算即可实现可控生成；在联合轨迹预测（不同数据质量）和可控生成任务上均取得更优性能。

### 5. [SocialMP: Learning Social Aware Motion Patterns via Additive Fusion for Pedestrian Trajectory Prediction](https://doi.org/10.24963/ijcai.2025/11)
`相关度 94` · 社会交互感知的行人轨迹预测

*International Joint Conference on Artificial Intelligence* | Tianci Gao, Yuzhen Zhang, Hang Guo, Pei Lv | 2025-09-01

**TLDR:** 无

> Accurately capturing social interaction in complex scenarios is essential for pedestrian trajectory prediction task. The uncertainty in pedestrian interactions and the physical constraints imposed by the environment make this task challenging. To solve this problem, existing methods adopt dimensionality reduction algorithms to capture explainable human motions and behaviors. However, these approaches not only suffer from weak social awareness due to the inadequate feature extraction, but also overlook physical constraints, leading to predicted trajectories often cross unwalkable areas. To overcome these problems, we build an attention-based motion pattern representation, named SocialMP, which can effectively enhance the social awareness and environmental perception of motion patterns. Specifically, our method first characterizes the motion patterns through singular value decomposition and defines a visual field-based rule to model environmental social interaction. Then, an attention-based additive fusion mechanism is designed to enhance social awareness and environment perception of motion patterns. Therein, we integrate social interactions into motion patterns through cross-attention mechanism to generate latent motion patterns, and feed them into our devised additive fusion structure with backward connection for multiple iterations. Lastly, we design a map loss function by applying an additional penalty into average displacement error to prevent the pedestrians from passing through the unwalkable area. Extensive experiments on ETH-UCY and SDD datasets demonstrate that our SocialMP can not only improve prediction accuracy but also generate plausible trajectories.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有行人轨迹预测方法在复杂场景中社交交互建模不足，且忽略环境物理约束，导致预测轨迹常穿越不可行走区域。

**[核心方法]**：提出基于注意力与加性融合的社交感知运动模式表示 SocialMP：通过奇异值分解表征运动模式，定义基于视野的规则建模环境社交交互；设计基于交叉注意力的加性融合结构（含反向连接、多次迭代），将社交交互融入潜在运动模式；额外设计地图损失函数，在平均位移误差上施加惩罚以阻止预测轨迹进入不可行走区域。

**[创新与效果]**：在 ETH-UCY 和 SDD 数据集上，SocialMP 在提升预测精度的同时生成符合物理约束的可行轨迹，兼具更强的社交感知与环境感知能力。
