### 1. [Pedestrian Crossing Prediction for Automated Vehicles: A Geometry-aware Method Using Surface Normal](https://doi.org/10.1145/3828157.3828789)
`相关度 95` · 直接研究行人过街意图预测，几何感知方法。

*未知会议/期刊* | A. Eskandari, Mahdi Rezaei, Mohsen Azarmi, D. R. Magee | 2026-09-20

**TLDR:** A geometry-aware framework that incorporates surface normal maps encoding pixel-level 3D surface orientation is proposed that incorporates surface normal maps encoding pixel-level 3D surface orientation and highlights the importance of geometry-informed features as an effective complement to conventional visual inputs.

> Accurate prediction of pedestrian crossing intention is critical for the safety of automated vehicles. Existing approaches mainly rely on visual appearance and scene context derived from raw images and semantic segmentation. We propose a geometry-aware framework that incorporates surface normal maps encoding pixel-level 3D surface orientation. To integrate heterogeneous information, we extend a multi-stream GRU–attention architecture with surface-normal features. The visual branch combines surface normals, local context, and segmentation overlays, while the non-visual branch includes body pose, bounding boxes, and ego-vehicle speed. Extensive ablation studies on the PIE dataset demonstrate that surface normal maps provide consistent performance gains. When combined with contextual features, they improve F1 score and AUC by 3.85% and 2.35%, respectively, and yield more stable predictions up to two seconds before crossing events. These results highlight the importance of geometry-informed features as an effective complement to conventional visual inputs.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶中仅依赖原始图像和语义分割等视觉外观难以稳定预测行人过街意图，尤其缺乏对场景三维几何信息的显式利用。

**[核心方法]**：提出几何感知框架，引入编码像素级3D表面朝向的表面法向图；扩展多流 GRU–注意力架构，视觉流融合表面法向、局部上下文和分割叠加，非视觉流输入人体姿态、边界框和自车速度，进行多模态异质信息集成。

**[创新与效果]**：表面法向图在 PIE 数据集上带来一致性能提升，与上下文特征结合使 F1 提升 3.85%、AUC 提升 2.35%，并在过街事件前 2 秒内获得更稳定的预测结果，验证了几何信息对常规视觉输入的有效补充。

### 2. [MTF-Net: Multi-Modal Temporal Feature Fusion Network for Pedestrian Intention Prediction](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cae0444b917d68c6ededde0b3cd51de235530844)
`相关度 93` · 多模态融合行人意图预测，强相关。

*未知会议/期刊* | Md Mahfuzur Rahman, Peng-Zhan Zhou, ..., Md Mustafizur Rahman, Fang Qu | 2026-07-24

**TLDR:** MTF-Net is proposed, a novel Multi-Modal Temporal Feature Fusion Network that jointly models kinematic, appearance, and contextual cues for pedestrian intention prediction and demonstrates that reliable pedestrian intention prediction arises from principled multi-modal fusion rather than excessive architectural complexity.

> Accurately predicting pedestrian intentions is crucial for ensuring safe and proactive interaction between autonomous vehicles and pedestrians. However, existing approaches often depend on architectures that either model temporal dependencies within individual modalities or fuse modalities only at coarse semantic levels. To address these limitations, we propose MTF-Net, a novel Multi-Modal Temporal Feature Fusion Network that jointly models kinematic, appearance, and contextual cues for pedestrian intention prediction. MTF-Net integrates four complementary modalities-bounding-box dynamics, human pose keypoints, local context, and scene-level semantics within a recurrent fusion framework enhanced by gated linear units (GLUs). These GLU-based modules adaptively regulate cross-modal information flow, enabling interpretable and efficient feature interaction across temporal scales. Through three dedicated temporal encoding branches and an attention-guided fusion head, the proposed model robustly anticipates pedestrian crossing intentions several frames before they occur. Extensive evaluations on the PIE and JAAD benchmarks demonstrate that MTF-Net surpasses recent transformer- and graph-based models, achieving up to 0.95 AUC on PIE and 0.94 AUC on JAAD, while maintaining real-time performance. The results highlight that reliable pedestrian intention prediction arises from principled multi-modal fusion rather than excessive architectural complexity.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有行人意图预测方法存在两个核心局限：仅在单一模态内部建模时序依赖，或仅在粗粒度语义层面对多模态进行融合，难以有效联合运动学、外观与上下文线索进行鲁棒预测。

**[核心方法]**：提出MTF-Net多模态时序特征融合网络，在门控线性单元（GLU）增强的循环融合框架中，整合边界框动态、人体姿态关键点、局部上下文与场景级语义四种互补模态；通过三条专用时序编码分支分别建模不同模态的时序依赖，并利用GLU模块自适应调控跨模态信息流，最后由注意力引导的融合头输出行人穿越意图预测。

**[创新与效果]**：在PIE和JAAD基准上分别达到0.95和0.94 AUC，超越近年基于Transformer和图神经网络的模型，且满足实时推理要求；GLU机制支持可解释的高效跨模态交互，可在行人穿越动作发生前数帧提前预测意图，表明可靠的意图预测源于原理性的多模态融合而非过度复杂的架构设计。

### 3. [Learning Pedestrian Actions to Ensure Safe Autonomous Driving](https://doi.org/10.1109/IV55152.2023.10186530)
`相关度 93` · 行人动作与轨迹预测，保障自动驾驶。

*2023 IEEE Intelligent Vehicles Symposium (IV)* | Jia Huang, Alvika Gautam, S. Saripalli | 2023-05-22

**TLDR:** A novel multi-task sequence to sequence Transformer encoders-decoders (TF-ed) architecture is proposed for pedestrian action and trajectory prediction using only ego vehicle camera observations as inputs and it reaches an accuracy of 81% on action prediction task on JAAD testing data and outperforms the LSTM-ed by 7.4%.

> To ensure safe autonomous driving in urban environments with complex vehicle-pedestrian interactions, it is critical for Autonomous Vehicles (AVs) to have the ability to predict pedestrians’ short-term and immediate actions in real-time. In recent years, various methods have been developed to study estimating pedestrian behaviors for autonomous driving scenarios, but there is a lack of clear definitions for pedestrian behaviors. In this work, the literature gaps are investigated and a taxonomy is presented for pedestrian behavior characterization. Further, a novel multi-task sequence to sequence Transformer encoders-decoders (TF-ed) architecture is proposed for pedestrian action and trajectory prediction using only ego vehicle camera observations as inputs. The proposed approach is compared against an existing LSTM encoders decoders (LSTM-ed) architecture for action and trajectory prediction. The performance of both models is evaluated on the publicly available Joint Attention Autonomous Driving (JAAD) dataset, CARLA simulation data as well as real-time self-driving shuttle data collected on university campus. Evaluation results illustrate that the proposed method reaches an accuracy of 81% on action prediction task on JAAD testing data and outperforms the LSTM-ed by 7.4%, while LSTM counterpart performs much better on trajectory prediction task for a prediction sequence length of 25 frames.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶在复杂车-人交互环境中需要实时预测行人短期/即时动作，但现有行人行为定义缺乏清晰分类，且基于视觉的预测精度不足。

**[核心方法]**：提出一种多任务序列到序列的 Transformer 编码器-解码器（TF-ed）架构，仅以自车摄像头观测为输入，同时进行行人动作分类与轨迹预测；并与 LSTM 编码器-解码器（LSTM-ed）基线对比，在 JAAD、CARLA 及校园实车数据上评估。

**[创新与效果]**：给出了行人行为表征的 taxonomy；在 JAAD 测试集上动作预测准确率达 81%，较 LSTM-ed 提升 7.4%；但轨迹预测在 25 帧序列长度下 LSTM 基线表现更好。

### 4. [Predicting pedestrian trajectories at different densities: A multi-criteria empirical analysis](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physa.2023.129440)
`相关度 92` · 不同密度行人轨迹预测，直接相关。

*Physica A: Statistical Mechanics and its Applications* | R. Korbmacher, Huu-Tu Dang, A. Tordeux | 2023-07-28

**TLDR:** This study investigates the suitability of deep learning algorithms for high-density scenarios by evaluating them on different error metrics and comparing their accuracy to that of knowledge-based models that have been used since long time in the literature.

> Predicting human trajectories is a challenging task due to the complexity of pedestrian behavior, which is influenced by external factors such as the scene's topology and interactions with other pedestrians. A special challenge arises from the dependence of the behaviour on the density of the scene. In the literature, deep learning algorithms show the best performance in predicting pedestrian trajectories, but so far just for situations with low densities. In this study, we aim to investigate the suitability of these algorithms for high-density scenarios by evaluating them on different error metrics and comparing their accuracy to that of knowledge-based models that have been used since long time in the literature. The findings indicate that deep learning algorithms provide improved trajectory prediction accuracy in the distance metrics for all tested densities. Nevertheless, we observe a significant number of collisions in the predictions, especially in high-density scenarios. This issue arises partly due to the absence of a collision avoidance mechanism within the algorithms and partly because the distance-based collision metric is inadequate for dense situations. To address these limitations, we propose the introduction of a novel continuous collision metric based on pedestrians' time-to-collision. Subsequently, we outline how this metric can be utilized to enhance the training of the algorithms.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有深度学习轨迹预测算法仅在低密度场景下验证有效，缺乏对高密度行人场景适用性的系统评估，且既有距离型碰撞指标在高密度条件下无法准确反映碰撞风险。

**[核心方法]**：采用多标准（multi-criteria）实证框架，在不同密度场景下将深度学习算法与知识驱动模型（knowledge-based models）进行轨迹预测精度对比；针对距离型碰撞指标缺陷，提出一种基于行人碰撞时间（time-to-collision, TTC）的连续碰撞度量，并将其用于算法训练。

**[创新与效果]**：深度学习在全部测试密度下均取得更优的距离误差指标，但预测轨迹中存在大量碰撞，且在高密度场景尤为严重；为此引入TTC连续碰撞度量替代距离型指标，并证明其可嵌入训练过程以缓解碰撞问题。

### 5. [Temporal and Fine-Grained Pedestrian Action Recognition on Driving Recorder Database](https://doi.org/10.3390/s18020627)
`相关度 92` · 细粒度行人动作识别，直接相关。

*Italian National Conference on Sensors* | Hirokatsu Kataoka, Y. Satoh, ..., S. Oikawa, Y. Matsui | 2018-02-01

**TLDR:** The paper thoroughly evaluated several types of configurations to explore an effective approach in fine-grained pedestrian action recognition without a large-scale database and found out how to learn an effective recognition model with only a small-scaledatabase.

> The paper presents an emerging issue of fine-grained pedestrian action recognition that induces an advanced pre-crush safety to estimate a pedestrian intention in advance. The fine-grained pedestrian actions include visually slight differences (e.g., walking straight and crossing), which are difficult to distinguish from each other. It is believed that the fine-grained action recognition induces a pedestrian intention estimation for a helpful advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS). The following difficulties have been studied to achieve a fine-grained and accurate pedestrian action recognition: (i) In order to analyze the fine-grained motion of a pedestrian appearance in the vehicle-mounted drive recorder, a method to describe subtle change of motion characteristics occurring in a short time is necessary; (ii) even when the background moves greatly due to the driving of the vehicle, it is necessary to detect changes in subtle motion of the pedestrian; (iii) the collection of large-scale fine-grained actions is very difficult, and therefore a relatively small database should be focused. We find out how to learn an effective recognition model with only a small-scale database. Here, we have thoroughly evaluated several types of configurations to explore an effective approach in fine-grained pedestrian action recognition without a large-scale database. Moreover, two different datasets have been collected in order to raise the issue. Finally, our proposal attained 91.01% on National Traffic Science and Environment Laboratory database (NTSEL) and 53.23% on the near-miss driving recorder database (NDRDB). The paper has improved +8.28% and +6.53% from baseline two-stream fusion convnets.

**[试图解决的问题]**：车载行驶记录仪视角下细粒度行人动作（如直行与横穿）视觉差异微小，且大规模标注数据库难以获取，需在小型数据库上实现准确识别以支撑ADAS预碰撞安全。

**[核心方法]**：系统评估多种配置（输入表征、网络结构、融合策略等），以双流融合卷积网络（two-stream fusion convnets）为基线，探索在无大规模数据条件下有效描述短时细微运动特征并抑制车辆运动背景干扰的识别模型。

**[创新与效果]**：在小型数据库上达到有效识别，NTSEL准确率91.01%，NDRDB准确率53.23%，较基线双流融合卷积网络分别提升+8.28%和+6.53%；解决了大规模数据缺失及强背景运动下的细粒度行人动作识别限制。
