### 1. [Pedestrian Intention Prediction Using LSTM for Right-of-Way Decision-Making of Connected Vehicles in Mixed Traffic Flow](https://doi.org/10.1145/3832928.3832931)
`相关度 95` · 直接研究行人过街意图预测用于车辆决策

*未知会议/期刊* | Xue Wang, Jia-Ming Wang, ..., Teng Zhao, Wei Qin | 2026-05-15

**TLDR:** A pedestrian intention prediction approach based on long short-term memory (LSTM) networks is presented and then a CAV's right-of-way decision-making algorithm under mixed traffic conditions is built and leads to reduced vehicular delay and unnecessary stoppage without compromising the pedestrian's safety.

> Connected and autonomous vehicles (CAVs) tend to drive in a mixed-traffic stream with the advancement of connected vehicle technologies, where the pedestrian-vehicle right-of-way conflict in an unsignalized intersection greatly impacts the traffic performance and safety, while existing CAV control policies usually yield conservatively, leading to unnecessary braking, or even halting the vehicle if a pedestrian's intent is not clear. To tackle the problem, this paper presents a pedestrian intention prediction approach based on long short-term memory (LSTM) networks and then builds a CAV's right-of-way decision-making algorithm under mixed traffic conditions. The temporal-spatial information, such as pedestrian trajectory, speed, acceleration, waiting time, and surrounding traffic conditions, is extracted for the construction of a multi-dimensional input sequence, and the LSTM model is applied to perform classification on pedestrians’ “stop/go” intention. The predicted intent is fed to a decision making module which determines the action of CAVs, taking into account the risk posed by pedestrians as well as the real time state of traffic. The simulation experiments carried out in the SUMO–Veins platform show that the proposed approach leads to reduced vehicular delay and unnecessary stoppage, without compromising the pedestrian's safety, to prove its efficacy and suitability for use with mixed traffic flows.

**[试图解决的问题]**：混行交通流中无信号交叉口CAV与行人路权冲突时，现有控制策略因无法判断行人意图而保守制动甚至停车，造成不必要的延误与停驶。

**[核心方法]**：基于LSTM网络构建行人“stop/go”意图分类模型，输入为行人轨迹、速度、加速度、等待时间及周围交通条件组成的多维时序信息；预测结果输入CAV路权决策模块，结合行人风险与实时交通状态决定车辆动作，并在SUMO–Veins平台进行仿真验证。

**[创新与效果]**：相比保守策略，该方法在保障行人安全的前提下减少了车辆延误与不必要停车，证明其适用于混行交通流场景。

### 2. [SocialCVAE: Predicting Pedestrian Trajectory via Interaction Conditioned Latents](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2402.17339)
`相关度 95` · 行人轨迹预测，直接相关

*AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence* | Wei Xiang, Haoteng Yin, He Wang, Xiaogang Jin | 2024-02-27

**TLDR:** The social conditional variational autoencoder (SocialCVAE) is proposed for predicting pedestrian trajectories, which employs a CVAE to explore behavioral uncertainty in human motion decisions and significantly improves prediction accuracy compared with the state-of-the-art methods.

> Pedestrian trajectory prediction is the key technology in many applications for providing insights into human behavior and anticipating human future motions. Most existing empirical models are explicitly formulated by observed human behaviors using explicable mathematical terms with deterministic nature, while recent work has focused on developing hybrid models combined with learning-based techniques for powerful expressiveness while maintaining explainability. However, the deterministic nature of the learned steering behaviors from the empirical models limits the models' practical performance. To address this issue, this work proposes the social conditional variational autoencoder (SocialCVAE) for predicting pedestrian trajectories, which employs a CVAE to explore behavioral uncertainty in human motion decisions. SocialCVAE learns socially reasonable motion randomness by utilizing a socially explainable interaction energy map as the CVAE's condition, which illustrates the future occupancy of each pedestrian's local neighborhood area. The energy map is generated using an energy-based interaction model, which anticipates the energy cost (i.e., repulsion intensity) of pedestrians' interactions with neighbors. Experimental results on two public benchmarks including 25 scenes demonstrate that SocialCVAE significantly improves prediction accuracy compared with the state-of-the-art methods, with up to 16.85% improvement in Average Displacement Error (ADE) and 69.18% improvement in Final Displacement Error (FDE). Code is available at: https://github.com/ViviXiang/SocialCVAE.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有基于经验模型的行人轨迹预测方法虽可解释，但其学习到的确定性转向行为限制了实际预测性能，无法刻画行人运动决策中的行为不确定性。

**[核心方法]**：提出 SocialCVAE（社会条件变分自编码器），以 CVAE 建模行人运动中的随机行为；利用基于能量的交互模型生成可解释的交互能量图（表征邻域占用与排斥强度），并将其作为 CVAE 的条件输入，从而学习 socially reasonable 的运动随机性。

**[创新与效果]**：在 25 个场景的两个公开基准上，相比 SOTA 方法显著提升精度：平均位移误差（ADE）最高降低 16.85%，最终位移误差（FDE）最高降低 69.18%。

### 3. [Analysis and Prediction of Pedestrians’ Violation Behavior at the Intersection Based on a Markov Chain](https://doi.org/10.3390/SU13105690)
`相关度 92` · 行人违规过街行为预测，直接相关

*Sustainability* | Cheng-Yuan Mao, Lewen Bao, ..., Wenjiao Xu, Qin Wang | 2021-05-19

**TLDR:** This study proposes an illegal pedestrian crossing behavior prediction approach that consists of a logistic regression model and a Markov Chain model and shows that the proposed approach has a robust ability in pedestrian violation behavior prediction.

> Pedestrian violations pose a danger to themselves and other road users. Most previous studies predict pedestrian violation behaviors based only on pedestrians’ demographic characteristics. In practice, in addition to demographic characteristics, other factors may also impact pedestrian violation behaviors. Therefore, this study aims to predict pedestrian crossing violations based on pedestrian attributes, traffic conditions, road geometry, and environmental conditions. Data on the pedestrian crossing, both in compliance and in violation, were collected from 10 signalized intersections in the city of Jinhua, China. We propose an illegal pedestrian crossing behavior prediction approach that consists of a logistic regression model and a Markov Chain model. The former calculates the likelihood that the first pedestrian who decides to cross the intersection illegally within each signal cycle, while the latter computes the probability that the subsequent pedestrians who decides to follow the violation. The proposed approach was validated using data gathered from an additional signalized intersection in Jinhua city. The results show that the proposed approach has a robust ability in pedestrian violation behavior prediction. The findings can provide theoretical references for pedestrian signal timing, crossing facility optimization, and warning system design.

**[试图解决的问题]**：行人违章过街行为预测多仅依赖人口统计学特征，忽视交通条件、道路几何和环境等因素，难以准确刻画信号周期内群体违章动态。

**[核心方法]**：结合逻辑回归模型与马尔可夫链：逻辑回归计算每个信号周期内首个违章过街行人的出现概率，马尔可夫链计算后续行人跟随违章的概率；基于浙江金华10个信号交叉口数据训练，并在额外1个交叉口数据上验证。

**[创新与效果]**：实现了对行人违章行为的多因素融合预测，验证集上表现出稳健的预测能力；可为行人信号配时、过街设施优化及违章预警系统设计提供理论参考。

### 4. [Neural Network Aided Potential Field Approach For Pedestrian Prediction](https://doi.org/10.1109/SDF.2019.8916659)
`相关度 92` · 行人轨迹预测用于自动驾驶

*Sensor Data Fusion* | F. Particke, Jiaren Zhou, ..., Christian Hofmann, J. Thielecke | 2019-10-01

**TLDR:** 无

> Autonomous driving is one of the key challenges in recent time. As pedestrians are the most vulnerable traffic participants, collisions with pedestrians have to be avoided under all circumstances. Hence, prediction of pedestrian trajectories is of high interest for automated vehicles. For this purpose, a plethora of algorithms has been proposed to model the pedestrian in the last decades, reaching from simple kinematic models to advanced microscopic models. In addition, the machine learning community started to learn the behavior of pedestrians and showed major improvements in complex scenarios or unexpected situations. However, as most of the machine learning algorithms are treated as black boxes, the safeguarding of the software is one key challenge which has to be solved. This contribution proposes to combine classic modeling of pedestrians with machine learning algorithms by learning the model errors between a simple physical model and real data. In particular, it is proposed to combine a physical model based on potential fields with a neural network to predict the future behavior of pedestrians. It is shown that the combined approach outperforms the physical model in learnable areas, whereas the physical model without the neural network is more robust in areas where almost no training data is available. In addition, different structures of neural networks are analyzed.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶中行人作为最脆弱交通参与者需绝对避免碰撞，而纯机器学习轨迹预测模型因“黑箱”特性难以通过功能安全验证。

**[核心方法]**：将基于势场（potential field）的经典行人物理模型与神经网络混合：用神经网络学习简单物理模型输出与真实轨迹数据之间的模型误差（model error），从而对物理模型预测进行修正；并比较了不同的神经网络结构。

**[创新与效果]**：混合方法在训练数据充足的“可学习区域”预测精度优于纯物理模型；而在训练数据近于无的稀疏区域，纯物理模型本身更具鲁棒性，兼顾了可学习性与可解释性/安全性。

### 5. [Trajectory prediction considering the behavior of pedestrians intersecting with vehicles](https://doi.org/10.1080/01691864.2023.2252046)
`相关度 90` · 行人轨迹预测且考虑人车交互

*Adv. Robotics* | Soichiro Tanno, Y. Tamura, Yasuhisa Hirata | 2023-09-04

**TLDR:** A trajectory prediction method that takes into account pedestrian behavior and is found to reduce the average displacement error and the final displacement error by 2.70% compared to the case where the target pedestrian's behavior is not considered.

> In this paper, we propose a trajectory prediction method that takes into account pedestrian behavior. To realize safe automated driving in urban areas, it is necessary to predict the future movements of road users. Pedestrians entering the vehicle's direction are a target of interest, but they are difficult to predict because they change their behavior through significant interactions with the vehicle. In this study, we first predict whether pedestrians will yield the way to a vehicle. Next, the predictions are then used to predict the future trajectories of all road users in the scene. The proposed model consists of two neural network structures: the Yielding judgment module to predict pedestrian behavior and the Trajectory prediction module. The yielding judgment module provides intuitive and easily understandable indicators, which also helps to increase the interpretability of the overall model. We evaluated the usefulness of the proposed model using a publicly available dataset. The proposed method was found to reduce the average displacement error by 2.79% and the final displacement error by 2.70% compared to the case where the target pedestrian's behavior is not considered. GRAPHICAL ABSTRACT

**[试图解决的问题]**：城市自动驾驶场景中，与车辆存在显著交互的行人行为难以预测，导致轨迹预测精度不足。

**[核心方法]**：提出包含两个神经网络模块的轨迹预测模型：Yielding judgment module 先预测行人是否会让行，再将行为预测结果输入 Trajectory prediction module，联合预测场景中所有道路使用者的未来轨迹；其中让行判断模块提供直观可理解的中间指标，增强模型可解释性。

**[创新与效果]**：在公开数据集上，相比不考虑目标行人行为的模型，平均位移误差（ADE）降低 2.79%，最终位移误差（FDE）降低 2.70%；同时通过行为判断模块提升了整体可解释性。
