### 1. [MambaIA: A Unified Framework for Pedestrian Crossing Intention and Action Prediction](https://doi.org/10.1109/CIML69155.2026.11692767)
`相关度 95` · 行人过街意图与动作预测统一框架

*2026 International Conference on Computational Intelligence and Machine Learning (CIML)* | Ke-Yu Feng, Dan Wei | 2026-03-01

**TLDR:** 无

> Accurate prediction of pedestrian crossing intention and future actions is essential for safe autonomous driving. This paper proposes MambaIA, a unified framework integrating multimodal perception with selective state-space modeling. Multimodal visual features—including RGB (Red, Green, Blue) images, optical flow and semantic segmentation—are fused to construct global scene representations. A vision–language module extracts pedestrian behavioral attributes, while a Mamba-Variational AutoEncoder (Mamba-VAE) models motion uncertainty. A multi-task decoder then jointly predicts crossing intentions and future actions. Experiments on JAAD and PIE datasets show that MambaIA achieves competitive performance, with an F1-score of 0.80/0.83 and accuracy of 0.82/0.87, respectively. This work provides a robust solution for pedestrian behavior prediction, enhancing safety and reliability in addressing complex, real-world challenges.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶中行人过街意图与未来动作预测的准确性问题，现有方法难以有效融合多模态信息并建模运动不确定性。

**[核心方法]**：提出统一框架 MambaIA，融合 RGB 图像、光流和语义分割构建全局场景表示；视觉-语言模块提取行人行为属性；Mamba-VAE 建模运动不确定性；多任务解码器联合预测过街意图与未来动作。

**[创新与效果]**：在 JAAD 和 PIE 数据集上，F1-score 分别达到 0.80/0.83，准确率分别达到 0.82/0.87，实现竞争性性能，为复杂真实场景下的行人行为预测提供鲁棒方案。

### 2. [iDMaTraj: Improved Diffusion Mamba Model for Stochastic Pedestrian Trajectory Prediction](https://doi.org/10.3390/computers15010012)
`相关度 95` · 扩散Mamba随机行人轨迹预测

*De Computis* | Yin Wang, Fei-Ran Fu, ..., Zheng-Wei Ren, Yuejianan Gu | 2026-01-01

**TLDR:** 无

> Trajectory prediction constitutes a key technology for intelligent systems to forecast future movements of dynamic agents, yet it faces significant challenges due to the uncertainty of motion behavior. We propose iDMa, a stochastic trajectory prediction framework that pioneers the integration of diffusion model with Mamba architecture to achieve high-precision and high-efficiency trajectory generation. Our approach introduces two key innovations: (1) a dual-parameter learning mechanism that optimizes noise estimation of mean and variance space, unlike conventional diffusion methods that employ fixed variance during the denoising process, so as to constrain the feasible domain more accurately; (2) a hybrid denoising backbone network that incorporates Transformer encoders and Mamba blocks. Compared to existing state-of-the-art methods, iDMa reduces the average displacement error (ADE) by 4.76% (0.20 vs. 0.21) on the ETH-UCY dataset and 1.85% (7.95 vs. 8.10) on the SDD dataset.

**[试图解决的问题]**：行人轨迹预测中运动行为固有的不确定性导致预测精度受限，且现有扩散模型在去噪过程中使用固定方差导致可行域约束不精确。

**[核心方法]**：提出 iDMa 随机轨迹预测框架，将扩散模型与 Mamba 架构结合；具体包括：(1) 双参数学习机制，在均值与方差空间同时优化噪声估计，替代传统去噪过程中的固定方差设定；(2) 混合去噪主干网络，由 Transformer 编码器与 Mamba 块构成。

**[创新与效果]**：相比 SOTA 方法，在 ETH-UCY 数据集上平均位移误差（ADE）降低 4.76%（0.20 vs 0.21），在 SDD 数据集上降低 1.85%（7.95 vs 8.10），实现了高精度与高时效的轨迹生成；双参数学习机制更精准地约束可行域，混合主干网络提升了去噪效率与建模能力。

### 3. [Gating Syn-to-Real Knowledge for Pedestrian Crossing Prediction in Safe Driving](https://doi.org/10.1109/TITS.2025.3554767)
`相关度 95` · 驾驶场景行人过街预测，利用合成知识

*IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print)* | Jie Bai, Jianwu Fang, ..., Jianru Xue, Zhengguo Li | 2024-08-24

**TLDR:** 无

> Pedestrian crossing prediction (PCP) in driving scenes plays a critical role in ensuring the safe decision of intelligent vehicles. Due to the limited observations and annotations of pedestrian crossing behaviors in real situations, recent studies have begun to leverage synthetic data with flexible variation to boost prediction performance, employing domain adaptation frameworks. However, different domain knowledge has distinct cross-domain distribution gaps, which necessitates suitable domain knowledge adaption ways for PCP tasks. In this work, we propose a gated syn-to-real knowledge transfer approach for PCP (Gated-S2R-PCP), which has two aims: 1) designing the suitable domain adaptation ways for different kinds of crossing-domain knowledge, and 2) transferring suitable knowledge for specific situations with gated knowledge fusion. Specifically, we design a framework that contains three domain adaption methods including style transfer, distribution approximation, and knowledge distillation for various information, such as visual, semantic, depth, bounding boxes, etc. A learnable gated unit (LGU) is employed to fuse suitable cross-domain knowledge to boost pedestrian crossing prediction. We construct a new synthetic benchmark S2R-PCP-3181 with 3181 sequences (489,740 frames) which contains the pedestrian bounding boxes, RGB frames, semantic segmentation maps, and depth maps. With the synthetic S2R-PCP-3181, we transfer the knowledge to two real challenging datasets of PIE and JAAD, and superior PCP performance is obtained to the state-of-the-art methods.

**[试图解决的问题]**：真实驾驶场景中行人过街行为（PCP）的观测与标注有限，现有方法利用合成数据做域自适应提升预测，但不同域知识（视觉、语义、深度、边框等）的跨域分布差异不同，需要合适的适配与融合方式。

**[核心方法]**：提出 Gated-S2R-PCP，对视觉、语义、深度、边框等异构信息分别采用风格迁移、分布近似、知识蒸馏三种域适配方法进行合成到真实的知识迁移，并用可学习门控单元（LGU）按具体情境融合合适的跨域知识以预测行人过街；构建合成基准 S2R-PCP-3181（3181段序列/489,740帧，含RGB、语义分割、深度图与行人边框标注）。

**[创新与效果]**：在真实数据集 PIE 和 JAAD 上取得优于现有 SOTA 的行人过街预测性能；同时提供多模态标注的合成基准 S2R-PCP-3181，支持分类型域适配与门控知识融合的验证。

### 4. [Predicting Pedestrian Crossing Behavior at Unsignalized Mid-Block Crosswalks Using Maximum Entropy Deep Inverse Reinforcement Learning](https://doi.org/10.1109/TITS.2023.3326276)
`相关度 95` · 逆强化学习预测过街行为，直接相关

*IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print)* | Yongjie Wang, Yuchen Niu, ..., Qiong Li, Tao Wang | 2024-05-01

**TLDR:** 无

> In the future driverless scenario, pedestrian-vehicle conflict is an unavoidable traffic problem at unsignalized mid-block crosswalks. Autonomous vehicles are obviously impossible to make eye contact with pedestrians the way a human driver can. Therefore, there is an urgent need for autonomous vehicles to achieve accurate predictions of pedestrian crossing behavior. In order to better solve the problem of high uncertainty of pedestrian crossing behavior, in this paper, a modeling framework combines Maximum Entropy Deep Inverse Reinforcement Learning (Deep MEIRL) and reinforcement learning is employed to predict pedestrian crossing behaviors. The dataset of drone-based video footage is collected in Xi’an (China) to train and validate the model. The trajectory dataset, extracted by computer vision algorithm, is implemented to derive state features of pedestrian behavior, including pedestrian to target area distance, vehicle type, vehicle speed, lateral/longitudinal distances, pedestrian lateral/longitudinal velocities. The results reveal that Deep MEIRL performs better than a baseline model MEIRL at micro-scales such as predicting pedestrian trajectories and evasive actions. Specifically, in predicting pedestrian trajectories, the Deep MEIRL outperforms the MEIRL by 33.09% and 15.16% on the basis of the MAE and HD, respectively. Meanwhile, the Deep MEIRL is 28.7% and 17.6% respectively more accurate than the MEIRL in predicting pedestrian evasive actions on lateral and longitudinal directions. Furthermore, we also found that there is heterogeneity in pedestrian crossing behavior when interacting with different vehicles. This research can contribute to a critical step toward addressing the safe and efficient movement at unsignalized mid-block crosswalks for autonomous vehicles.

**[试图解决的问题]**：无信号灯路段行人过街行为高不确定性，且自动驾驶车辆无法像人类驾驶员那样与行人进行眼神交流，需准确预测行人过街行为以避免人车冲突。

**[核心方法]**：提出结合最大熵深度逆强化学习（Deep MEIRL）与强化学习的建模框架，使用西安无人机视频提取的行人轨迹数据集，构造行人到目标区域距离、车辆类型、车速、横纵向距离、行人横纵向速度等状态特征，用于预测行人过街行为。

**[创新与效果]**：相比基线模型MEIRL，Deep MEIRL在微观尺度上表现更优：行人轨迹预测MAE提升33.09%、HD提升15.16%；行人横向规避动作预测准确率提升28.7%，纵向提升17.6%。此外发现行人与不同类型车辆交互时过街行为存在异质性。

### 5. [Trustworthy Pedestrian Trajectory Prediction via Pattern-Aware Interaction Modeling](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/951306673f9307bc4d4f546be20535eaf3d78fe6)
`相关度 92` · 可信行人轨迹预测与交互建模

*未知会议/期刊* | Kaiyuan Zhai, Juan Chen, ..., Zeyi Xu, Guoming Tang | 2025-07-16

**TLDR:** 无

> Accurate and reliable pedestrian trajectory prediction is critical for the application of intelligent applications, yet achieving trustworthy prediction remains highly challenging due to the complexity of interactions among pedestrians. Previous methods often adopt black-box modeling of pedestrian interactions. Despite their strong performance, such opaque modeling limits the reliability of predictions in real-world deployments. To address this issue, we propose InSyn (Interaction-Synchronization Network), a novel Transformer-based model that explicitly captures diverse interaction patterns (e.g., walking in sync or conflicting) while effectively modeling direction-sensitive social behaviors. Additionally, we introduce a training strategy, termed Seq-Start of Seq (SSOS), designed to alleviate the common issue of initial-step divergence in numerical time-series prediction. Experiments on the ETH and UCY datasets demonstrate that our model not only outperforms recent black-box baselines in prediction accuracy, especially under high-density scenarios, but also provides transparent interaction modeling, as shown in the case study. Furthermore, the SSOS strategy proves to be effective in improving sequential prediction performance, reducing the initial-step prediction error by approximately 6.58%. Code is avaliable at https://github.com/rickzky1001/InSyn

**[试图解决的问题]**：行人轨迹预测中，现有方法对行人交互进行黑盒建模，导致预测在真实部署中缺乏可解释性与可靠性，尤其在复杂交互场景下。

**[核心方法]**：提出 InSyn（Interaction-Synchronization Network），一种基于 Transformer 的模型，显式捕获多样化交互模式（如同步行走或冲突），并对方向敏感的社交行为进行建模；同时引入训练策略 Seq-Start of Seq（SSOS），缓解数值时间序列预测中常见的初始步发散问题。

**[创新与效果]**：在 ETH 和 UCY 数据集上，InSyn 在预测精度上优于近期黑盒基线，尤其在高密度场景中表现出色；案例研究展示了透明的交互建模能力。SSOS 策略有效提升序列预测性能，将初始步预测误差降低约 6.58%。
