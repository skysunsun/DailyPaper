### 1. [Prediction of Multi-Pedestrian Interaction with Vehicles in Shared Spaces: A Particle-Based Graph Neural Network Approach](https://doi.org/10.1109/ITSC60802.2025.11423786)
`相关度 90` · 直接研究多行人-车辆交互预测

*2025 IEEE 28th International Conference on Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITSC)* | Manman Wei, Masaki Onishi, Yingjie Yin | 2025-11-18

**TLDR:** 无

> Modeling pedestrian motion is essential for safe navigation of autonomous vehicles in shared spaces. The pedestrian movement is inherently complex and characterized by intricate interactions and frequent transitions between behavioral modes, making accurate prediction challenging. Rather than predicting a single trajectory, modeling the potential spatial distribution of pedestrian locations is more meaningful because autonomous vehicles are primarily concerned with the likely positions of pedestrians along a specific path. Although existing methods based on deep generative models have improved the prediction accuracy by incorporating uncertainty, they often encounter challenges, such as limited model interpretability and unreasonable predictions. To address these issues, this paper proposes a novel approach that integrates graph neural networks (GNNs) with a particle-based modeling framework. By leveraging the particle-based method, the proposed model captures various possibilities of pedestrian behavior, with the aim of enhancing both the rationality and interpretability of trajectory predictions. The effectiveness of the proposed method was validated using publicly available datasets, demonstrating its capability to produce more reliable and informative trajectory-prediction distributions. The proposed model enables safer and more reliable autonomous navigation in shared spaces by accurately predicting diverse interaction-aware pedestrian trajectories.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有深度生成模型在预测共享空间行人与车辆交互轨迹时，存在模型可解释性差、预测不合理的问题。  
**[核心方法]**：将图神经网络（GNN）与基于粒子的建模框架（particle-based modeling framework）相结合，通过粒子方法捕捉行人行为的多模态可能性。  
**[创新与效果]**：在公开数据集上验证，该方法能产生更可靠、信息更丰富的轨迹预测分布，提升了共享空间自动驾驶的安全性与可靠性。

### 3. [A Glimpse into the Future: An Inverse Soft Q-Learning's Soft Actor-Critic Approach for Pedestrian Path Prediction](https://doi.org/10.1109/IV64158.2025.11097431)
`相关度 90` · 直接研究行人路径预测

*2025 IEEE Intelligent Vehicles Symposium (IV)* | Laura Dietl, Christian Facchi | 2025-06-22

**TLDR:** 无

> Anticipating the future trajectories of pedestrians is a essential ability in autonomous vehicles to perform proactive actions and thus reduce dangerous encounters. However, predicting human motion is a task that is inherently challenging due to the influence of social and environmental factors, and the multimodality of future predictions based solely on partial history of their trajectory. The underlying scene and the past trajectory of a pedestrian provide useful indicators for predicting their future steps. Unlike other approaches that utilize supervised learning or generative modeling, Inverse Reinforcement Learning enables a model to learn the pedestrian's reward function that encodes their intentions. This work proposes a framework based on Inverse soft Q-Learning's Soft Actor-Critic Version. The framework utilizes the information about the scene and the past trajectory of a pedestrian together with an attention mechanism to learn the pedestrian's behavior policy. Quantitative and qualitative evaluation on existing pedestrian trajectory prediction benchmarks show comparable results to state-of-the-art baselines.

**[试图解决的问题]**：行人轨迹预测受社会与环境因素及未来多模态性影响，传统方法难以学习行人意图编码的奖励函数。

**[核心方法]**：基于逆软Q学习的软演员-评论家框架，结合场景信息、历史轨迹与注意力机制学习行人行为策略。

**[创新与效果]**：在行人轨迹预测基准上实现了与最先进基线相当的性能。

### 4. [Optimal Behavior Planning for Implicit Communication Using a Probabilistic Vehicle-Pedestrian Interaction Model](https://doi.org/10.1109/IV64158.2025.11097656)
`相关度 90` · 车-人交互建模，行人意图

*2025 IEEE Intelligent Vehicles Symposium (IV)* | Markus Amann, Malte Probst, ..., Thomas H. Weisswange, Miguel 'Angel Sotelo | 2025-04-21

**TLDR:** A combined prediction and planning approach that allows to consider the influence of the planned vehicle behavior on a pedestrian and predict a pedestrian's reaction, and probabilistic modeling of the pedestrian reaction facilitates considering the pedestrian's costs, thereby improving cooperative behavior planning.

> In interactions between automated vehicles (AVs) and crossing pedestrians, modeling implicit vehicle communication is crucial. In this work, we present a combined prediction and planning approach that allows to consider the influence of the planned vehicle behavior on a pedestrian and predict a pedestrian's reaction. We plan the behavior by solving two consecutive optimal control problems (OCPs) analytically, using variational calculus. We perform a validation step that assesses whether the planned vehicle behavior is adequate to trigger a certain pedestrian reaction, which accounts for the closed-loop characteristics of prediction and planning influencing each other. In this step, we model the influence of the planned vehicle behavior on the pedestrian using a probabilistic behavior acceptance model that returns an estimate for the crossing probability. The probabilistic modeling of the pedestrian reaction facilitates considering the pedestrian's costs, thereby improving cooperative behavior planning. We demonstrate the performance of the proposed approach in simulated vehicle-pedestrian interactions with varying initial settings and high-light the decision making capabilities of the planning approach.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶车辆与过街行人交互中，如何建模隐式通信并规划行为以引发预期行人反应。
**[核心方法]**：结合预测与规划，通过变分法解析求解两个连续最优控制问题（OCPs），并利用概率性行为接受模型（估计过街概率）进行验证步骤，评估计划行为对行人反应的闭环影响。
**[创新与效果]**：概率性建模行人反应以考虑其成本，提升合作行为规划能力，在模拟场景中展示了决策性能。

### 5. [BP-SGCN: Behavioral Pseudo-Label Informed Sparse Graph Convolution Network for Pedestrian and Heterogeneous Trajectory Prediction](https://doi.org/10.1109/TNNLS.2025.3545268)
`相关度 90` · 行人轨迹预测，直接相关

*IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks and Learning Systems* | Ruochen Li, Stamos Katsigiannis, Tae-Kyun Kim, Hubert P. H. Shum | 2025-02-20

**TLDR:** This work introduces the behavioral pseudo-labels that effectively capture the behavior distributions of pedestrians and heterogeneous agents solely based on their motion features, significantly improving the accuracy of trajectory prediction.

> Trajectory prediction allows better decision-making in applications of autonomous vehicles (AVs) or surveillance by predicting the short-term future movement of traffic agents. It is classified into pedestrian or heterogeneous trajectory prediction. The former exploits the relatively consistent behavior of pedestrians, but is limited in real-world scenarios with heterogeneous traffic agents such as cyclists and vehicles. The latter typically relies on extra class label information to distinguish the heterogeneous agents, but such labels are costly to annotate and cannot be generalized to represent different behaviors within the same class of agents. In this work, we introduce the behavioral pseudo-labels that effectively capture the behavior distributions of pedestrians and heterogeneous agents solely based on their motion features, significantly improving the accuracy of trajectory prediction. To implement the framework, we propose the behavioral pseudo-label informed sparse graph convolution network (BP-SGCN) that learns pseudo-labels and informs to a trajectory predictor. For optimization, we propose a cascaded training scheme, in which we first learn the pseudo-labels in an unsupervised manner, and then perform end-to-end fine-tuning on the labels in the direction of increasing the trajectory prediction accuracy. Experiments show that our pseudo-labels effectively model different behavior clusters and improve trajectory prediction. Our proposed BP-SGCN outperforms existing methods using both pedestrian [ETH/UCY, pedestrian-only Stanford Drone Dataset (SDD)] and heterogeneous agent datasets (SDD and Argoverse 1).

**[试图解决的问题]**: 现有行人轨迹预测依赖行为一致性假设，但现实场景存在异构交通参与者（如自行车、车辆）；异构预测需额外类别标签，标注成本高且无法区分同类内的不同行为。

**[核心方法]**: 提出行为伪标签（Behavioral Pseudo-Label），仅基于运动特征以无监督方式学习异构体行为分布；构建BP-SGCN（行为伪标签引导的稀疏图卷积网络），采用级联训练：先无监督学习伪标签，再端到端微调以提升轨迹预测精度。

**[创新与效果]**: 有效建模不同行为簇，提升轨迹预测精度；在行人（ETH/UCY）及异构数据集（SDD、Argoverse 1）上超越现有方法。
