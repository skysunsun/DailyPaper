### 1. [Unified Pedestrian Path Prediction Using Inverse Reinforcement Learning](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1fb56a746e9d414c691ad030ce6ad7dd5a84d8a9)
`相关度 95` · 直接研究行人路径预测

*未知会议/期刊* | Šimon Sukup, Ariyan Bighashdel, P. Jancura | 2026-08-16

**TLDR:** The proposed learning-task formulations improve prediction performance across the selected benchmark datasets compared with the standard supervised-learning formulation and demonstrate that reformulating the decision process and training objective can improve an advanced pedestrian trajectory prediction architecture.

> Pedestrian path prediction is crucial for enhancing the safety of autonomous vehicles and advanced driver-assistance systems. Previous studies explored different learning-task formulations for pedestrian path prediction and compared these formulations using shallow neural networks, but did not extend this analysis to more complex deep-learning models. This paper adapts the Spatial-Temporal Graph Attention Network (STGAT) to a unified pedestrian path prediction framework and introduces state and action definitions specific to STGAT. The resulting formulations support deterministic and stochastic policies, one-time and sequential decision-making, and reinforcement-learning algorithms including REINFORCE and proximal policy optimization. The proposed learning-task formulations improve prediction performance across the selected benchmark datasets compared with the standard supervised-learning formulation. These results demonstrate that reformulating the decision process and training objective can improve an advanced pedestrian trajectory prediction architecture and may provide a path toward improving other graph-based prediction models.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有行人路径预测方法大多采用标准监督学习，但未深入探讨将强化学习（尤其是逆强化学习）框架应用于复杂深度学习模型（如STGAT）对预测性能的影响。

**[核心方法]**：将STGAT改编为统一的行人路径预测框架，定义针对STGAT的状态和动作空间，支持确定性/随机策略、一次性/序贯决策，并采用REINFORCE和PPO等强化学习算法（基于逆强化学习范式）进行训练。

**[创新与效果]**：在多个基准数据集上，所提强化学习公式相比标准监督学习显著提升了STGAT的预测性能，验证了重构决策过程与训练目标能改进图轨迹预测模型。

### 2. [Incorporating multi-path risk assessment in transformer-based pedestrian crossing action prediction.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aap.2025.108002)
`相关度 95` · 直接预测行人过街动作

*Accident Analysis and Prevention* | Bowen Liu, Meng Li, ..., Wei Zhou, Zhibin Li | 2025-03-24

**TLDR:** A Transformer-based framework for predicting pedestrian crossing actions that uses visualized pedestrian-vehicle collision risks, which are assessed from multiple potential paths and proves to be an effective method for processing multi-path risk images.

> This paper proposes a Transformer-based framework for predicting pedestrian crossing actions that uses visualized pedestrian-vehicle collision risks, which are assessed from multiple potential paths. Our framework contains two sequential steps: (1) multi-path risks of a pedestrian-vehicle interaction (PVIs) at each time point are estimated and encoded into an RGB image, which captures a high-density array of safety information. (2) a multi-modal fusion architecture that incorporates both risk images and historical sequential data (e.g., pedestrian action and vehicle velocity) is developed based on the Cross-Attention Transformer. The model outputs are also risk-informed, categorized as yielding, risky crossing, and safe crossing. Experiments are conducted on real-world data from the Euro-PVI dataset. Through two-dimensional mapping tests, risk images are validated to have significant spatiotemporal feature differences and transition associations under different PVIs. The Transformer architecture proves to be an effective method for processing multi-path risk images. Prediction accuracy reaches 87.34% for short-term forecasts (0.5 s ahead), maintains stability as the prediction time horizon progressively extends to 2 s, and improves the prediction of abrupt action switches. For further exploration and validation, the risk image data and imaging code are available at www.github.com/Sivan0227/PVI-Risk-Image.

**[试图解决的问题]**：行人过街动作预测中，现有方法缺乏对多路径碰撞风险的有效评估与融合，难以处理长时域预测和突发动作切换。

**[核心方法]**：两步式Transformer框架：① 将每个时间点行人-车辆交互的多路径碰撞风险编码为RGB图像（高密度安全信息）；② 基于Cross-Attention Transformer构建多模态融合架构，融合风险图像与历史序列数据（行人动作、车速），输出让行/危险过街/安全过街三类风险标签。

**[创新与效果]**：在Euro-PVI数据集上，短期预测（0.5s）准确率达87.34%，且预测时间窗口延长至2s时仍保持稳定；风险图像经二维映射验证具有显著时空特征差异与转移关联；有效改善突发动作切换的预测性能。

### 3. [Transformers for Multivariate Time Series Forecasting: Comprehensive Analysis, Challenges, Research Opportunities, and Future Prospects](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2026.3654408)
`相关度 50` · 通用时序预测综述，方法层面相关

*IEEE Access* | M. Saravana, M. S. Roopa, J. Arunalatha, K. R. Venugopal | 2026

**TLDR:** This survey reviews the main Transformer-based approaches developed for MTS forecasting, covering early architectures, efficient attention variants, decomposition-oriented designs, patch-based techniques, hybrid frameworks, and large-scale pretrained models, and discusses the relative strengths and limitations of these methods.

> Multivariate time series (MTS) forecasting appears in many applied settings, ranging from finance and healthcare to energy systems, climate analysis, and IoT-based platforms. In these fields, practitioners rely on forecasts not just for reporting, but for making everyday operational and planning decisions. Over the years, a variety of sequence-modeling methods such as RNNs, LSTMs, CNNs, and TCNs have been used for this purpose. In practice, however, these models often have difficulty dealing with long temporal dependencies, interactions across multiple variables, and the computational demands that arise when datasets grow large. As a result, their performance and scalability can be inconsistent when they are deployed in real applications. In recent years, Transformer-based models have gained attention as an alternative because their self-attention mechanism and parallel processing capabilities allow more flexible handling of long and complex sequences. This survey therefore reviews the main Transformer-based approaches developed for MTS forecasting, covering early architectures, efficient attention variants, decomposition-oriented designs, patch-based techniques, hybrid frameworks, and large-scale pretrained models. In addition, we organize the existing literature into a clear taxonomy that includes commonly used datasets and evaluation metrics, and we discuss the relative strengths and limitations of these methods. Additionally, we discuss key challenges such as computational complexity, interpretability, data sparsity, and generalization, and outline future research directions, including spatiotemporal modeling, self-supervised learning, physics-informed designs, explainable architectures, and federated learning. The survey consolidates existing knowledge and provides guidance for researchers and practitioners to select, adapt, and innovate Transformer-based models for effective MTS forecasting.

[试图解决的问题]：传统序列模型（RNN、LSTM、CNN、TCN）处理多变量时间序列预测时，难以应对长时依赖、多变量交互及大数据集的计算需求，导致性能和可扩展性不一致。

[核心方法]：系统综述基于Transformer的MTS预测方法，涵盖早期架构、高效注意力变体、分解导向设计、补丁技术、混合框架及大规模预训练模型，并建立分类法。

[创新与效果]：汇总常用数据集与评估指标，明确各类方法的相对优势与局限，指出计算复杂度、可解释性、数据稀疏性及泛化性等关键挑战，提出时空建模、自监督学习、物理信息设计、可解释架构及联邦学习等未来方向，为研究者提供选择与创新指南。

### 4. [Post-crash analysis and injury severity prediction in vehicle-pedestrian collisions using logistic regression and AI-based predictive analytics](https://doi.org/10.30574/ijsra.2025.16.1.2077)
`相关度 50` · 车辆-行人碰撞后分析，弱相关

*International Journal of Science and Research Archive* | Samuel Omefe | 2025-07-30

**TLDR:** A hybrid modeling method based on logistic regression and explainable AI and real-time data integration is a promising direction to go in terms of both predictive accuracy and policy explainability and to improve pedestrian safety and post-crash interventions in the complex urban transportation setting.

> Post-crash analysis and road safety management is critical for prediction of injury severity after a vehicle-pedestrian collision. Efficient prediction of injury severity after the occurrence of such events is necessary to guideline emergence medical care enhancement, transportation planning, as well as evidence-based safety courses of action. Traditional statistical methods, such as logistic regression, have been utilized vastly because of their transparent and interpretable results. However, recent developments in Artificial Intelligence (AI) have proposed machine learning (ML) and deep learning methods, which demonstrate better predictive capabilities, particularly on processing complicated, high-dimensional, and nonlinear crash data. This reviewed study aims to reveal how AI-based predictive analytics can improve injury severity prediction in vehicle-pedestrian crashes. It contrasts the advantages and weaknesses of both logistic regression and AI techniques in terms of their methodological aspects and examines the suitability of both approaches in different data conditions and realities of decision-making. An integrative review of recent empirical research and technical developments was performed, covering the application of ML algorithms such as decision trees, random forests, support vector machines, and gradient boosting and also deep neural networks (DNNs) and convolutional neural networks (CNNs). Model performance measures, explanation frameworks like SHAP and LIME, and real-world application scenarios in real time traffic systems were all assessed. An AI model will almost always be more accurate and flexible in its predictions than a logistic regression. The relative research proves that ensemble and deep learning models are especially useful in discovering nonlinear relationships and dealing with the class imbalance. However, logistic regression is still useful in its interpretability and less data demands. Logistic regression remains valuable due to its ease of understanding and interpretability, AI-based models offer better performance in terms of predictive performance of injury severity, their practical use requires trade-offs between accuracy and explainability and ethical concerns which create possibilities to make more nuanced and correct predictions. A hybrid modeling method based on logistic regression and explainable AI and real-time data integration is a promising direction to go in terms of both predictive accuracy and policy explainability and to improve pedestrian safety and post-crash interventions in the complex urban transportation setting.

**[试图解决的问题]**：行人-车辆碰撞后伤害严重程度的预测，以及传统统计方法（逻辑回归）与AI方法在准确性、可解释性及实际决策中的权衡问题。

**[核心方法]**：对比逻辑回归与多种AI方法，包括决策树、随机森林、支持向量机、梯度提升、深度神经网络（DNN）和卷积神经网络（CNN）；采用SHAP、LIME等可解释性框架，并集成实时交通数据。

**[创新与效果]**：AI模型（特别是集成学习与深度学习）在预测准确性上显著优于逻辑回归，能有效处理非线性关系和类别不平衡；逻辑回归仍具可解释性和低数据需求优势。提出逻辑回归+可解释AI+实时数据集的混合建模方法，兼顾预测精度与政策可解释性，提升行人安全及碰撞后干预效果。

### 5. [GinAR+: A Robust End-to-End Framework for Multivariate Time Series Forecasting With Missing Values](https://doi.org/10.1109/TKDE.2025.3569649)
`相关度 40` · 通用时序预测缺失值方法

*IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering* | Chengqing Yu, Fei Wang, ..., Qi Wang, Yongjun Xu | 2025-08-01

**TLDR:** 无

> Spatial-Temporal Graph Neural Networks (STGNNs) have been widely utilized in multivariate time series forecasting (MTSF), but they rely on the assumption of data completeness. In practice, due to factors such as natural disaster, STGNNs frequently encounter the challenge of missing data resulting from numerous malfunctioning data collectors. In this case, on the one hand, due to the presence of missing values, STGNNs easily generate incorrect spatial correlations, leading to the performance degradation. On the other hand, STGNNs require separate training of models for different missing rates, limiting their robustness. To address these challenges, we first propose two important components (interpolation attention and adaptive graph convolution), which utilize normal values to recover missing values into reliable representations and reconstruct spatial correlations. Then, we replace the fully connected layers in simple recursive units with these two components and propose Graph Interpolation Attention Recursive Network (GinAR), aiming to recursively correct spatial correlations and achieve end-to-end MTSF with missing values. Finally, we use data with different missing rates as positive and negative data pairs. By employing contrastive learning to train GinAR, we propose GinAR+ and enhance its robustness to data with different missing rates. Experiments validate the superiority of GinAR+ and our motivation.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有STGNN在进行多变量时间序列预测时假设数据完整，但实际中大量传感器故障导致数据缺失，从而产生错误的空间相关性，且不同缺失率需单独训练模型，缺乏鲁棒性。

**[核心方法]**：提出插值注意力与自适应图卷积，利用正常值恢复缺失值并重建空间相关性；用这两个组件替换简单递归单元中的全连接层，构建GinAR；进一步通过对比学习，将不同缺失率的数据作为正负对训练GinAR+。

**[创新与效果]**：实现了端到端的多变量时间序列预测，无需对缺失率分别建模。实验表明GinAR+在缺失数据场景下预测性能显著优于现有方法，且对不同缺失率具有鲁棒性。
