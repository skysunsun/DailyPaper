### 1. [Understanding Pedestrian Behavior in Complex Traffic Scenes](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIV.2017.2788193)
`相关度 95` · 复杂交通场景行人过街行为理解

*IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Vehicles* | Amir Rasouli, Iuliia Kotseruba, John K. Tsotsos | 2018-03-01

**TLDR:** 无

> Designing autonomous vehicles for urban environments remains an unresolved problem. One major dilemma faced by autonomous cars is understanding the intention of other road users and communicating with them. To investigate one aspect of this, specifically pedestrian crossing behavior, we have collected a large dataset of pedestrian samples at crosswalks under various conditions (e.g., weather) and in different types of roads. Using the data, we analyzed pedestrian behavior from two different perspectives: the way they communicate with drivers prior to crossing and the factors that influence their behavior. Our study shows that changes in head orientation in the form of looking or glancing at the traffic is a strong indicator of crossing intention. We also found that context in the form of the properties of a crosswalk (e.g., its width), traffic dynamics (e.g., speed of the vehicles) as well as pedestrian demographics can alter pedestrian behavior after the initial intention of crossing has been displayed. Our findings suggest that the contextual elements can be interrelated, meaning that the presence of one factor may increase/decrease the influence of other factors. Overall, our work formulates the problem of pedestrian-driver interaction and sheds light on its complexity in typical traffic scenarios.

**[试图解决的问题]**：自动驾驶车辆在复杂城市交通中难以理解和预测行人过街意图，尤其是缺乏对行人–驾驶员交互信号的建模。

**[核心方法]**：采集了多种天气和道路类型下路口/人行横道的大规模行人样本数据集；从两个角度分析行为：过街前与驾驶员的沟通信号，以及影响过街决策的情境因素。重点提取行人头部朝向变化（注视/瞥向交通流）作为意图特征，并建模人行横道属性（如宽度）、交通动态（如车速）和行人人口学特征对行为的调节作用。

**[创新与效果]**：发现头部朝向变化是过街意图的强预测指标；同时证明情境因素并非独立作用，而是相互关联地共同影响行人过街行为，从而显式刻画了行人–驾驶员交互问题的复杂性，为自动驾驶决策提供了可用的行为学依据。

### 2. [Learning in the Curbside Coordinate Frame for a Transferable Pedestrian Trajectory Prediction Model](https://doi.org/10.1109/ITSC.2018.8569812)
`相关度 93` · 路口行人轨迹预测，直接相关

*International Conference on Intelligent Transportation Systems* | Nikita Jaipuria, Golnaz Habibi, J. How | 2018-11-01

**TLDR:** 无

> This paper presents a novel framework for accurate pedestrian trajectory prediction in intersection corners or near crosswalks. Given prior knowledge of curbside geometry (i.e. angle made by intersecting curbs at the corner point of interest and the coordinates of the corner itself), the presented framework can accurately predict pedestrian trajectories even in new, unseen intersections. This is achieved by learning motion primitives in a common frame, called the curbside coordinate frame. A key insight in developing this common frame is to ensure that trajectories from intersections with different geometries, representing the same behavior, are spatially similar in the common frame. Motion primitives learned in such a common frame, can then be easily generalized to predict in new intersections, with different geometries than the ones trained on. We test our algorithm on real pedestrian trajectory datasets collected at two intersections, with distinctly different curbside and crosswalk geometries. A comparison of our algorithm with [1] demonstrates improved prediction accuracies of pedestrian trajectory prediction in the case of same training and test intersections, and the improvement of accuracy in the most different training and test intersections scenarios. The result also shows additional context, such as information about pedestrian traffic lights, if available, can be easily incorporated in our prediction model for further improvement in prediction accuracy.

**[试图解决的问题]**：在路口拐角或人行横道附近的行人轨迹预测中，模型难以泛化到几何结构不同的新路口。

**[核心方法]**：利用路缘几何先验（相交路缘的夹角和拐角坐标）构建“路缘坐标系”（curbside coordinate frame），在该公共坐标系中学习“运动原语”（motion primitives），使得不同路口几何下相同行为对应的轨迹在公共坐标系中空间相似；预测时先转换到该坐标系，再推广到未见过的路口，并可扩展融合行人红绿灯等额外上下文信息。

**[创新与效果]**：在两个路缘及人行横道几何差异明显的真实行人轨迹数据集上，与[1]相比，在训练/测试路口相同场景下提升了预测精度，在训练/测试路口差异最大的场景下同样提升了精度；额外上下文（如行人红绿灯信息）可被轻松融入以进一步提升预测精度。

### 3. [OOSTraj: Out-of-Sight Trajectory Prediction With Vision-Positioning Denoising](https://doi.org/10.1109/CVPR52733.2024.01402)
`相关度 92` · 行人轨迹预测，自动驾驶中行为理解

*Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition* | Haichao Zhang, Yi Xu, ..., Takayuki Shimizu, Yun Fu | 2024-04-02

**TLDR:** 无

> Trajectory prediction is fundamental in computer Vision and autonomous driving, particularly for understanding pedestrian behavior and enabling proactive decision-making. Existing approaches in this field often assume precise and complete observational data, neglecting the challenges associated with out-of-view objects and the noise in-herent in sensor data due to limited camera range, physical obstructions, and the absence of ground truth for denoised sensor data. Such oversights are critical safety concerns, as they can result in missing essential, non-visible objects. To bridge this gap, we present a novel method for out-of-sight trajectory prediction that leverages a vision-positioning technique. Our approach denoises noisy sensor observations in an unsupervised manner and precisely maps sensor-based trajectories of out-of-sight objects into visual trajectories. This method has demonstrated state-of-the-art performance in out-of-sight noisy sensor trajectory denoising and prediction on the Vi-Fi and JRDB datasets. By enhancing trajectory prediction accuracy and addressing the challenges of out-of-sight objects, our work significantly contributes to improving the safety and reliability of autonomous driving in complex environments. Our work represents the first initiative towards Out-Of-Sight Trajectory prediction (OOSTraj), setting a new benchmark for future research.

**[试图解决的问题]**：现有轨迹预测方法假设观测数据精确完整，忽略了因相机视场有限、物理遮挡导致的视场外（out-of-view）物体缺失，以及传感器数据固有的噪声且缺乏去噪真值，造成安全隐患。

**[核心方法]**：提出一种基于视觉-定位联合（vision-positioning）去噪的视场外轨迹预测方法，以无监督方式对含噪传感器观测进行去噪，并将视场外物体的传感器坐标系轨迹精确映射为视觉坐标系轨迹。

**[创新与效果]**：首次提出视场外轨迹预测任务（OOSTraj），在Vi-Fi和JRDB数据集上达到视场外含噪传感器轨迹去噪与预测的最优性能（SOTA），提升了复杂环境下自动驾驶的安全性与可靠性，并建立了新基准。

### 4. [Building Prior Knowledge: A Markov Based Pedestrian Prediction Model Using Urban Environmental Data](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICARCV.2018.8581368)
`相关度 90` · 马尔可夫行人预测，自动驾驶应用

*International Conference on Control, Automation, Robotics and Vision* | Pavan Vasishta, Dominique Vaufreydaz, A. Spalanzani | 2018-09-17

**TLDR:** 无

> Autonomous Vehicles navigating in urban areas have a need to understand and predict future pedestrian behavior for safer navigation. This high level of situational awareness requires observing pedestrian behavior and extrapolating their positions to know future positions. While some work has been done in this field using Hidden Markov Models (HMMs), one of the few observed drawbacks of the method is the need for informed priors for learning behavior. In this work, an extension to the Growing Hidden Markov Model (GHMM) method is proposed to solve some of these drawbacks. This is achieved by building on existing work using potential cost maps and the principle of Natural Vision. As a consequence, the proposed model is able to predict pedestrian positions more precisely over a longer horizon compared to the state of the art. The method is tested over “legal” and “illegal” behavior of pedestrians, having trained the model with sparse observations and partial trajectories. The method, with no training data, is compared against a trained state of the art model. It is observed that the proposed method is robust even in new, previously unseen areas.

**[试图解决的问题]**：城市环境中自动驾驶车辆需预测行人未来位置，但现有隐马尔可夫模型（HMM）依赖人工设定的先验知识，在稀疏观测和部分轨迹条件下难以有效学习行人行为。

**[核心方法]**：在生长隐马尔可夫模型（GHMM）基础上进行扩展，结合势成本图（potential cost maps）与自然视觉（Natural Vision）原理构建先验知识，利用城市环境数据对行人“合法”与“非法”行为建模，支持稀疏观测和部分轨迹训练。

**[创新与效果]**：相比现有最先进模型（state of the art），在更长预测时域上行人位置预测更精确；无需任何训练数据即可与已训练模型对比，并能在全新未见区域保持鲁棒性。

### 5. [Hierarchical Reinforcement Learning with Successor Representation for Intelligent Vehicle Collision Avoidance of Dynamic Pedestrian](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCCS61882.2024.10603229)
`相关度 85` · 车辆避撞行人决策，涉及人车交互建模

*International Conference on Communication, Computing & Security* | Haoyang Wang, Chao Lu, Jian-Wei Gong | 2024-04-19

**TLDR:** 无

> In urban environments, intersection scenarios with dynamic pedestrians are challenging for intelligent vehicles (IV). To ensure safety and efficiency, it is crucial to develop an effective decision-making and control system that can avoid collision with other pedestrians. Considering pedestrian trajectory prediction is beneficial for collision avoidance systems. However, current collision avoidance methods mostly use short-time trajectory prediction results. The exploration and utilization of long-time trajectory prediction and behavior pattern of pedestrians are inadequate. to solve this problem, we propose a decision-making and control system based on pedestrian risk prediction. It combines the idea of successor representation (SR) with hierarchical reinforcement learning (HRL). It builds predictive risk map (PRM) based on SR, and implement decision-making and control by selecting decision primitives (DPs) to complete driving tasks. The proposed method is tested with dynamic pedestrians in intersection scenarios based on the CARLA simulator. The pedestrian trajectories in simulation are built from stochastic data as well as real-world data to reflect the dynamics and uncertainty of real pedestrians. After online learning and testing, the proposed method is proved to have the best performance compared to baseline methods.

**[试图解决的问题]**：城市路口动态行人场景中，现有智能车辆碰撞规避方法多依赖短期轨迹预测，对行人长期轨迹预测与行为模式利用不足，影响行车安全与效率。

**[核心方法]**：提出基于行人风险预测的决策与控制系统，将后继表示（SR）与分层强化学习（HRL）结合；基于SR构建预测风险图（PRM），并通过选择决策原语（DPs）完成驾驶任务；在CARLA仿真中采用随机数据及真实世界行人轨迹数据进行测试。

**[创新与效果]**：利用SR构建PRM以融合长期行人轨迹预测与行为模式，结合HRL实现分层决策与控制；在线学习与测试结果表明，该方法在动态行人路口场景下性能优于基线方法。
